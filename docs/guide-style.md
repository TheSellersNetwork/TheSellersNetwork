# Guide style brief

Guides live in `content/guides/<slug>.mdx`. Read `content/guides/how-to-start-amazon-fba-uk.mdx` first: that is the voice, the density and the level of proof expected.

## Voice

- Plain UK English. Short sentences. Second person ("you"), first person plural for the site ("we").
- Written for someone who does this for money and wants to do it better. Not a blog post, a manual.
- No em dashes anywhere. No exclamation marks. No emoji. No "in this article we will".
- No personal names. Never "Tom". Never "I". Where experience is needed, "sellers we know" or "in practice".
- No product endorsements, no named tools, banks, apps, prep centres, courses or accountants. Describe the type of tool and what it does. No affiliate links, no referral codes.
- Do not copy anyone's structure or sentences. Research widely, then write from a blank page.

## Facts

- Never invent a number. Every figure that matters (fees, prices, thresholds, deadlines, policy limits) must come from an official source and link to it inline: eBay's own help and fees pages, Royal Mail's price pages, GOV.UK, HMRC, Vinted's or Facebook's own help pages, Amazon's own pages.
- If you cannot find an official figure, do not guess: describe how to look it up and link the page.
- Say when something changes often ("check the current page before relying on this").
- UK only. If a rule differs for Northern Ireland or for businesses outside the UK, say so briefly.
- Nothing is tax, legal or financial advice; say so once where relevant.

## Shape

Do not copy the FBA guide's section names. Choose the shape that fits the topic, but every guide should have:

1. Frontmatter (exactly this format, values filled in):

```
---
title: "..."
excerpt: "One or two sentences. What the reader will be able to do."
categories: [slug, slug]
module: optional-course-module-slug-or-omit
order: 10
published: 2026-09-25
---
```

Category slugs that exist: ebay-listings-and-titles, ebay-pricing-and-offers, ebay-postage-and-packaging, ebay-buyers-and-disputes, ebay-account-health-and-policy, ebay-promoted-listings-and-traffic, amazon-fba-and-fbm, amazon-listings-and-content, amazon-ads-and-ppc, amazon-account-health-and-suspensions, amazon-sourcing-and-wholesale, vinted, facebook-marketplace, whatnot, ebay-live, tiktok-live-and-other, running-a-show, depop-and-clothing-resale, etsy-and-handmade, tiktok-shop, own-website-and-shopify, sourcing-and-stock, tax-bookkeeping-and-legal, tools-and-automation, multi-channel-selling, wins-and-case-studies.

2. A two or three sentence opening that says who it is for and what they will be able to do after reading. No throat clearing.
3. `##` and `###` headings only. Tables where a table is clearer than prose (fees, options, decision matrices, troubleshooting).
4. Real decisions with a recommendation and the reasoning, not a list of options with "it depends".
5. A short "mistakes" or "what goes wrong" section near the end.
6. End with a pointer to the relevant forum(s) as an internal link, for example `[Postage and packaging](/community/c/ebay-postage-and-packaging)`, then the line `<Signup source="/guides/<slug>" />` on its own.

Length: 1,500 to 3,000 words. Longer only if the topic needs it.

## Do not

- Do not mention mentoring, courses, Autopilot or anything paid.
- Do not write "[TOM: ...]" or any placeholder. If a fact is missing, describe how to find it.
- Do not include images.
- Do not use the words "unlock", "leverage", "supercharge", "game changer", "ultimate", "secret".
