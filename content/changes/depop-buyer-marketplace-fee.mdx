## The short version

- UK buyers pay a Marketplace fee on every Depop purchase in pounds, [from 15 April 2024](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee).
- It is [up to 5% of the item price, plus a fixed amount of up to £1](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee). Postage and taxes are not included in the calculation.
- The seller does not pay it. The exact amount is shown to the buyer at checkout.
- It is refunded in full if the item is refunded.
- It came in alongside the removal of the [10% selling fee for UK sellers](/blog/depop-removes-selling-fee).

Neutral to mildly helpful for sellers overall: you keep more, but your items cost buyers a little more than your price.

## Who it affects

In: buyers in the UK paying in GBP, and so indirectly every UK seller whose buyers are in the UK.

Out: sellers do not pay the fee. Buyers in Australia have their [own version of the policy](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee).

## What changed

| | Before 15 April 2024 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer pays | Item price, postage, taxes | Item price, postage, taxes, plus the Marketplace fee |
| Marketplace fee | None | [Up to 5% of item price plus up to £1](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee) |
| Bundles | | Percentage on the combined item price, fixed amount charged once |
| Refunds | | Fee refunded in full with the item |

Depop gives the fee as a maximum ("up to") rather than a fixed rate, and says the exact amount appears at checkout.

## What it means for your money

The fee does not come out of your payout. It changes what the buyer pays.

On a £20 item, the buyer pays **at most** £1 (5%) plus £1, so up to £2 on top of your price and postage. On a £60 item, it is at most £3 plus £1, so up to £4.

Two practical effects:

- **Low prices carry a bigger share of fees.** Up to £2 on a £20 item is 10%. On a £5 item it could be up to £1.25, which is 25%.
- **Bundles help.** The fixed part is [charged once per transaction](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee), so three items bought together cost the buyer less in fees than three separate orders.

We have not added a calculator because Depop publishes maximums, not exact figures.

## What to do now

1. When comparing your prices with other platforms, remember the Depop buyer pays your price, postage and the Marketplace fee.
2. Look at your cheapest items. If they are slow, the fixed part of the fee may be putting buyers off. Grouping them into bundles is one answer.
3. Offer bundle discounts to encourage multi-item orders.
4. Price from sold items rather than asking prices. Our guide on [pricing from sold comps](/guides/pricing-from-sold-comps) explains how.
