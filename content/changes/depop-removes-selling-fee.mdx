## The short version

- Depop removed its [10% selling fee for UK sellers](https://news.depop.com/company-news/evolving-our-fee-structure-with-zero-selling-fees-on-depop/) selling in pounds, starting with new listings created from 20 March 2024.
- Depop's [seller fees page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) now shows no selling fee at all for UK-based sellers.
- You still pay the payment processing fee, currently [2.9% plus 30p](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) per sale.
- The cost moved to buyers, who now pay a [Marketplace fee](/blog/depop-buyer-marketplace-fee).

Good news for UK sellers: most of what Depop used to take from each sale is gone.

## Who it affects

In: sellers based in the UK, selling in GBP.

Out: sellers outside the UK, US and Australia, who still pay a [10% selling fee](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges). The US and Australia have their own processing fees on the same page.

## What changed

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Depop selling fee (UK, GBP) | [10%](https://news.depop.com/company-news/evolving-our-fee-structure-with-zero-selling-fees-on-depop/) | [None](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) |
| Payment processing (Depop Payments) | Applied | Still applies, [2.9% plus 30p](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) |
| Buyer Marketplace fee | None | [Up to 5% plus up to £1](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/21752555753361-What-is-the-Marketplace-fee), paid by the buyer from 15 April 2024 |

The processing fee is worked out on the item price plus postage and any taxes. The 10% figure is from Depop's announcement, and the 2.9% plus 30p is today's rate. We could not find an official page giving the processing fee as it stood in March 2024.

Boosted listings are separate and optional. For UK sellers, the [boosting fee is 12%](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges) on new listings from 23 March 2026. If you boost everything, you pay more than the old selling fee on those sales.

## What it means for your money

Take a £20 item with £4 postage.

- **Selling fee:** 10% of £20 was £2. Now nothing.
- **Processing fee:** 2.9% of £24 is about 70p, plus 30p, so about £1.

So on that sale you keep about £2 more than you would have with the old selling fee. Across a month, the saving is roughly a tenth of your item sales.

## What to do now

1. Check your recent payouts in Depop Balance to confirm no selling fee is being taken.
2. Rework your margins with only the processing fee, and boosting where you use it.
3. Think about what buyers now pay. The [Marketplace fee](/blog/depop-buyer-marketplace-fee) makes your items cost buyers a little more than your price.
4. If you sell the same stock elsewhere, compare what you keep per sale on each platform. Our guide on [multi-channel selling](/guides/multi-channel-selling) helps.

## Key dates

| Date | What happens |
|---|---|
| 20 March 2024 | No selling fee on new UK listings created from this date |
| 21 March 2024 | Depop newsroom announcement published |
| 15 April 2024 | Buyer Marketplace fee starts in the UK |

## What we do not know yet

Depop's announcement only covered new listings from 20 March 2024. Today's fees page shows no selling fee for UK sellers at all, but we have not found an official date for when older listings stopped paying it. Fees can change, so check the current page before relying on this.
