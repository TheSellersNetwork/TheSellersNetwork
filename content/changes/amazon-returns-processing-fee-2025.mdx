## The short version

- Since [1 February 2025](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/74912302-a5d7-4477-a458-3483da000eef), FBA products with a return rate above their category's threshold pay a fee on returns above that threshold. Clothing and shoes are left out because they already pay a returns fee on every return.
- Only the returns above the threshold are charged, not every return.
- The aged inventory surcharge now starts on stock older than 240 days, a month earlier than before.
- The minimum referral fee on heavy items fell from £25 to £20.
- Storage, removal, disposal and liquidation fees were adjusted at the same time. Check the rate card for your size tiers.

## Who it affects

**In:** FBA sellers in the UK, Germany, France, Italy and Spain with products that come back more often than is normal for their category. Sellers holding stock for eight months or more. Sellers of heavy items fulfilled by Amazon.

**Not in:** merchant-fulfilled orders, since this is an FBA fee. Clothing and shoes, which instead pay a returns processing fee equal to [50% of the fulfilment fee](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/250120-FBA-Rate-Card-UK_Eng.pdf) on every return. Products with return rates at or under their category threshold.

## What changed

| | Before | After (from 1 February 2025) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Returns fee on high-return-rate products (not clothing or shoes) | None | Fee per returned unit above the category threshold |
| Aged inventory surcharge, 241 to 270 days (not clothing, eyewear, bags, luggage, jewellery or watches) | None | £1.18 per cubic foot per month (from 15 February 2025) |
| Minimum referral fee, heavy items via FBA | £25 | £20 |

Sources: [announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/74912302-a5d7-4477-a458-3483da000eef) and [rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/250120-FBA-Rate-Card-UK_Eng.pdf).

**How the threshold works.** The [rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/250120-FBA-Rate-Card-UK_Eng.pdf) defines your return rate as the share of units shipped in one month that customers return during that month and the next two calendar months. Each category has its own threshold. An [Amazon staff reply](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/394152d9-226e-43cb-a4fd-c3ed681409c6) confirms that only the returned units beyond the threshold are charged. The thresholds by category are listed on the [returns processing fee help page](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/GZGEQLTM3RZXUV6T?locale=en-GB), which needs a Seller Central login. We have not repeated them here because they can change.

**The fee itself** depends on size and weight, like the fulfilment fee. In the UK it starts at £1.70 for the lightest envelope and is £2.27 for a small parcel up to 400 g ([rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/250120-FBA-Rate-Card-UK_Eng.pdf)).

**The heavy item minimum** in the announcement is for heavy non-sortable items. The rate card shows £20 for Heavy Oversize items, between 23 kg and 31.5 kg. Heavy Bulky items, over 31.5 kg or very long, still show a £25 minimum.

This fee was first due in 2024. Amazon's [February 2024 rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/240201-FBA-Rate-Card-UK.pdf) listed it for 1 October 2024, and the 2025 announcement pushed it back to 1 February 2025.

## What it means for your money

Amazon's own worked example: you ship 1,000 units in a month and 100 come back. If the category threshold were 7.14%, the first 71 returns are free and the fee applies to the other 29. For a small parcel up to 400 g in the UK at £2.27, that is £65.83 on top of the refund and any lost stock.

The way to cut the cost is to get returns back under the threshold. Each return you prevent above the line saves the fee as well as the sale.

## What to do now

1. Find your category thresholds on the [help page](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/GZGEQLTM3RZXUV6T?locale=en-GB) and compare them with your own return rates in the FBA customer returns report.
2. Read the return reasons on your worst products. "Not as described" and "wrong size" usually point to a listing you can fix. "Defective" points to the supplier.
3. Tighten listings for products near the line: accurate photos, dimensions, compatibility, what is in the box.
4. Check the inventory age report and clear stock before it reaches 241 days, not 271.
5. If you sell heavy items, recheck the referral fee on your cheapest heavy lines, as the lower minimum may help.

## Key dates

| Date | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| 1 October 2024 | Original start date for the returns fee, later delayed |
| 1 February 2025 | Returns processing fee for high-return-rate products and heavy item minimum change take effect |
| 15 February 2025 | Aged inventory surcharge extends to 241 to 270 days |

## What we do not know yet

- The category thresholds can be revised. Check the help page rather than relying on a figure you saw once.
- Fees and thresholds were revised again for 2026, including rules for selected categories. Check the current rate card before relying on this.
