## The short version

- From [8 April 2024](https://community.ebay.co.uk/t5/Announcements/Selling-clothing-items-will-be-free-for-private-sellers-from-8/ba-p/7594172), UK private sellers paid no selling fees on clothing.
- It covered clothing that was already listed and unsold on that date, as well as new listings.
- It did not cover business sellers, or categories outside clothing.
- It has since been **superseded**. Since 1 October 2024, [private sellers pay no final value fee in almost every category](/blog/ebay-free-selling-private-sellers), so the clothing-only rule no longer makes a difference.

Still in effect in the sense that clothing is free for private sellers, but it is now part of a wider change.

## Who it affects

In: UK-based private sellers listing clothing.

Out: business sellers, and anything outside the clothing categories. Other categories became free for private sellers later, with the [October 2024 change](/blog/ebay-free-selling-private-sellers).

## What changed

| | Before 8 April 2024 | 8 April to 30 September 2024 | From 1 October 2024 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Private seller, clothing | Selling fees charged | [Free](https://community.ebay.co.uk/t5/Announcements/Selling-clothing-items-will-be-free-for-private-sellers-from-8/ba-p/7594172) | [Free](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822) |
| Private seller, other categories (not motors) | Selling fees charged | Selling fees charged | [Free](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ebay-uk-announces-it-is-now-free-to-sell-across-its-categories/) |
| Business seller | Business fees | Not part of this change | Business fees |

## What it means for your money

Nothing new today. If you are a private seller, the [current private seller fees page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822) sets what you pay, including the monthly free listing allowance and the fee on items sent abroad.

If you sell clothing you have bought to resell, read the section on the private and business line in our page on [free selling for private sellers](/blog/ebay-free-selling-private-sellers).

## What to do now

1. Treat this as history. Use the [October 2024 change](/blog/ebay-free-selling-private-sellers) as your reference for private seller fees.
2. Check the current private seller fees page before relying on this.
