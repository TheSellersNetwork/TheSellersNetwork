## The short version

- From [4 February 2025](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/news/2025-january/buyer-protection), buyers pay a Buyer Protection fee when they buy from a UK private seller.
- The fee is [built into the price buyers see](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594) while browsing. You, the seller, still receive your own price.
- Private sellers are [paid after the item is delivered](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/news/2025-january/buyer-protection), not straight after the buyer pays, unless they qualify for faster payment.
- [Coupons, multi-buy and other discount tools](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/news/2025-january/buyer-protection) are now for business sellers only.
- Business sellers do not have the fee added. For them it is included at no extra cost.

Mostly neutral for your takings, but it changes how your prices look next to other sellers'.

## Who it affects

In:

- UK-based **private** sellers, whose listings show a higher price to buyers.
- **Business** sellers indirectly: their prices now sit next to private listings that carry the fee, and they keep the discount tools.

Out, according to the [Buyer Protection fee page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594):

- Private sellers based outside the UK.
- Cars, Motorcycles and Vehicles, Classified Ads, and Property.

## What changed

| | Before 4 February 2025 | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Price a buyer sees on a private listing | Your price | Your price plus the Buyer Protection fee |
| Who pays the fee | No such fee | The buyer, to eBay |
| When a private seller is paid | On the usual payout timing | [After delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/news/2025-january/buyer-protection), or faster if you qualify |
| Coupons, multi-buy, other discounts | Private and business sellers | [Business sellers only](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/news/2025-january/buyer-protection) |

The current fee is set out on eBay's [Buyer Protection fee page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/buying/paying-items/buyer-protection-fee?id=5594):

| Part of the item price | Fee |
|---|---|
| Flat fee | 10p per item |
| First £20 | 7% |
| £20 to £300 | 4% |
| £300 to £4,000 | 2% |
| Above £4,000 | No fee |

Those figures include VAT. The flat fee is charged once if a buyer takes several of the same item from one listing. If you refund in full, the buyer gets the fee back too, and partial refunds return a matching share of it.

## What it means for your money

Your takings do not change. The buyer pays the fee on top of your price. What changes is the number the buyer sees.

Worked from the bands above:

- **£20 item:** 10p plus 7% of £20 (£1.40) is £1.50. The buyer sees £21.50.
- **£50 item:** 10p plus £1.40 on the first £20, plus 4% of the next £30 (£1.20), is £2.70. The buyer sees £52.70.

So if you are a private seller and the going rate for an item is £50 all in, you would need to list at a little over £47 to show buyers a similar price. Whether you do that is your call. It depends on how price-sensitive your buyers are.

We have not added a calculator because the fee is banded rather than a single rate.

On timing, a private seller's funds are [typically available two days after delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814) for tracked items, or 14 days from the order for untracked ones. Since 6 August 2025, private sellers with at least 10 completed sales worth £150 or more in the last five years, and a clean record on cases, can be paid within about 24 hours of the buyer paying. The page has the full criteria.

## What to do now

1. If you are a private seller, look at one of your listings as a buyer would, and see the price they actually see.
2. Compare that with sold prices for the same item. Our guide on [pricing from sold comps](/guides/pricing-from-sold-comps) covers how.
3. Use tracked postage where it makes sense. Tracked items get paid out sooner after delivery than untracked ones.
4. If you relied on multi-buy or coupons as a private seller, those are gone. If you are really running a business, you should be on a business account anyway (see [who counts as a private seller](/blog/ebay-free-selling-private-sellers)).
5. If you are a business seller, note that private listings now show a higher price than the seller receives. Your prices may compare better than they used to.

## What we do not know yet

- The announcement did not give the fee amounts, and eBay can change the bands at any time. The figures above are from the current help page, so check the current page before relying on this.
- Whether the fee puts buyers off private listings in your category. Watch your sell-through rate against earlier months.
