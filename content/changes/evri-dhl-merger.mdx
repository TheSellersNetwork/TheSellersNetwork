## The short version

- Evri [announced](https://www.evri.com/news/dhl-ecommerce-uk-to-merge-with-evri-news) on 14 May 2025 that it would merge with DHL eCommerce UK.
- The competition regulator cleared it without conditions, and Evri says the merger [completed on 1 October 2025](https://www.evri.com/press/introducing-the-evri-group-the-uks-premier-parcel-delivery-company).
- DHL eCommerce UK is being renamed "Evri Premium, a network of DHL" and run as a separate network for high-value, time-sensitive and larger parcels.
- Evri's announcements do not mention any change to prices, services or drop-off for people who send parcels with Evri today. For most small sellers this is background news.

## Who it affects

**In:** sellers who send with Evri, whether through Evri directly or through a marketplace label, and business sellers who have an account with DHL eCommerce UK.

**Not directly affected:** sellers who only use Royal Mail, Parcelforce or other couriers. The deal covers DHL eCommerce UK, DHL's UK parcel network. Evri's announcements do not mention DHL Express, so do not assume anything changes there.

## What changed

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ownership | Evri and DHL eCommerce UK were separate companies | One group, the Evri Group. Evri says DHL Group holds a significant minority stake, and funds managed by Apollo remain majority owners ([announcement](https://www.evri.com/news/dhl-ecommerce-uk-to-merge-with-evri-news)) |
| DHL eCommerce UK brand | DHL eCommerce UK | To become Evri Premium, a network of DHL, in 2026 ([CMA release](https://www.evri.com/press/dhl-ecommerce-uk-and-evri-secure-unconditional-cma-approval)) |
| Premium network | Separate DHL network | Still a separate network for high-value and time-sensitive items |
| Drop-off points | Two separate networks | Evri says the group has more than 10,000 ParcelShops and lockers ([October 2025 release](https://www.evri.com/press/introducing-the-evri-group-the-uks-premier-parcel-delivery-company)) |

The May 2025 announcement described 15,000 UK out-of-home access points for the combined business. The October 2025 release counts more than 10,000 ParcelShops and lockers. The two figures appear to count different things, and Evri has not explained the difference.

The group also now handles business letters, which Evri says it has not offered before.

## What it means for your money

Nothing yet. Neither announcement sets a new price, surcharge or fee for senders. If your Evri price changes, it will come through Evri's own price pages or your marketplace's label prices, not through this deal.

The likely longer term effect is choice: a cheaper courier network for low-value, lightweight parcels, and a premium van network for items where you want more security. Whether the premium option is worth it will depend on prices Evri has not yet published for small senders.

## What to do now

1. Nothing urgent. Keep posting as you do now.
2. If you have a DHL eCommerce UK business account, watch for emails about the Evri Premium name and any new account terms.
3. When Evri Premium prices appear for smaller senders, compare them with Royal Mail tracked services on your higher-value items. Our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) covers how to weigh cost against protection.

## What we do not know yet

- **Rebrand date.** Evri has said the Evri Premium name arrives in 2026, but we have not found an official date for the switch or confirmation that it has happened.
- **Access for small senders.** It is not clear whether individual sellers will be able to book Evri Premium directly, or only through business accounts and marketplace integrations.
- **Prices.** None published for Evri Premium for small senders. Check Evri's own site before relying on anything here.
