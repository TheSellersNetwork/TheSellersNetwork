## The short version

- eBay [agreed to buy Depop from Etsy](https://news.depop.com/company-news/ebay-to-acquire-depop-from-etsy/), announced on 18 February 2026, for about $1.2 billion in cash.
- The deal [completed on 30 July 2026](https://news.depop.com/company-news/ebay-completes-acquisition-of-depop/). Depop is now owned by eBay.
- Depop is to keep its own name, brand, app and culture, and its chief executive stays.
- Nothing about seller fees, listings or payouts has been announced as part of the deal.

Mostly noise for now. Nothing changes on either platform because of this announcement alone.

## Who it affects

Anyone selling on Depop, and to a lesser extent eBay sellers in fashion. There is no change to your account, fees or payouts today.

## What changed

| | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Owner of Depop | Etsy | eBay |
| Depop brand and app | Separate | [Still separate](https://news.depop.com/company-news/ebay-completes-acquisition-of-depop/) |
| Depop fees | See Depop's fees page | No change announced |

eBay says it will bring its strengths in [shipping, personalisation, compliance and trust](https://www.ebayinc.com/stories/news/ebay-completes-acquisition-of-depop/) to Depop. It has not said what that means in practice.

## What it means for your money

Nothing yet. Your Depop fees are still those on Depop's [seller fees page](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791127-Seller-fees-and-charges), and buyers still pay the [Marketplace fee](/blog/depop-buyer-marketplace-fee).

## What to do now

1. Nothing is needed. Keep selling as normal.
2. Watch for emails from Depop about changes to its terms, fees or postage options, and read them properly when they arrive.
3. If you sell on both platforms, keep your listings and records separate as you do now. Our guide on [multi-channel selling](/guides/multi-channel-selling) covers running more than one shop.

## What we do not know yet

- Whether Depop's fees, postage options or payouts will change. No plans have been announced.
- Whether Depop listings will ever appear on eBay, or the other way round. Nothing has been said.
- What "shipping, personalisation, compliance and trust" will mean for Depop sellers in practice.

We will update this page if eBay or Depop announce anything that affects sellers. Check the Depop newsroom before relying on this.
