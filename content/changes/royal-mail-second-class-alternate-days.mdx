## The short version

- From 28 July 2025, [Ofcom allowed Royal Mail](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/post/royal-mail/reforming-the-postal-service-so-it-delivers-what-people-need) to deliver 2nd Class letters on alternate weekdays, Monday to Friday, instead of six days a week.
- There are no Saturday deliveries of 2nd Class letters under the new pattern. The aim is still delivery within three weekdays.
- 1st Class letters are still delivered next day, Monday to Saturday. Parcels are still delivered up to seven days a week.
- Royal Mail is rolling it out gradually across around 1,200 delivery offices and expects to finish by [December 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/receiving/the-future-of-letter-deliveries).
- For most resellers this is minor. It matters if you post small flat items, such as trading cards, as 2nd Class letters.

## Who it affects

**In:** anyone who posts items as a 2nd Class **letter**, the smallest format (up to 24cm by 16.5cm, 5mm thick and 100g, per Royal Mail's [online price guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-03/online-price-guide-april-2026-v1-ta.pdf)). For resellers that usually means single trading cards, stamps, patches and other very flat, light items.

**Out, according to eBay:** eBay's [announcement on the change](https://community.ebay.co.uk/forum/announcements-28/topic/royal-mail-2nd-class-letter-whats-changing-and-what-isnt-17671/) says large letters, parcels and 1st Class services are not affected. Royal Mail's own pages describe the change as applying to "2nd Class and other non-1st Class letters" and confirm that [parcels are not affected](https://www.royalmail.com/receiving/the-future-of-letter-deliveries). Royal Mail does not spell out large letters on its page, which is covered below.

Tracked services are parcels or large letters with their own delivery aims, so they are outside this change.

## What changed

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd Class letter delivery days | Monday to Saturday | Alternate weekdays, Monday to Friday |
| 2nd Class letters on Saturday | Yes | No |
| 2nd Class delivery aim | Within three working days | Within three weekdays |
| 1st Class letters | Next day, Monday to Saturday | No change |
| Parcels | Up to seven days a week | No change |
| 1st Class target | 93% next day | 90% next day |
| 2nd Class target | 98.5% within three days | 95% within three days |

The targets are from [Ofcom's decision](https://www.ofcom.org.uk/post/royal-mail/reforming-the-postal-service-so-it-delivers-what-people-need). Ofcom also set a new backstop: 99% of mail must arrive no more than two days late. Royal Mail's [FAQ](https://www.royalmail.com/receiving/the-future-of-letter-deliveries) says the new targets came into effect on 1 April 2026.

Royal Mail publishes the expected delivery day for a 2nd Class letter once an area has switched over:

| Posted on | Due by |
| --- | --- |
| Monday | Thursday |
| Tuesday | Friday |
| Wednesday | Monday |
| Thursday | Tuesday |
| Friday | Wednesday |
| Saturday or Sunday | Thursday |

Bank holidays can push these back, as Royal Mail notes on the same page.

## What it means for your money

The price does not change because of this. What changes is time. A card posted 2nd Class on a Wednesday can now be due the following Monday, and one posted on Friday can be due the following Wednesday. That is a longer wait for the buyer and more "where is my item" messages before it lands.

There is no calculator here, because the cost to you is in late delivery marks and messages, not a fee.

## What to do now

1. On eBay, make sure a letter-sized item uses the specific "Royal Mail 2nd Class Letter" service, not a general 2nd Class option. eBay's [announcement](https://community.ebay.co.uk/forum/announcements-28/topic/royal-mail-2nd-class-letter-whats-changing-and-what-isnt-17671/) asks sellers to do this, and says estimated delivery dates will be updated to the new pattern.
2. Check that anything listed as a letter really fits the letter limits. If it does not, it should be a large letter, which eBay says is outside this change.
3. If you promise a delivery window in your own listings or on your own site, allow for the new schedule for 2nd Class letters, especially for Wednesday and Friday posting.
4. For anything valuable or time sensitive, use 1st Class or a tracked service. Our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) covers when tracking is worth it.
5. On Vinted and other apps where the app sets the service and label, check which service your label is for before assuming anything changes.

## What we do not know yet

- **Your area's switch date.** Royal Mail is rolling this out office by office and has not published a date per area. Royal Mail's [delivery times page](https://www.royalmail.com/receiving/delivery-times) still describes 2nd Class as six days a week in places, which reflects the gradual rollout.
- **Large letters.** eBay says they are not affected. Royal Mail's page talks about letters without naming large letters either way. Until Royal Mail says so in writing, treat eBay's statement as the best official answer.
- **Prices.** Ofcom has said there will still be a cap on the price of a 2nd Class stamp and has started a review of pricing and affordability. Check the current page before relying on this.
