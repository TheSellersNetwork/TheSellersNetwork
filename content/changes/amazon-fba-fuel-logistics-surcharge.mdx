## The short version

- Since [17 April 2026](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/cb3452e9-3803-435b-bfc2-e3b6a10a9e42), Amazon adds a 1.5% surcharge to FBA fulfilment fees in the UK and nine EU stores.
- It is 1.5% of the fulfilment fee, not of your sale price. Amazon puts the [average at about 5p a unit](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/cb3452e9-3803-435b-bfc2-e3b6a10a9e42), more on big or heavy items.
- Multi-Channel Fulfilment (Amazon shipping your orders from other sites) picked up the same surcharge from 2 May 2026.
- Amazon describes it as temporary, but there is no end date. Treat it as a permanent cost until Amazon says otherwise.
- Mostly a small cost per unit. It matters at volume, and on items where FBA was already only just worth it.

## Who it affects

**In:** anyone using FBA on Amazon.co.uk, and on the France, Germany, Italy, Spain, Poland, Sweden, Netherlands, Ireland and Belgium stores. Anyone using Multi-Channel Fulfilment from UK, French, German, Italian or Spanish stock ([announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/cb3452e9-3803-435b-bfc2-e3b6a10a9e42)).

**Not in:** orders you ship yourself (merchant fulfilled). Referral fees, storage fees and your sale price are not touched. The surcharge sits only on the fulfilment fee.

## What changed

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| FBA fulfilment fee | Standard rate card fee | Fee plus 1.5% (from 17 April 2026) |
| Multi-Channel Fulfilment fee | Standard MCF fee | Fee plus 1.5% (from 2 May 2026) |
| Average extra per FBA unit | None | About 5p, per Amazon |
| End date | Not applicable | None given |

Amazon's reasoning is that fuel and logistics costs have stayed high and it wants to recover part of that, in the same way parcel carriers add fuel surcharges. It says it will keep the surcharge under review. Because it is a percentage of the fulfilment fee, larger and heavier items pay more in pence, and a small envelope item pays very little.

## What it means for your money

Take the fulfilment fees you pay in a month and multiply by 1.5%. If your FBA fulfilment fees come to £500 a month, the surcharge adds £7.50. At £2,000 a month it adds £30. That is £360 a year you did not budget for, which is roughly the kind of number that is worth knowing before you set prices for the next quarter.

Per unit, the effect is small. A unit with a £3.00 fulfilment fee pays an extra 4.5p. Where it bites is on cheap, fast-selling lines with thin margins, where a few pence a unit across thousands of units is real money.

## What to do now

1. Check your fee preview for your active listings. Amazon says the [revenue calculator](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing), the Fee and Economics Preview report and Profit Analytics include the surcharge, so recheck any line you last costed before April.
2. Update your own profit spreadsheet or repricing floor. If you work out fees yourself from the rate card, add 1.5% to every fulfilment fee.
3. Look again at the lines with the thinnest margins. If FBA was only just beating shipping it yourself, rerun that comparison.
4. If you use Multi-Channel Fulfilment for eBay or website orders, add the same 1.5% to those costs from 2 May 2026 onwards.
5. Check the announcement page every few months. Amazon has said it will review the surcharge, and it could change in either direction.

## What we do not know yet

- When, or whether, it ends. Amazon calls it temporary but has not tied it to any fuel price or date.
- Whether the percentage will rise or fall if fuel costs move. Amazon only says it will keep evaluating it.
- How it interacts with the festive season peak fulfilment fee later in the year. Check the current rate card in Seller Central before relying on this.
