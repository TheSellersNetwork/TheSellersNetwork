## The short version

- Vinted has published new UK terms that apply from [8 October 2026](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions). You need to accept them to keep using Vinted.
- The fee buyers pay at checkout is renamed from the "Buyer Protection fee" to the "Vinted fee", and is described as a platform fee that helps pay for running the site.
- If a buyer claims an item is fake and Vinted has strong evidence, sellers now get 48 hours to prove it is genuine, up from 24 hours.
- If Vinted's paid verification service decides an item is counterfeit, Vinted can hold it for six weeks for you to challenge, then dispose of it without compensation.
- The fee is still paid by the buyer, not the seller. For most sellers this is a small change, but sellers of branded goods should read the counterfeit rules.

## Who it affects

**All UK members**, since everyone has to accept the new terms.

**Sellers of branded and designer items** most of all, because the counterfeit and verification rules are where the real changes are for sellers.

**Buyers** see the fee under a new name. The amount is set by Vinted's own help page, not by the terms.

Pro sellers have their own [Vinted Pro terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pro-terms-and-conditions), which this page does not cover.

## What changed

The "before" column is Vinted's [current terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/old-terms-and-conditions), which apply until 8 October 2026. The "after" column is the [new terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions).

| | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Name of the buyer's checkout fee | Buyer Protection fee | Vinted fee |
| How the fee is described | Named after the Buyer Protection policy | A mandatory platform fee that helps cover the costs of running the site |
| Name of the refund rules | Buyer Protection policy | Refund policy |
| Time for a seller to prove an item is authentic, when Vinted has strong evidence it is a counterfeit | 24 hours | 48 hours |
| Items that fail verification as counterfeit | No retention and disposal clause | Held for six weeks for you to challenge, then may be disposed of, recycled or destroyed without notice or compensation |
| Items that fail verification | No listing restriction clause | Cannot be listed on Vinted again |

The refund rules for lost, damaged and "significantly not as described" items are now called the refund policy, separate from the fee's name. The two-day window for a buyer to report a problem, and for a seller to confirm a returned item, stays.

## What it means for your money

For sellers, nothing changes in fees. You still receive the item price and any shipping the buyer paid besides prepaid shipping, and the buyer pays the Vinted fee on top.

Vinted's [fee help page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1654) says the fee is usually a percentage of 3% to 8% of the item or bundle price, plus a fixed £0.30 to £0.80, and that it varies by item, order value and whether it is a bundle. That is a range, not a fixed rate, so there is no calculator on this page.

Where it can cost a seller real money is counterfeits. Under the new terms, if a buyer pays for verification and your item fails as counterfeit, the sale is cancelled and you have six weeks to challenge it. If you do not provide proof or a formal objection in that time, you lose the item as well as the sale.

## What to do now

1. Read and accept the new terms in the app or on the site before 8 October 2026, so your account is not interrupted.
2. If you sell branded or designer items, keep receipts, proof of purchase and clear photos of labels and serial numbers for every item. You may need them within 48 hours of a counterfeit claim.
3. If an item of yours fails verification, act within the six weeks. After that, Vinted can destroy it and you give up any claim to it.
4. Do not relist an item that has failed verification. The new terms say it cannot be listed again.
5. If buyers ask about the fee, the Vinted fee is the same checkout charge under a new name. Point them to Vinted's [fee help page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1654).

## What we do not know yet

Vinted has not published a summary of what changed, so this page is based on comparing the two versions of the terms. We cannot say whether the fee range on the help page has changed at the same time, as Vinted does not publish past versions of that page. Vinted also lists a new payment company, Vinted Pay Limited, among its wallet providers, but has not said when or whether your wallet moves to it. Check the current page before relying on this.
