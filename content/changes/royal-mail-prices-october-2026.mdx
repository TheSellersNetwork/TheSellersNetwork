## The short version

- Royal Mail prices for personal customers and marketplace sellers change on [5 October 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/prices2026), six months after the [April rise](/blog/royal-mail-prices-april-2026).
- Stamps and 1st and 2nd Class large letters do not change. A 2nd Class large letter up to 100g bought online stays at £1.55.
- Untracked parcels take the biggest hit. A 2nd Class small parcel bought online goes from £3.95 to £4.40, up 45p.
- Tracked 48 and Tracked 24 rise by 5p to 20p online. A Tracked 48 small parcel goes from £3.65 to £3.75.
- Mostly bad news for parcel senders, mostly noise for anyone who ships flat items as large letters.

## Who it affects

Anyone who sends parcels with Royal Mail and pays online (through Royal Mail's own site or a marketplace that sells Royal Mail labels) or at a Post Office counter. Royal Mail lists the October changes for this group as Special Delivery Guaranteed, Tracked, Signed For parcels and Parcel Collect, and the price guides show 1st and 2nd Class parcels rising too.

Who is not affected much: sellers who post letters and large letters only. Those prices are the same in the October guides as they are now.

Business account customers on Royal Mail 24 and 48 also get new prices on 5 October, in a separate [business price guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/business-price-guide-october-2026-v2-ta.pdf). This page covers the online and Post Office prices only.

The pattern from April carries on. Untracked parcels rise much faster than tracked ones. Online, a Tracked 48 small parcel was already 30p cheaper than a 2nd Class small parcel. From October it is 65p cheaper (£3.75 against £4.40), and it comes with tracking and £75 compensation against £20.

## What it means for your money

Round examples using only the online figures above.

- 50 small parcels a month by Tracked 48: 10p more on each, so £5 more a month.
- 50 small parcels a month by untracked 2nd Class: 45p more on each, so £22.50 more a month. Moving the same 50 parcels to Tracked 48 would cost £32.50 a month less than staying on 2nd Class after October.
- 100 clothing items a month as 2nd Class large letters: no change at all.

## What to do now

1. Check your listing postage charges against the [October online guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf) before 5 October, so you are not undercharging from day one.
2. If you still send small parcels by untracked 2nd Class, compare them with Tracked 48. On the October prices Tracked 48 is the cheaper of the two.
3. Look again at anything you send as a small parcel that could pack flat. A 2nd Class large letter up to 750g stays at £2.70 online. Our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) and [packaging guide](/guides/packaging-cheaply) cover the size limits.
4. If you buy postage at a Post Office counter, work out what buying online would save you. On a Tracked 48 small parcel the gap is 95p from October.
5. Update postage policies and templates on every platform you sell on in one go, so the numbers match everywhere.

Check the [current postage prices page](https://www.royalmail.com/current-postage-prices) before relying on any figure here. Royal Mail has already published several price changes this year.

## What we do not know yet

How and when each marketplace passes the new prices on to labels bought through it. Some platforms set their own postage prices, so check the price shown in your selling account after 5 October rather than assuming it matches Royal Mail's guide.

Royal Mail has also announced a peak surcharge for business account customers from 2 November 2026 to 10 January 2027, on its [surcharges page](https://www.royalmail.com/surcharges). The prices page does not list a peak surcharge for online or Post Office postage.
