This is a historical page. The 2024 figures below have been replaced by later rate cards, including the [2025 changes](/blog/amazon-returns-processing-fee-2025) and the [2026 changes](/blog/amazon-2026-fee-changes-fba-and-clothing). It is here so you can see how the fees got to where they are.

## The short version

- New FBA fulfilment fees took effect on [5 February 2024](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/a20cc09a-b8e9-47a0-8a69-d92756ff290f).
- The aged inventory surcharge roughly doubled for stock between 271 and 365 days old from 15 February 2024.
- Monthly storage fees rose on several tiers from 1 March 2024.
- A new low-inventory cost coverage fee started on 1 April 2024, but only for Pan-EU sellers. UK-only stock was not affected.
- A returns processing fee for high-return-rate products was also scheduled. It was later delayed to 1 February 2025.

## Who it affects

**In:** FBA sellers on Amazon.co.uk, especially those holding stock for nine months or more. Sellers using Pan-European FBA for standard-size products sold in Germany, France, Italy and Spain.

**Not in:** sellers using FBA only in the UK were not charged the low-inventory fee. Merchant-fulfilled orders were not affected by any of the FBA changes.

## What changed

UK figures from the [February 2024 rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/240201-FBA-Rate-Card-UK.pdf):

| UK fee | Before | After |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Aged inventory surcharge, 271 to 300 days (per cubic foot per month) | £1.11 | £2.32 (15 February 2024) |
| Aged inventory surcharge, over 365 days | £4.30 | £5.71 (15 February 2024) |
| Storage, standard-size, most categories, Oct to Dec | £1.10 | £1.21 (1 March 2024) |
| Storage, standard-size clothing, shoes, bags, Jan to Sep | £0.47 | £0.52 (1 March 2024) |
| Storage, oversize, Jan to Sep | £0.46 | £0.51 (1 March 2024) |
| Low-inventory cost coverage fee (Pan-EU only) | None | Per-unit fee in euros (1 April 2024) |

The storage rate for ordinary standard-size stock from January to September stayed at £0.78. The storage utilisation surcharge was also reworked from 1 June 2024 so that it started at a lower ratio of stock to sales. The rate card has the bands.

**The low-inventory fee** applied to standard-size Pan-EU products when both the 90 day and 30 day historical days of supply fell below 28 days. In plain terms: if you kept too little stock relative to your sales across the four big EU stores, Amazon charged extra per unit shipped. The rate card set it at up to €0.52 a unit, depending on size and how low your cover was.

## What it means for your money

If you held 100 cubic feet of ordinary standard-size stock from October to December, storage went from £110 to £121 a month. If 10 cubic feet of that was between 271 and 300 days old, the aged surcharge on it went from £11.10 to £23.20 a month. The message was clear: Amazon wanted stock to turn faster, and slow stock became much more expensive to keep.

## What to do now

1. Nothing on this page is current. Use the latest rate card in Seller Central for today's figures.
2. The lesson still holds: watch the inventory age report and clear stock well before the aged surcharge starts.
3. Pan-EU sellers should keep enough cover to stay above 28 days of supply, especially after a stockout.
