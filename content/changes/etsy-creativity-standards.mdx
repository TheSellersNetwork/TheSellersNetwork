## The short version

- On 9 July 2024 Etsy [restructured its rules](https://www.etsy.com/seller-handbook/article/1276491338090) around four types of item: made by, designed by, handpicked by and sourced by a seller.
- Etsy said this clarified its rules rather than changing them, but it put the lines in writing, and listing pages now show which type each item is.
- For resellers: vintage must be at least [20 years old](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity). Newer items that only look vintage are not allowed.
- Craft supplies and party supplies can be resold. Ready-to-use goods, such as blank t-shirts or resold clothing, cannot.
- AI-generated art is allowed if it comes from your own prompts and you say so in the listing. Selling bundles of AI prompts is not.

## Who it affects

**Most affected:** vintage sellers, craft supply sellers and anyone who has been listing resold new goods on Etsy. These are the groups the rules draw the clearest lines around.

**Also affected:** makers who use production partners or print on demand, and sellers of AI-generated art, who have disclosure rules to follow.

**Not affected:** sellers on other platforms. This is Etsy's policy only, and applies wherever you are based.

## What changed

The [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/legal/creativity) sort every allowed item into one of four types. The parts that matter most to a reseller:

| Type | Allowed | Not allowed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Handpicked: vintage | Items 20 years old or more that you chose yourself | Commercial items under 20 years old, even if made to look vintage |
| Handpicked: from nature | Rocks, shells, fossils, dried flowers you found or grew | Resold natural items, such as bulk landscape stone |
| Handpicked: gift baskets | Themed sets of different item types you chose and packed, with every item listed | Single-brand baskets, one item type in bulk, mystery boxes |
| Sourced: craft supplies | Tools and materials mainly for making things by hand, craft blanks, craft kits | Blanks ready to use as they are, generic cooking, DIY, office or gardening supplies |
| Sourced: party supplies | Decorations for a specific event | Items with everyday uses, general seasonal decor, costumes, party favours |
| Designed | Your own designs, printed by a partner or sold as downloads, and AI art from your own prompts | Scans or bundles of other people's work, AI prompt bundles |
| Made | Items you made, altered or assembled with real skill | Flat-pack or kit items built to the maker's instructions, items with only a sticker added |

Etsy also sets photo rules. Handpicked items, including vintage, must be shown with photos or video of the actual item. Sourced items, such as craft supplies, can use the manufacturer's images, subject to Etsy's intellectual property rules. Etsy may ask vintage sellers for more evidence of an item's source, age, designer or materials.

Sellers using a production partner must say so and give accurate information about where the item ships from. AI-generated items must be disclosed in the listing description.

The policy page was last updated on 10 June 2025, so it has moved since the July 2024 launch.

## What it means for your money

There is no new fee. The cost is in what you can no longer list. Etsy says it can remove listings that break the policy and that you still owe any fees on them. So a listing removed for being out of policy loses you the listing fee as well as the sale.

For a vintage seller, the real risk is dating. An item from 2010 is not yet vintage on Etsy in 2026. Listing it as vintage is the kind of thing that gets listings taken down.

## What to do now

1. Go through your vintage listings and check every one is at least 20 years old. Remove or move anything newer to another platform.
2. Make sure every vintage listing uses your own photos of the actual item, not stock or catalogue images.
3. Craft supply sellers: check nothing you list is really a finished, ready-to-use product. Blank t-shirts, hats and tumblers are named as not allowed.
4. If you sell mixed bundles, check each has a clear theme, different types of item, and a full list of contents. Mystery boxes are out.
5. If you use AI for any design, say so in the listing description. If you use a production partner, say so and give the right dispatch location.
6. Anything Etsy will not take, such as newer resold clothing, is better placed on a platform built for it. Our [multi-channel selling guide](/guides/multi-channel-selling) covers where to list what.
