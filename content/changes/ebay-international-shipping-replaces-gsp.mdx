## The short version

- eBay International Shipping (eIS) is taking over from the Global Shipping Programme (GSP) for eligible ebay.co.uk sellers. [Enrolment happens in phases from August 2026](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping), and eBay emails you when your account moves.
- You still post the parcel to a UK hub. eBay handles the international leg, customs paperwork and returns.
- [International selling fees are waived](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) on eIS orders, and the buyer pays the international postage and import charges.
- Once the hub scans your parcel, a return, loss or damage claim does not come out of your payout.
- You can opt out, but once you have been moved to eIS, [GSP is no longer available to you](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) even if you leave eIS.

For most sellers who already use GSP this is good news: fewer fees and less risk on overseas sales.

## Who it affects

In:

- Sellers with [listings on ebay.co.uk](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) whose items are physically in the UK.
- Items in eligible categories, up to [£900](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) per shipment.
- Accounts that meet eBay's standards. The [help page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348) lists Above Standard or Top Rated performance, low or average service metrics, eligibility for seller protections, and following eBay's rules.

Out:

- Restricted items. eBay's list includes alcohol, live animals, some batteries, weapons, e-bikes, plants and supplements. Check [the full list](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) if you sell anything near those lines.
- Parcels over the size and weight limits on the [help page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348) (31.5kg, and length and volume limits).
- Sellers who post abroad themselves with their own carrier. Nothing forces you into eIS for those orders.

## What changed

| | Global Shipping Programme | eBay International Shipping |
|---|---|---|
| Countries reached | [105](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) | [195 or more](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) |
| International selling fees | Charged as normal | [Waived on eIS orders](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) |
| Payout protection on returns | Not built in | [Built in](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping): eBay refunds the buyer and you keep the payout once the hub has scanned the parcel |
| Lost or damaged abroad | See the [GSP help page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/global-shipping-programme?id=4646) | [eBay refunds the buyer and you keep the sale](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348) |
| Buyer return window | Your returns policy | [30 days from delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348) |
| Can you use GSP? | Yes | No. Once moved, GSP is gone for you even if you opt out |

The way you work day to day barely changes. You still send the item to a UK hub. What changes is who carries the risk after that point: under eIS, eBay does.

eBay also says eIS is [up to 30% cheaper than GSP](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) on its busiest routes for parcels up to 3kg. That saving is on the postage the buyer pays, which can make your items more attractive abroad, but it is not money in your pocket directly.

## What it means for your money

The main saving is the international fee. For business sellers, eBay's [business fees page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-business-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4809) lists an international fee that varies by destination. For example, it shows 1.8% for the US and Canada. On a £200 order to the US, that is £3.60 you no longer pay when the order goes through eIS. For other destinations, check the current rate on that page.

The second saving is harder to put a number on: returns. Under eIS, if an overseas buyer sends an item back after the hub has scanned it, eBay deals with the refund and you keep the sale. You also do not have to receive or inspect the returned item. If overseas returns have cost you in the past, this is where most of the value is.

We have not added a calculator here because the international fee depends on the destination.

## What to do now

1. Watch your inbox for the enrolment email. It gives your exact date.
2. Check your listings are eligible: item located in the UK, category not restricted, value under £900.
3. Think hard before opting out. If you leave eIS, you cannot return to GSP, and you would have to post internationally yourself.
4. If you want to leave, the [Seller Centre page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) gives the path: My eBay account, Postage Preferences, eBay Shipping Options, then opt out. The [help page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348) says you can opt back in to eIS 24 hours later.
5. Review your country exclusions in the same settings if there are places you do not want to sell to.
6. Keep packing well. The protection only starts once the hub accepts and scans the parcel.

## What we do not know yet

- The exact order in which accounts are moved. eBay has not said who goes first.
- How the £900 limit is counted. The [Seller Centre page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/postage/ebay-international-shipping) says excluding duties, while the [help page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/shipping-items/setting-shipping-options/ebay-international-shipping-program?id=5348) says including shipping. Treat items close to £900 with care until this is clarified.
- Whether private sellers get the same fee waiver. The help page frames the fee saving for business sellers, while private sellers pay a separate [3% fee on items sent outside the UK](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822). If you are a private seller, check your first eIS sale's fees.
- When GSP closes completely. No end date has been announced.

This is being rolled out now, so check the current page before relying on this.
