## The short version

- Royal Mail's annual price change took effect on 7 April 2026. A 1st Class stamp went from £1.70 to £1.80 and a 2nd Class stamp from 87p to 91p.
- For online postage, the lightest 2nd Class large letter stayed at £1.55, but the heavier large letter bands went up 10p to 20p.
- The biggest rise for resellers was the 2nd Class small parcel bought online: up 45p, from £3.50 to £3.95.
- Tracked 48 and Tracked 24 rose by 10p to 20p on large letters and small parcels.
- This is not the last move this year. Royal Mail has published another set of prices for [5 October 2026](/blog/royal-mail-prices-october-2026), so check the current page before you reprice.

## Who it affects

Everyone who posts with Royal Mail, whether you buy stamps, pay at a Post Office counter or buy labels online through Royal Mail or a marketplace that sells Royal Mail postage.

The figures below are Royal Mail's own online prices, which are what most sellers pay when they buy labels online. Post Office counter prices are higher for parcels and are on the [April 2026 stamp prices wallchart](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-03/uk-stamp-prices-wallchart-april-2026-v1-ta.pdf). Business account and franking customers have their own price guides, linked from the [2026 prices page](https://www.royalmail.com/prices2026).

## What changed

The "before" column is Royal Mail's [online price guide from 6 October 2025](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2025-09/online-price-guide-october-2025-v2-ta.pdf), which was in force until the change. The "after" column is the [online price guide from 7 April 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-03/online-price-guide-april-2026-v1-ta.pdf).

| Service (online price) | Before | After | Change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1st Class letter, up to 100g | £1.70 | £1.80 | +10p |
| 2nd Class letter, up to 100g | 87p | 91p | +4p |
| 2nd Class large letter, up to 100g | £1.55 | £1.55 | none |
| 2nd Class large letter, up to 250g | £1.80 | £1.90 | +10p |
| 2nd Class large letter, up to 500g | £2.20 | £2.40 | +20p |
| 2nd Class large letter, up to 750g | £2.50 | £2.70 | +20p |
| 1st Class large letter, up to 100g | £3.05 | £3.20 | +15p |
| 2nd Class small parcel, up to 2kg | £3.50 | £3.95 | +45p |
| 1st Class small parcel, up to 2kg | £4.40 | £5.15 | +75p |
| Tracked 48 large letter, up to 1kg | £2.75 | £2.85 | +10p |
| Tracked 48 small parcel, up to 2kg | £3.55 | £3.65 | +10p |
| Tracked 48 medium parcel, up to 2kg | £5.35 | £5.55 | +20p |
| Tracked 24 large letter, up to 1kg | £3.65 | £3.80 | +15p |
| Tracked 24 small parcel, up to 2kg | £4.45 | £4.65 | +20p |

The stamp prices match Royal Mail's wallcharts for [April 2025](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2025-03/uk-stamp-prices-wallchart-april-2025-v1-ta.pdf) and [April 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-03/uk-stamp-prices-wallchart-april-2026-v1-ta.pdf).

The pattern matters more than any single line. Untracked parcels rose much faster than tracked ones. Before April, a Tracked 48 small parcel online cost 5p more than a 2nd Class small parcel. After April it cost 30p less (£3.65 against £3.95), so for small parcels the tracked service became the cheaper option as well as the safer one.

## What it means for your money

Two round examples, using only the figures above.

- If you send 50 small parcels a month by 2nd Class bought online, you pay 45p more on each, so £22.50 more a month.
- If you send 100 clothing items a month as 2nd Class large letters between 100g and 250g, you pay 10p more on each, so £10 more a month.

If you were already using Tracked 48 for small parcels, the rise is 10p a parcel, or £5 on 50 parcels.

## What to do now

1. Check your listing postage charges against the [current online prices](https://www.royalmail.com/current-postage-prices). Anything priced on the old figures is now losing you money on every sale.
2. If you still send small parcels by untracked 2nd Class, compare them with Tracked 48 in the April 2026 guide. On the April prices Tracked 48 was cheaper for a small parcel up to 2kg.
3. Look at what you send as a small parcel that could pack flat. The gap between a 2nd Class large letter and a small parcel is still over £1. Our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) and [packaging guide](/guides/packaging-cheaply) cover the size limits.
4. Update any postage policies or templates on eBay, Vinted and your own site in one go, so the numbers match everywhere.
5. Before you do all of this, read our breakdown of the [October 2026 prices](/blog/royal-mail-prices-october-2026). It may be worth making one change, not two.
