## The short version

- Referral fees fell sharply on cheap items in four areas: Home up to £20, Pet Clothing and Food up to £10, and Grocery and Vitamins up to £10 ([announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/a3b6d192-d78f-4b16-add1-e251e8959783)).
- Low-Price FBA, the cheaper fulfilment rate for small, light, low-priced items, now covers most products at £20 or less, up from £10.
- The variable fee on Best Deals and Lightning Deals is now capped at £200 in the UK.
- All of this started on 5 January 2026, earlier than the 1 February date first announced.
- Good news for anyone selling cheap everyday items. Nothing gets more expensive in this part of the package.

## Who it affects

**In:** sellers of items priced at £20 or less in the Home Products category, and items at £10 or less in Pet Clothing and Food, Grocery and Gourmet, and Vitamins, Minerals and Supplements. FBA sellers of small, light items up to £20. Sellers who run Best Deals or Lightning Deals.

**Not in:** items above those price points, which stay at the higher rate. Several categories keep the old £10 Low-Price FBA limit: the [February 2026 rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260114-FBA-Rate-Card-EN.pdf) lists Beauty, Health and Personal Care; Business, Industrial and Scientific Supplies; Office Products; Grocery and Gourmet; Books; Amazon Device Accessories; and Kitchen.

"Home Products" and "Vitamins, Minerals and Supplements" are fee categories Amazon created for this change. Your item's fee category is not always the category you would guess, so check it in the revenue calculator.

## What changed

| Fee | Before | After (from 5 January 2026) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home Products, items up to £20 | 15% | 8% |
| Pet Clothing and Food, items up to £10 | 15% | 5% |
| Grocery and Gourmet, items up to £10 | 8% | 5% |
| Vitamins, Minerals and Supplements, items up to £10 | 8% | 5% |
| Low-Price FBA price limit (most categories) | £10 | £20 |
| Best Deals and Lightning Deals variable fee cap (UK) | See Seller Central | £200 |

Figures are from the [January announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/a3b6d192-d78f-4b16-add1-e251e8959783) and the [rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260114-FBA-Rate-Card-EN.pdf). Above each price point, the rate card shows 15% for all four categories.

Amazon says the wider Low-Price FBA band saves [about 40p a unit on average](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/a3b6d192-d78f-4b16-add1-e251e8959783) on the products it now covers. The Low-Price FBA rates themselves are in the rate card; they apply only to small, light items, so a £15 item in a big box will not qualify.

These cuts were part of a larger package [announced in December](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/b3ec0524-ae08-4f43-85fe-81988e194d7c). The rest of that package, including some storage and removal fee rises, is covered on its own page.

## What it means for your money

A Home item selling at £16 used to cost £2.40 in referral fee at 15%. At 8% it costs £1.28, so you keep £1.12 more per sale.

A bag of pet treats at £8 used to cost £1.20 at 15%. At 5% it costs 40p, so 80p more per sale.

A grocery item at £6 used to cost 48p at 8%. At 5% it is 30p, 18p more per sale. On very cheap items, check whether a per-item minimum referral fee applies in your category, as that can override the percentage.

## What to do now

1. Run your cheapest products through the [revenue calculator](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) to see which fee category Amazon puts them in and whether they now get Low-Price FBA rates.
2. Look at items priced just over £20 (Home) or £10 (Pet, Grocery, Vitamins). A small price cut may drop them into the lower rate, and the saving can be bigger than the price you give up.
3. Recheck Low-Price FBA eligibility on small, light items between £10 and £20 in the categories it now covers.
4. Revisit products you rejected as too thin on margin. Some low-priced lines that did not work at 15% may work at 8% or 5%.
5. If you run large deals, check the deal fee in Seller Central before booking, as the cap changes the maths on high-volume deals.

## What we do not know yet

- The UK deal fee cap before the change was not stated in the announcement. Check the deals fee page in Seller Central for current figures.
- Fee categories and thresholds can move each year. Check the current rate card before relying on this.
