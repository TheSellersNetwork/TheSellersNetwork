This policy explains what personal information The Sellers Network collects when you use the site, why, who else handles it, how long we keep it, and what you can ask us to do with it. It is written to be read. If anything is unclear, [contact us](/contact).

## Who we are

<OperatorDetails />

We are the controller of your personal information for the purposes of UK data protection law (the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

## What we collect and why

| What | Why | Lawful basis |
|---|---|---|
| Your email address and sign-in codes | To create your account and let you sign in | Contract: we need it to provide the account you asked for |
| Username, optional display name, bio, flair, the platforms you sell on | To run your profile | Contract |
| Your posts, replies, photos, likes, poll votes, bookmarks, follows and setups | To run the forum | Contract |
| When you were last active, and which forums you have visited | To show who is online, mark unread topics and set trust levels | Legitimate interests: running a useful, spam-resistant forum |
| Your IP address, briefly | To stop spam and abuse (rate limits, bot checks) | Legitimate interests: security |
| Reports, flags and moderation decisions about you | To keep the forum safe and meet our legal duties | Legal obligation and legitimate interests |
| Who wrote an anonymous post | So staff can act on reports and legal notices about it | Legitimate interests: safety and legal compliance |
| Your newsletter email address, and when and where you signed up | To send the newsletter you asked for | Consent, which you can withdraw at any time |
| Push notification details from your browser | To send the notifications you turned on | Consent, withdrawn by turning notifications off |
| Analytics about how pages are used | To understand what works and improve the site | Consent, given in the cookie banner |
| Messages you send through the contact form | To answer you and keep a record of complaints and notices | Legitimate interests, and legal obligation for legal notices |

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising profiles. We do not ask for your real name, address or payment details.

## What is public

Your username, display name, profile, flair, posts and replies are public: anyone on the internet can read them and search engines can index them. So can the fact that you were recently active. Do not post anything you would not want a stranger to read, and check your photos for addresses, faces, documents or order details before you upload them.

**Anonymous posts** hide your name from other members and the public. Staff can still see who wrote them, and we may disclose that if the law requires it or a court orders it.

## Who else handles your information

We use a small number of service providers (processors) who handle information only on our instructions:

| Provider | What for |
|---|---|
| Supabase | Database, sign-in and file storage |
| Vercel | Hosting the website |
| Resend | Sending emails |
| PostHog (EU hosting) | Analytics, only if you accept analytics |
| Cloudflare Turnstile | Checking that sign-ups are not bots |

Some of these companies are based in, or use servers in, the United States. Where your information leaves the UK, we rely on the protections UK law allows for this: the UK-US data bridge where the provider is certified under it, or the International Data Transfer Addendum in the provider's data processing terms.

We may also share information if the law requires it, to protect people from harm, or in connection with a legal claim.

## How long we keep it

| Information | How long |
|---|---|
| Your account and profile | Until you delete your account |
| Your posts | Until you delete them or your account. When you delete your account, your posts stay but show as "Deleted member", unless you choose to delete them too |
| IP addresses used for rate limits | 30 days |
| Newsletter sign-ups never confirmed | 30 days |
| Newsletter subscription | Until you unsubscribe, then we keep your address on a suppression list so we never email you again |
| Moderation records, reports and legal notices | 3 years |
| Analytics | 12 months |

## Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your information (you can also download most of it yourself from your [account page](/account))
- correct information that is wrong
- delete your information (you can delete your account yourself from your [account page](/account))
- restrict or object to how we use it, including objecting to anything we do on the basis of legitimate interests
- move your information to another service
- stop using it where we rely on your consent

Use the [contact form](/contact) and choose "My data". We will reply within one month. There is no charge.

## Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your information, tell us through the [contact form](/contact) first. We will acknowledge your complaint within 30 days and tell you what we are doing about it. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at [ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint](https://ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint/).

## Children

The Sellers Network is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, [tell us](/report) and we will remove it.

## Cookies

We use a few cookies and similar storage to keep you signed in and remember your choices, and analytics only if you agree. The full list is on our [cookies page](/cookies).

## Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will say so on the site before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
