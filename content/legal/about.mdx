The Sellers Network is a free forum for people in the UK who sell online: on eBay, Amazon, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace, Whatnot, TikTok Shop, Depop, Etsy and their own sites. It exists because the answers sellers need are usually buried in official forums that lock threads, or in paid groups that want something back. Here you can ask a question and get an answer from someone who has actually done it.

## How it works

- **Free to join and read.** No paid tiers for the forums.
- **No selling in the threads.** Members come here to learn, not to be sold to.
- **Real numbers welcome.** The best answers say what someone did and what happened.
- **Fee and policy changes explained.** When a platform changes its fees or rules, we write it up in plain English with a link to the official announcement, and members compare notes underneath.

## Who writes what

Forum posts are written by members and are their own views. Guides, the blog and the fee and policy breakdowns are written by the site's own writers from official sources, with every figure linked to where it came from. **Jamie Callaghan** and **Rachel Doyle** are the pen names our staff writers use on the blog; they are not separate people with their own qualifications, and nothing we publish is tax, legal or financial advice.

## How it is paid for

The forum is free. To cover the cost of running it, some pages carry adverts from businesses useful to sellers. Adverts are always labelled **Ad**, and where we earn a commission from a link it is labelled **Ad · affiliate link**. Advertisers do not get a say in what members post or what we write, and cannot pay to have posts removed or promoted. Our [partners page](/partners) lists every business we work with; entries marked **Partner, unpaid** are ones where no money or free products change hands.

## Keeping it safe

Anyone can [report content](/report), with or without an account. We explain moderation decisions, never lock a thread for criticising a platform or us, and anyone can appeal. The details are in our [terms](/terms) and [house rules](/community/rules).
