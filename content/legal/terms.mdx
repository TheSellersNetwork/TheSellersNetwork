These terms are the agreement between you and us when you use The Sellers Network. They are written to be read. The short version: be useful, be honest, do not sell in the threads, and do not post anything illegal. The [house rules](/community/rules) are part of these terms.

## Who can use the site

- You must be **18 or over** to create an account. Anyone can read the public forums.
- **One account per person.** Business accounts are fine if you say so on your profile.
- Keep your sign-in to yourself. You are responsible for what is posted from your account.

## What you post

- **You own what you write.** By posting, you give us a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to host, show, copy and format it on the site and in our newsletter and guides, and to keep showing it after you delete your account if you choose to leave your posts (they then show as "Deleted member"). You can delete your posts at any time.
- **Only post what you have the right to post.** That includes photos and screenshots. Remove order numbers, addresses, names and faces of other people before you upload.
- **Do not post anything illegal.** That includes fraud and scams, stolen or counterfeit goods, threats, harassment, hate, content that sexualises children, terrorism, encouraging self-harm, and sharing other people's personal information without their consent.
- **Do not post anything defamatory.** Say what happened to you; do not make claims about people or businesses you cannot back up.
- **No selling in the threads.** No links to your listings or streams, no account trading, no feedback swaps, no undisclosed promotion. If you work for, or are paid by, a business you mention, say so.
- **No advice on evading platform rules, tax or the law.**
- **Anonymous posts** hide your name from members and the public, but not from staff. The rules still apply, and we may identify you if the law requires it.

## How we moderate

We aim to keep intervention light and explain our decisions.

- Posts that break these terms may be edited, hidden or removed. Posts reported by several established members are hidden automatically until staff review them.
- For repeated or serious breaches we may give a warning, then a temporary suspension, then remove the account. We may skip steps for illegal content, scams or threats.
- We keep a record of moderation decisions for 3 years.
- We do not lock or remove threads for criticising a platform, a fee or us. We remove posts for breaking the rules, not for being critical.

## Reporting content

Anyone can report content, with or without an account, using the [report form](/report). Members can also use the flag button on any post. We look at reports of illegal content as a priority, usually within 48 hours, and remove illegal content when we become aware of it.

If you think something about you is **defamatory** or uses your **copyright** work, use the [report form](/report) and choose the matching option. A flag on the post is not enough for a defamation complaint, because the law asks for particular information.

For defamation, we follow the process in section 5 of the Defamation Act 2013 and the Defamation (Operators of Websites) Regulations 2013. In short: within two working days of a complete complaint we send it to the person who posted the statement (without the complainant's name or email unless they agreed), or remove the statement if we have no way to contact them privately. The poster then has until midnight at the end of the fifth day after we contact them to reply. If they do not reply in time, agree to removal, or object without giving their full name and postal address, we remove the statement. If they object and give those details, the statement can stay up, and we pass their details to the complainant only if they agree or a court orders us to. ## Complaints and appeals

- If you disagree with a moderation decision about your post or account, you can **appeal** through the [contact form](/contact), even if your account is suspended. Someone who did not make the original decision will review it, usually within 7 days, and tell you the outcome and why.
- For any other **complaint**, including about how we handle reports or your data, use the [contact form](/contact). We acknowledge complaints within 30 days.

## Ending your account

You can delete your account at any time from your [account page](/account). We may suspend or close accounts that break these terms, as described above. If we close your account, you can still ask for a copy of your data.

Guides, blog posts, fee breakdowns, calculators and forum posts are **general information**, not tax, legal or financial advice. Figures come from official sources and are dated, but fees and rules change: always check the linked official page before you rely on a figure, and speak to a qualified adviser about your own situation. Forum posts are members' own views and experience; we do not check them before they appear.

Some pages include clearly labelled adverts and affiliate links, and our [partners page](/partners) explains how the site is funded.
