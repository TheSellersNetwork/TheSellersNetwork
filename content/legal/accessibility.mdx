We want everyone who sells online to be able to use this site, including people who use a screen reader, keyboard, magnification or voice control.

## What we aim for

We build to the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. That means, for example:

- every page works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline and a "Skip to content" link
- text has enough contrast in both light and dark mode, and you can zoom to 200% without losing content
- images have text descriptions, and forms have labels
- animation is reduced if your device asks for reduced motion
- you can choose a different forum layout, including a more compact one, from your [account page](/account)

## What we know is not right yet

- Photos members upload do not have a text description yet.
- Some charts and timelines are easier to read visually than with a screen reader. The same information is always in the text as well.

## Asking for help

If something is hard to use, or you need information in another format, use the [contact form](/contact) and choose "Accessibility or a reasonable adjustment". We will reply within 7 days. That includes help with reporting, appeals or anything else that involves a process on this site.
