This is for UK resellers who sell on more than one platform, or are about to, and want to know when the money from a sale actually lands in the bank. By the end you will know, for eBay, Vinted, Amazon, Depop, Etsy, TikTok Shop, Whatnot and Facebook Marketplace, when funds become available, how payouts work, what holds new sellers face, and what each platform publishes about payout fees and minimums. Everything comes from each platform's own help pages or terms, read in September 2026. Payout rules change, so check the linked page before you plan around a date. This is general information, not financial advice.

## The short version

- Almost every platform now pays after **delivery**, not after the sale. Tracking is what starts the clock, so untracked parcels usually mean a longer wait.
- The quickest published routes are eBay for qualifying private sellers (typically within 24 hours of the buyer paying) and Whatnot's Early Payout (available when you create the label), both only once you meet their criteria.
- The slowest start is TikTok Shop for new shops: 31 days after delivery on the introductory settlement period.
- Amazon holds each order until seven days after delivery by default (DD+7), then pays on a fourteen day cycle or when you ask.
- Etsy's new sellers wait about 14 days after a sale, and Etsy can hold a percentage of each sale in a reserve.
- None of the pages we read lists a fee for moving your own money to a UK bank. Minimums are published by Vinted (£0.10), Amazon (£1) and Etsy (£15, daily deposits only).
- Facebook's UK help pages say selling with delivery is not available in the UK, so Marketplace sales here are local and paid directly by the buyer.
- On payout set-up alone, we would choose eBay for a reseller who needs cash flow: business sellers are typically paid 24 hours after the buyer pays, daily by default. New business sellers can wait around a month first.

## What matters most for resellers

Before the table, the questions that decide your cash flow:

- **Trigger.** Does the clock start at payment, dispatch or delivery?
- **Extra days.** How long after that trigger before money is available?
- **New seller rules.** Is there an introductory period, reserve or hold, and what gets you out of it?
- **Getting it out.** Automatic payouts, on demand, or both? How long does the bank transfer take?
- **Costs and limits.** Any payout fee, and any minimum amount?
- **Tracking.** Does using the platform's own label speed things up?

## eBay

eBay pays private and business sellers differently ([getting paid](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814)).

**Private sellers.** Funds go into your eBay balance. For tracked items they are typically available 2 calendar days after delivery is confirmed. Untracked items, or tracked ones with no delivery confirmation, wait 14 calendar days from the order date. Orders of £10 or less, and tracked orders of £750 or less, can be released straight away when the buyer leaves positive feedback. Since August 2025, private sellers with at least 10 completed sales worth £150 or more in the last 5 years, and no more than 2 open requests, cases or transaction defects in the last 12 months, typically get funds within 24 hours of the buyer paying. You then withdraw to your bank whenever you like, and eBay says the bank transfer takes 0 to 4 business days.

**Business sellers.** Funds are typically available 24 hours after the buyer's payment is confirmed. Payouts are daily by default; you can switch to weekly, fortnightly or monthly and request funds on demand in between ([payouts on demand](https://www.ebay.co.uk/sellercentre/selling/managed-payments/payouts-on-demand)).

**Holds.** eBay can hold funds for open cases, account issues or new and infrequent business sellers. It says holds are usually resolved within 30 days, new business sellers typically receive funds within 31 days, and payment disputes can take up to 90 days or more ([payments on hold](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/payouts-work-managed-payments-sellers/payments-hold-managed-payments-seller?id=4816)). See our [eBay payment holds guide](/guides/ebay-payment-holds).

**Good**
- Qualifying private sellers are typically paid within 24 hours of payment, before delivery.
- Business sellers choose their own payout schedule and can withdraw on demand if not on daily payouts.
- Tracking uploaded on time is the main lever, and Simple Delivery uploads it automatically.

**Bad**
- Untracked private sales wait 14 days from the order.
- New business sellers can wait around a month.
- No payout minimum is published on the pages we read.

## Vinted

Money from a sale sits in your **pending balance** until the order completes. An order completes when the buyer presses "Everything is OK", or automatically 2 days after delivery if the buyer neither confirms nor reports a problem. The payment then moves to your available balance within 2 days ([getting paid for a completed sale](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/235-getting-paid-for-a-completed-sale), [pending balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/460-pending-balance)).

Vinted Pro orders are different. Because buyers from a Pro seller have a 14 day right to withdraw, those orders complete automatically after 14 days unless the item is returned.

Withdrawals go only to a UK bank account in pounds, the minimum is £0.10, and they can take up to 5 business days. You cannot use the same bank account for more than one Vinted account, and Vinted's payment provider may ask for an identity check, which pauses withdrawals until it is done ([how to withdraw](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/68-how-to-withdraw-earnings), [Vinted balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/437-vinted-balance-how-it-works)). Our [Vinted payments guide](/guides/vinted-payments-and-balance) goes into the detail.

**Good**
- Money moves after delivery plus a short window, even when a buyer never presses the button.
- Very low minimum withdrawal.
- No withdrawal fee is mentioned on Vinted's withdrawal or balance pages.

**Bad**
- Pro sellers wait at least 14 days per order, which slows cash for resellers.
- Withdrawals can take up to 5 business days.
- One bank account per Vinted account.

## Amazon

Amazon reserves your sale proceeds until the delivery date plus a reserve period. Amazon staff describe seven days after delivery as the "default standard setting", with the example of an item sold on 1 January, delivered on 6 January and available from 14 January. The period can be extended "following an assessment of your overall risk and historical performance" ([Amazon staff post](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/aac4b8e9-c776-418e-8373-9484fbf8baa1)).

Once funds are available, the UK payments agreement (last updated 28 August 2026) says Amazon transfers your balance automatically fourteen days after the first business day on which the transactions completed, and that you can request a transfer once in every 24 hours, with a minimum of £1. Amazon can also require a separate reserve and restrict access to your balance for pending chargebacks, disputes or identity checks ([Payments User Agreement, sections 2.6 and 2.7](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/GTVFVCWBJ6RJGKHM?locale=en-GB)). Amazon's own seller site sums it up as paying "available funds every two weeks" ([Sell on Amazon](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/sell-online)). The full mechanics, with FBA and FBM timelines, are in [Amazon DD+7 explained](/blog/amazon-dd7-payouts-explained).

**Good**
- You can request available funds every 24 hours instead of waiting for the cycle.
- FBA orders use Amazon's own delivery date, so the clock starts reliably.

**Bad**
- At least seven days after delivery before anything is available, and the reserve grows with your sales.
- FBM orders without valid tracking use the latest estimated delivery date, which is later.
- The criteria for extending your reserve are not published.

## Depop

Depop pays into your Depop Balance and then automatically to your bank. With Depop Shipping, it sends payment 2 business days after the item shows as delivered, or 10 business days after the sale, whichever comes first. With your own postage, it pays 10 business days after delivery. Funds can take up to 5 days to appear in your bank. Nothing is paid out until you have added a bank account and verified your identity ([how do I get paid, UK](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360001791147-How-do-I-get-paid-UK)).

Depop is also rolling out **Withdrawals**, where earnings become available once the item is delivered and you choose when to withdraw. It says this is not yet available to everyone ([withdrawals](https://depophelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-gb/articles/34471924527761-Withdrawals-Depop-Payments-only)).

**Good**
- With Depop Shipping, the longest wait is capped at 10 business days after the sale.
- Payouts are automatic once you are set up, with no withdrawal step.

**Bad**
- Posting your own way means waiting 10 business days after delivery.
- No payout fee or minimum is published on the pages we read.
- Some sellers are on different schedules, which Depop says is due to early sign-up or its Top Seller programme.

## Etsy

Etsy says new sellers' funds are generally eligible for deposit 14 days after a sale, possibly longer depending on the shop's standing, while established shops may have funds available the next business day ([why hasn't my money been deposited](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360002080688)). New sellers are on weekly deposits every Monday by default, and you can choose daily, weekly, fortnightly or monthly. Daily deposits in pounds need at least £15 available. Deposits can take up to 3 to 5 business days to reach your bank, and changing your bank details puts a 5 day hold on your funds ([receiving your deposit](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360046998234)).

Etsy can also apply a **Payment account reserve**, where a percentage of each physical sale is held for a set period. It names your first sale on Etsy, a sharp rise in orders or refunds, missing tracking and late dispatch as risk factors. Adding valid tracking that shows the order in transit releases the reserved funds for that order, and Star Sellers are not given a reserve ([Payment account reserve](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360058722214)).

**Good**
- A clear choice of deposit schedules.
- Tracking releases reserved funds early.
- No UK deposit fee in Etsy's published table.

**Bad**
- About two weeks' wait for new sellers, plus bank time.
- The reserve percentage and holding time vary by account and are only shown inside your Payment account.
- Daily deposits need £15 or more available.

## TikTok Shop

TikTok Shop pays after a settlement period that starts at delivery. New shops and shops in probation are on the **introductory** period of 31 days. The **standard** period is 8 days, **accelerated** is 3 days and **express** is 1 day, based on your Shop Performance Score and sales, reviewed at the start of each month. Shops under security checks can be put on a **deferred** period of 31 days ([Payment Settlement Period](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099475714&default_language=en-GB)).

After the period, TikTok starts a transfer if your balance is positive and your bank details are valid, which usually takes 3 business days. Shops that miss the seller-fault cancellation rate criteria can have funds held in a reserve for 30 calendar days after delivery ([Settlement on TikTok Shop](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5082281622406945&default_language=en-GB)). Our [TikTok Shop statements guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-statements-and-settlements) explains the statements themselves.

**Good**
- Established, high-scoring shops can be paid 1 to 3 days after delivery.
- Eligibility is visible on the Shop Health page.

**Bad**
- New shops wait 31 days after delivery, plus bank time.
- A missed cancellation target can add a 30 day reserve.
- No payout fee or minimum is published on the pages we read.

## Whatnot

For UK sellers, earnings become available only when delivery is confirmed **and** 96 hours have passed since the sale, then within up to 4 hours. Some shipping methods without standard tracking follow fixed timelines instead, for example 7 days after you enter a tracking number for your own label ([when you'll get paid](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360061604191)).

You can start a payout any time you have a positive available balance, to a bank account set up through Stripe, and it typically arrives within 1 to 2 business days ([set up and start payouts](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061604151-Set-up-and-start-payouts)). Sellers who qualify for **Early Payout** have earnings available as soon as they generate a shipping label. The criteria include a seller rating, a number of lifetime shows and a sales or orders milestone, shown in Seller Hub ([Early Payout](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4417983584141-Early-Payout-Program)).

**Good**
- Fast once delivery is confirmed, and bank transfers of 1 to 2 business days.
- Early Payout pays on label creation for established sellers.

**Bad**
- The 96 hour settlement period applies in the UK even if delivery is quick.
- Early Payout criteria changed from 31 August 2026, and the exact thresholds are only shown in Seller Hub.
- No payout fee or minimum is published on the pages we read.

## Facebook Marketplace

When we opened Facebook's help pages on selling with delivery and on when you get paid for it from the UK, both said the feature "isn't available in your location at the moment" ([when you'll get paid](https://www.facebook.com/help/620680138523186), [sell with delivery](https://www.facebook.com/help/773379109714742)). So for UK sellers, Marketplace is a local listing site: the buyer pays you directly, usually on collection, and Facebook is not involved in the payment. That makes the money immediate, but it also means none of Facebook's payout rules or protections apply. Our [collection safety guide](/guides/facebook-marketplace-collection-safety) covers taking payment safely.

**Good**
- Paid on the spot, with no platform hold.

**Bad**
- No shipped sales with Facebook checkout in the UK, according to its own help pages.
- Payment safety is entirely down to you.

## A worked example

Say you sell one item on each platform on the same day, it is delivered three days later, the buyer does nothing more, and you are a new seller everywhere. To keep it simple, treat every day as a working day and ignore bank transfer time. These are the earliest dates each set of published rules allows, not promises.

| Platform | Earliest the money is available to withdraw or be paid out |
| --- | --- |
| eBay, private seller, tracked | Day 5 (2 days after delivery) |
| Vinted, standard account | Order completes day 5, available by day 7 |
| Depop, Depop Shipping | Sent to your bank on day 5 |
| Whatnot | Day 4 (96 hours after the sale, with delivery already confirmed) |
| Amazon, DD+7 | Around day 11 (Amazon's own example releases funds 8 days after delivery), then the next automatic transfer or your request |
| Etsy, new seller | Around day 14, then your deposit schedule |
| TikTok Shop, introductory | Day 34, then about 3 business days to your bank |

## How to choose

This is about planning your cash. Ask yourself:

- How much stock money can you afford to have tied up in sales that have not paid out yet?
- Do you post with tracking every time? If not, which platforms penalise that with longer waits?
- Are you a new seller on this platform, and what does it take to leave the introductory period or reserve?
- Would you sell as a business on this platform, and does that change the timing (Vinted Pro and eBay business accounts both work differently)?
- Do you need to pay suppliers weekly? Match that to platforms that let you withdraw on demand.

Our view: for a reseller starting out, the new seller periods on TikTok Shop, Etsy and Amazon matter more than the headline fees, because they decide how long your first month's stock money is locked up. Our [Amazon cash flow guide](/guides/amazon-cash-flow-and-overheads) shows how to plan for that gap.

## Our verdict

This verdict is only about when the money arrives, not fees or buyers. On payout set-up alone, if we had to choose one platform for a typical UK reseller who needs steady cash flow, we would choose **eBay**. By typical we mean someone selling as a business, posting with tracking, and buying new stock each week from the money coming in.

Why:

- **The clock starts at payment, not delivery.** Business sellers' funds are typically available 24 hours after the buyer's payment is confirmed. Most of the other platforms here wait for delivery first.
- **Daily payouts by default**, with weekly, fortnightly or monthly schedules and payouts on demand if you prefer.
- **No payout fee** on the pages we read.

The honest catch: eBay says new or infrequent business sellers typically receive funds within 31 days, so the first month can be slow. Private sellers who meet eBay's criteria are also paid within 24 hours of payment, but a reseller buying to sell is usually a business seller.

Picks by situation:

- **If you are brand new everywhere and need money in the first month:** Depop with Depop Shipping, because payment is sent 2 business days after delivery and never later than 10 business days after the sale.
- **If you sell through live shows:** Whatnot, because UK earnings are available once delivery is confirmed and 96 hours have passed, payouts reach the bank in 1 to 2 business days, and Early Payout pays on label creation once you qualify.
- **If you sell on Amazon:** request your available balance every 24 hours rather than waiting for the fourteen day cycle, because the payments agreement allows it.
- **If you need cash the same day:** Facebook Marketplace, because UK sales are local and the buyer pays you directly, with no platform hold.

What we would avoid, and why:

- **Relying on a new TikTok Shop for cash flow**, because new shops wait 31 days after delivery, plus about 3 business days to the bank.
- **Vinted Pro as your only source of cash**, because Pro orders take 14 days to complete.

*We are not paid by any platform named here and there are no affiliate links. This is our opinion based on the published terms when we checked them in September 2026; check each platform's current terms before you plan around them.*

## Common mistakes

- **Counting sales as cash.** A sale that has not been delivered, or is inside a reserve, is not money you can spend yet.
- **Posting without tracking.** eBay makes untracked private sales wait 14 days, Amazon uses the latest estimated delivery date, and Etsy cannot release reserves early.
- **Changing bank details just before a big payout.** Etsy adds a 5 day hold when you do.
- **Using one bank account for two Vinted accounts.** Vinted says withdrawals will fail.
- **Forgetting the bank's own time.** Every platform adds 1 to 5 business days after it sends the money.
- **Assuming a schedule is permanent.** Settlement periods, reserves and fast-payout criteria are reviewed and can change.
