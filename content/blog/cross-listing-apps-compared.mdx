This is for UK resellers who list the same stock on Vinted, eBay and one or two other sites, and are wondering whether a cross-listing app is worth paying for. It puts seven apps side by side using only what each one publishes on its own pricing and product pages: price, Vinted support, automatic delisting and trial. It then sets out what Vinted's and eBay's own terms say about outside software, because that is the part the sales pages leave out. We have no arrangement with any of these companies, and our own pick, at the end, rests only on the published facts. It is general information, not legal or financial advice.

## The short version

- Cross-listing apps copy one listing to several marketplaces and, on some plans, remove it from the others when it sells. That second part, automatic delisting, is what stops you selling one item twice.
- Published prices run from £9 a month to about $100 a month. Most are listed in US dollars, and only one of the seven pricing pages we checked (Zipsale) says VAT is added.
- Several apps name Vinted, but support differs. One lists Vinted as a "Lite" marketplace where sales are not detected, and another names Vinted as supported but leaves it off its list of marketplaces where sales are detected.
- Vinted's terms ban external software tools, including bots, unless Vinted has authorised them. eBay's user agreement bans automated access unless eBay has given prior express permission.
- Some sellers report Vinted restrictions after using automation. Vinted does not publish figures, so we do not repeat any.
- The free route is copying and pasting by hand. Our [listing builder](/tools/listing-builder) makes a copy pack for eBay, Vinted, Depop and Etsy and never posts anything for you.
- For most resellers who sell on Vinted, we would choose the free copy-and-paste route and end sold listings by hand. If you do want an app, we would choose FLUF Connect. See our verdict below.

## What matters most for resellers

Before comparing prices, check these for your own set-up:

1. **Vinted UK support.** Does the app name Vinted, and does it say it works in the UK?
2. **Automatic delisting, and where it works.** Which plan includes it, and does it detect sales on Vinted or only on other sites?
3. **How often it checks for sales.** The gap between checks is the window in which you can sell the same item twice.
4. **The real monthly cost.** Currency, VAT, listing limits and whether the feature you need is only on a higher plan.
5. **How it connects to each marketplace.** Through the marketplace's own authorised route, or by acting inside your logged-in account.
6. **The trial.** Length, whether a card is needed, and what happens when it ends.

## Crosslist

Crosslist lists four plans: Bronze $29.99, Silver $34.99, Gold $39.99 and Diamond $44.99 a month, with 10% off quarterly and two months free on yearly billing ([pricing](https://crosslist.com/pricing/)). Plans are capped by new listings a month: 200, 500, 1,000 and unlimited. An AI add-on is $4.99 a month.

**Good**

- Its [Vinted page](https://crosslist.com/marketplaces/vinted) says it is available in the United Kingdom, as well as the US, Canada and Australia.
- Quarterly and yearly discounts are published.

**Bad**

- Automatic delisting is only on Gold and Diamond, so the two cheaper plans do not remove sold items for you.
- Its FAQ says the mobile app has all core features "except auto-delisting for some marketplaces".
- No free trial: a 3-day money-back guarantee applies if you have created fewer than 20 listings.
- The pricing page does not say whether VAT is added.

Our view: the same Vinted page says relisting is not against Vinted's terms "as long as it is not done excessively". Vinted's own terms give no number, so read the rules section below before relying on that.

## Vendoo

Vendoo lists Starter at $14.99, Growth at $29.99 and Pro at $59.99 a month, or $12.49, $24.99 and $49.99 a month billed yearly, with unlimited items on each and an Enterprise plan on request ([pricing](https://vendoo.co/pricing)).

**Good**

- A 14-day free trial on every plan.
- "Sale Detection and AutoDelisting" is on every plan, including Starter.
- Its [UK page](https://www.vendoo.co/uk) names eBay, Depop, Vinted, Facebook Marketplace, Etsy and Whatnot.

**Bad**

- The pricing page names the marketplaces where sales are detected as Poshmark, eBay, Mercari, Depop, Whatnot and Etsy. Vinted is not in that list, so check how a Vinted sale is handled before you rely on it.
- Prices are in US dollars, and VAT is not mentioned.

## List Perfectly

List Perfectly lists Simple at $29, Business at $49, Pro at $69 and Pro Plus from $99 a month ([pricing](https://listperfectly.com/pricing)). No annual prices are shown.

**Good**

- A "100 Free Listings Money Back Guarantee".
- Every plan covers the same marketplaces: Depop, eBay, Etsy, Facebook, Grailed, Mercari, Poshmark, Vestiaire Collective and Vinted.

**Bad**

- Vinted is a "Lite" marketplace. The pricing page says a sale on a Lite marketplace "isn't detected, so it won't trigger Auto Delist" on your other listings. A Vinted sale has to be ended elsewhere by hand.
- Auto Delist is only on Pro Plus, the most expensive plan.
- The page does not say whether Vinted UK specifically is covered, or give a currency or VAT position beyond the prices shown.

## Zipsale

Zipsale's UK site lists three ways to pay: a subscription from £15 a month plus VAT, listing credits from £0.18 an item plus VAT, and add-ons for Depop and Vinted sellers from £10 a month plus VAT ([pricing](https://www.zipsale.co.uk/pricing)).

**Good**

- Prices are in pounds and clearly stated as plus VAT.
- It names eBay, Etsy, Depop, Poshmark, Shopify, WooCommerce, ASOS and Vinted.
- A pay-per-item option if you do not want a subscription, and a free plan with no card needed.

**Bad**

- The FAQ says automatic delisting is not included in the free plan, and it does not cover Facebook Marketplace on any plan.
- The page does not say whether listing credits include automatic delisting.
- The Vinted and Depop add-ons are described only as "extra automation tools". Check exactly what they do before adding one to a Vinted account.

## FLUF Connect

FLUF Connect lists Starter at £9 a month (£59 a year), Seller at £29 (£199 a year), Pro at £99 (£709 a year) and a custom Super Seller plan ([pricing](https://fluf.io/pricing)).

**Good**

- The lowest published monthly price here, in pounds.
- The pricing FAQ says Vinted is supported "on every plan", and all plans include the same 50+ marketplaces.
- Sold items come down on other marketplaces on every plan. The pricing page says sales are checked every 90 minutes on Starter, 60 on Seller, 30 on Pro and 15 on Super Seller.
- A 7-day trial for £1.

**Bad**

- The cheaper the plan, the longer the gap between sales checks, so the bigger the window for a double sale.
- The pricing page does not mention VAT.

## SellerAider

SellerAider sells two products ([pricing](https://selleraider.com/pricing)). The crosslister is $12.99 a month (Standard) or $29.99 a month (Pro), or $140 and $299 a year. Separate "Grow" plans, from $18 a month, are automation tools that send offers, relist, refresh, share and send automatic messages.

**Good**

- A 14-day free trial.
- The Standard crosslister is one of the lower dollar prices here.

**Bad**

- "Automatic Delisting" is listed only on Crosslister Pro.
- Vinted appears on the pricing page, but the page does not say which countries' sites are covered.
- Our view: offers, relisting and messages sent automatically inside a Vinted account are the kind of activity Vinted's terms cover. See the rules section below before using any tool like this on Vinted.

## PrimeLister

PrimeLister's cross-listing plan is $49.99 a month or $399.99 a year, and its FAQ says amounts are charged in US dollars ([pricing](https://www.primelister.com/pricing/cross-listing)).

**Good**

- A 7-day free trial with no card required.
- Automatic delisting is included on both monthly and yearly billing.
- The pricing page names Poshmark, Mercari, Facebook Marketplace, eBay, Etsy, Depop, Whatnot and Vinted.

**Bad**

- The highest single monthly price in this comparison.
- Charged in dollars, so the sterling cost moves with the exchange rate.
- The page does not say whether Vinted UK or eBay UK specifically are supported.

## The platform rules: what Vinted and eBay say

This is the section most comparisons skip.

**Vinted.** Section 6 of Vinted's [terms and conditions](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions) lists what you agree not to do. It includes using "any kind of external software tools" such as bots, scraping programs and spiders when using the site, including to promote items or add items to favourites, unless Vinted has authorised, offered or otherwise allowed that use. The same section bans deleting and re-listing the same item multiple times, or multiple items in bulk.

Vinted does run an official route for software: Vinted Pro Integrations. Its [documentation](https://pro-docs.svc.vinted.com/) says it is available only to "a limited set of allowlisted Vinted Pro businesses". We found no Vinted UK page listing third-party cross-listing apps as authorised for standard accounts. If an app says Vinted allows it, ask the app for Vinted's written confirmation, and ask Vinted through its Help Centre.

Some sellers report restrictions on their Vinted accounts after using automation tools. Vinted does not publish how often this happens, and reports vary, so we do not repeat figures. Our guide to [Vinted relisting and automation rules](/guides/vinted-relisting-and-automation-rules) sets out what Vinted can do to an account, and [Vinted algorithm myths](/blog/vinted-algorithm-myths) tests the claim that automated relisting helps at all. If an account has already been restricted, [banned on Vinted: how to appeal](/blog/banned-on-vinted-how-to-appeal) covers the appeal route and your data rights.

Remember too that Vinted's rules say anyone selling to make a profit needs a Pro account. See [Vinted Pro and selling as a business](/guides/vinted-pro-and-selling-as-a-business).

**eBay.** Section 3 of eBay's [user agreement](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/user-agreement?id=4259) says you will not use any robot, spider, scraper or "other automated means to access our Services for any purpose, except with the prior express permission of eBay". So ask any app how it connects to eBay: through a sign-in and permission screen on eBay's own site, or by acting inside your logged-in browser. If it is the second, ask what permission from eBay it relies on.

Our view: the rules risk is highest where an app acts inside your Vinted account on a standard account. Copying a listing to eBay and ending it by hand when it sells is lower risk than letting software relist, send offers or favourite for you.

## How to choose

Ask yourself:

1. How many new listings do I add a month, and how many sites do I actually sell on? If it is a handful on two sites, copying by hand may take less time than you think.
2. Have I sold the same item twice this year? If not, automatic delisting may not be worth the jump to a higher plan.
3. Is Vinted one of my main sites? If so, check how each app handles a Vinted sale and read Vinted's terms yourself.
4. Am I on a standard Vinted account or Pro? The rules above apply either way, and the commercial selling rules only allow profit-making on Pro.
5. What will it cost me each month in pounds, including VAT and the exchange rate, and what does that come to against my monthly profit, not my sales?
6. Can I test it properly within the trial, on real stock, and cancel in time?

## Our verdict

If we had to choose one route for a typical UK reseller, we would choose **the free copy-and-paste route, using our [listing builder](/tools/listing-builder) and ending sold listings by hand**. By typical we mean someone listing a few dozen items a month on Vinted and eBay, with Vinted as one of their main sites.

Why:

- Vinted's terms ban external software tools unless Vinted has authorised them, and its official software route is for a limited set of allowlisted Vinted Pro businesses only. We found no Vinted UK page authorising any of these apps for standard accounts.
- eBay's user agreement bans automated access without eBay's prior express permission.
- The main thing you pay for is automatic delisting, and on Vinted it is the least certain part: List Perfectly says a Vinted sale is not detected, and Vendoo leaves Vinted off its sale detection list.
- Copying costs nothing, and ending a listing by hand when it sells is the lower-risk route set out in the rules section above.

If you do use an app, we would choose **FLUF Connect**, because it has the lowest published price here in pounds (£9 a month), says Vinted is supported on every plan, and includes automatic delisting on every plan with published check times. Before you connect a Vinted account, ask FLUF in writing how it connects to Vinted and eBay, and whether a Vinted sale is detected. Starter checks only every 90 minutes, so a double sale is still possible.

By situation:

- **If you cross-list mainly between eBay, Depop and Etsy, not Vinted:** Vendoo, because it has a 14-day free trial and sale detection with automatic delisting on every plan, covering eBay, Depop and Etsy.
- **If you want to pay per item in pounds:** Zipsale, from £0.18 an item plus VAT, with a free plan and no card needed. Check whether credits include automatic delisting first, as the page does not say.
- **If you list only a handful of items on two sites:** the listing builder alone, as above.

What we would avoid:

- **Any tool that relists, sends offers or favourites inside a Vinted account,** such as automation plans that do those jobs. That is the activity section 6 of Vinted's terms describes.
- **List Perfectly if Vinted is your main site,** because Vinted is a "Lite" marketplace there and a Vinted sale does not trigger delisting elsewhere.

## Common mistakes

- **Assuming "supports Vinted" means sales are detected there.** On at least two of these apps, a Vinted sale may not trigger delisting elsewhere.
- **Buying the cheapest plan and expecting automatic delisting.** On Crosslist, List Perfectly and SellerAider it is only on higher plans.
- **Forgetting the gap between checks.** An app that checks every 90 minutes can still let a second buyer pay.
- **Letting an app relist, favourite or send offers on Vinted.** That is the activity section 6 of Vinted's terms describes.
- **Comparing dollar prices with pound prices.** Convert, and add VAT where the page says it is extra.
- **Letting a free trial roll into a paid plan.** Put the end date in your calendar the day you start.
