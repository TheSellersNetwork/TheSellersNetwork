This is for resellers on eBay, Vinted, Amazon and elsewhere who want to know what a business current account would cost them. We checked 18 providers against their own fee pages and tariffs, plus the FSCS and the Bank of England's list of banks, in September 2026. By the end you will know what each charges for the things resellers do most, which are FSCS protected, and how to open or switch. Fees change often, so check each provider's current tariff (linked below) before deciding. This is general information, not financial advice.

## The short version

- A limited company needs its own account. Sole traders are not legally required to have one, but several banks' personal account terms ban business use.
- Banks are covered by the FSCS up to £120,000. E-money accounts are safeguarded instead, which is not the same thing.
- Cash costs vary most: paying in £400 of car boot takings costs from £1 to £7.50 depending on the account.
- Some accounts charge for every incoming payment, which adds up when marketplaces pay you often.
- Free periods end. Price the account for month 13 or month 31, not month one.
- Halifax does not offer business accounts, and TSB was only accepting existing customers when we checked.

## Do you need a business account at all?

**Limited companies: yes.** A limited company is legally separate from the people who own it ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/limited-company-formation)), and there are rules on [taking money out of it](https://www.gov.uk/running-a-limited-company/taking-money-out-of-a-limited-company). Its money belongs in an account in its own name.

**Sole traders: not by law, but check your bank's terms.** HMRC says you might be able to use a personal or business account and to check with your bank ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/self-employed-records/what-records-to-keep)). Several banks say no:

- Lloyds' personal terms say its accounts "must not be used by a business" and it may close an account used that way ([Lloyds](https://www.lloydsbank.com/assets/media/pdfs/current-accounts/personal_banking_terms_and_conditions.pdf)).
- Starling's personal account cannot be used for any business purposes, including as a sole trader ([Starling](https://www.starlingbank.com/docs/legal/terms/starling-personal-account-schedule-and-rfc-may-2026.pdf)).
- TSB says using a personal account for business is not allowed ([TSB](https://www.tsb.co.uk/business/accounts.html)).

**Why separating helps anyway.** One selling account makes Self Assessment easier, and Making Tax Digital for Income Tax now applies to sole traders with qualifying income over £50,000, dropping to £30,000 from April 2027 and £20,000 from April 2028 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/find-out-if-and-when-you-need-to-use-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax)). See our [tax calculator](/tools/tax) and [bookkeeping and tax guide](/guides/bookkeeping-and-tax-for-resellers).

**Platform payouts.** Marketplaces pay out in batches, and Amazon holds funds until after delivery ([Amazon DD+7 explained](/blog/amazon-dd7-payouts-explained)). Every payout is an incoming payment.

## What matters most for resellers

1. **Cash deposits** from car boots or collections: cost per deposit and per £100 (see our [car boot guide](/guides/car-boot-sales-for-resellers)).
2. **Many small payments in**: per-item charges grow with you.
3. **FSCS or e-money**: know which before holding a large balance.
4. **Accounting links**: a bank feed saves hours at tax time.
5. **Fees as you grow**: allowances, turnover caps and free periods ending.

## FSCS protection and e-money: the difference

If a UK-authorised bank fails, the FSCS automatically compensates eligible depositors up to £120,000 (it rose from £85,000 on 1 December 2025). A sole trader's personal and business accounts at the same bank share one £120,000 limit; a limited company gets its own, separate from yours ([FSCS](https://www.fscs.org.uk/making-a-claim/claims-process/small-business/)). The limit is per banking licence, not per brand ([FSCS](https://www.fscs.org.uk/what-we-cover/banks-building-societies/)). Mettle, for example, is provided by National Westminster Bank plc.

The FSCS cannot protect money held with e-money institutions ([FSCS](https://www.fscs.org.uk/news/protection/e-money-and-fscs-protection/)). They must safeguard it instead, in a separate account or with insurance. The FCA says that if such a firm fails you should get most of your money back, but it may take time and may not be the full amount ([FCA](https://www.fca.org.uk/consumers/using-payment-service-providers)).

Some apps are not banks but place your account with one: Tide with ClearBank and Countingup with Griffin Bank, both on the Bank of England's [list of banks](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/authorisations/which-firms-does-the-pra-regulate). Tide, Mettle and Countingup each say some customers hold e-money accounts with PrePay Technologies instead, so check your own terms or the [FSCS checker](https://www.fscs.org.uk/check/check-your-money-is-protected/).

## Each account: the good and the bad

### Zempler Bank (formerly Cashplus)

Cashplus Bank renamed itself Zempler Bank ([Zempler](https://www.zemplerbank.com/new-name/)). [Fees](https://www.zemplerbank.com/business/current-account/pricing/).

- **Good:** No monthly fee on Business Go. Receiving payments free and unlimited. Invoicing, receipt capture, pots and accounting integration on all plans. Phone support.
- **Bad:** Go allows 3 free payments out a month, then 35p. Cash £4 minimum. Go charges 2.99% on card spending abroad. Inbound international payments up to £22.

### Santander

[Business Current Account Classic](https://www.santander.co.uk/business/current-accounts/business-current-account-classic).

- **Good:** 12 months free for start-ups and switchers. Electronic payments in and Faster Payments out free ([general terms](https://assets.santandermedia.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:f283bd9f-9a4d-48d0-86bd-57412b1a4d6f/original/as/Business_Banking_General_Terms_and_Conditions_ANB90361APR26PUBCDT.pdf)). £1,000 of cash a month free at Santander machines. Branches, phone and chat.
- **Bad:** £9.99 a month afterwards. Counter and Post Office cash £1.25 per £100. No interest. Maximum two directors, owners or partners ([key facts](https://assets.santandermedia.com/adobe/assets/urn:aaid:aem:7dda1f16-7693-41a6-a66d-7e1061b53a5a/original/as/Business_Current_Account_Classic_Key_Facts_Document_ANBMC0867APR26PUBCDT.pdf)).

### Halifax

Halifax says it does not provide business banking and points people to Lloyds and Bank of Scotland ([Halifax](https://www.halifax.co.uk/business-banking.html)).

### Lloyds

[Business Account](https://www.lloydsbank.com/business/business-accounts.html), [charges](https://www.lloydsbank.com/assets/assets-business-banking/pdfs/lb-account-charges-and-processing-times.pdf).

- **Good:** 12 months free if new to Lloyds. Electronic payments in and out included. No per-credit fee at Post Office counters. Free Making Tax Digital software for sole traders.
- **Bad:** £10 a month after year one. Cash £1.60 per £100 at a counter or Post Office (£0.85 at a deposit machine). No credit interest. Turnover cap £3 million.

### NatWest

[Business account](https://www.natwest.com/business/bank-accounts/business-bank-account.html), [charges leaflet](https://www.natwest.com/content/dam/natwest/business/current-accounts/documents/natwest-business-account-charges.pdf).

- **Good:** No monthly charge. Two years' free banking for start-ups (turnover up to £1 million) and switchers (up to £2 million). FreeAgent free while you keep the account. Branches and Post Office.
- **Bad:** Afterwards every electronic payment in or out costs 35p. Free banking on new accounts ends if cash deposits pass £200,000 in 12 months.

Say 40 payouts arrive and you make 20 payments in a month: 60 × 35p is £21. Our view: per-item pricing suits low-volume sellers more than daily payouts.

### Royal Bank of Scotland

Same tariff as NatWest. [Business account](https://www.rbs.co.uk/business/bank-accounts/business-bank-account.html), [charges leaflet](https://www.rbs.co.uk/content/dam/rbs_co_uk/Business_and_Content/PDFs/royal-bank-business-account-charges.pdf).

- **Good:** No monthly charge, two years' free banking on NatWest's terms, FreeAgent free.
- **Bad:** 35p per electronic payment afterwards. The switch offer excludes moves from NatWest or Ulster Bank.

### Barclays

[Business Current Account](https://www.barclays.co.uk/business-banking/accounts/business-current-account/), [tariff](https://www.barclays.co.uk/content/dam/documents/business/Accounts/B0213_BAR0324764636-001_PN8206607_9918661_UK_0324_WCAG.pdf).

- **Good:** 12 months free. Electronic and debit card payments free. Post Office cash 60p per £100 in the tariff. Sage free for 12 months, then 50% off.
- **Bad:** £8.50 a month afterwards, and existing Barclays business customers do not get the offer. Counter cash £1.20 per £100. 2.75% on debit card use abroad.

### HSBC

[Small Business Banking Account](https://www.business.hsbc.uk/en-gb/products/small-business-bank-account).

- **Good:** No monthly fee. Standard UK digital payments free. Eligible non-VAT-registered sole traders get My Business Finances (Sage) free for 12 months, then £5.99 + VAT a month. Phone support six days a week, and branches.
- **Bad:** The dearest cash in our example: £1.50 per credit plus 1.5% at a branch or Post Office. Charges change on 14 December 2026, so recheck then.

### Starling

[Limited company](https://www.starlingbank.com/business-account/) and [sole trader](https://www.starlingbank.com/business-account/sole-trader/) accounts, [fees schedule](https://www.starlingbank.com/docs/legal/account/starling-business-soletrader-account-schedule-and-rfc-apr-2026.pdf).

- **Good:** No monthly fee or UK payment fees. Xero, QuickBooks and FreeAgent links. Spaces for tax. 24/7 chat and phone.
- **Bad:** Post Office cash 0.7%, £3 minimum, with daily and yearly limits. No interest on the account. Company owners must all be UK-resident individuals.

### Tide

Tide is not a bank; ClearBank provides its current accounts ([pricing](https://www.tide.co/pricing/)). [Costs](https://www.tide.co/support/joining/what-is-tide/how-much-does-tide-cost/).

- **Good:** Free plan. Sole traders and companies open in the app. Xero Connector on paid plans. Interest on savings, by plan. 24/7 chat.
- **Bad:** Transfers in or out cost 20p beyond your allowance (5 a month on Free), and inbound payments arrive minus the fee. PayPoint cash 3%. Free plan charges 2.75% on foreign currency card payments.

### Monzo Business

[Plans](https://monzo.com/business-banking/plans-pricing), [fee information](https://monzo.com/legal/files/business-account-fee-information/fee-information-1-16.pdf).

- **Good:** Lite is free. UK payments in and out free. Cash £1 per deposit at PayPoint or Post Office. Pro (£9, first month free) adds invoices, tax pots and accounting links. 24/7 support.
- **Bad:** Foreign currency received costs 1% to convert. Invoicing and tax pots need Pro. Sole traders and company directors only.

### Mettle

A trading name of National Westminster Bank plc ([Mettle](https://www.mettle.co.uk/business-bank-account/)). [Fees](https://www.mettle.co.uk/fee-information-document/).

- **Good:** No monthly fee. FreeAgent included if you make one transaction a month. Tax pots, invoicing, interest on a savings pot.
- **Bad:** Cash £2.75 per deposit. Inbound international payments over £10 cost £4 plus 1.5%. Companies limited to two owners, one with access. Support is not 24/7 ([contact](https://www.mettle.co.uk/contact-us/)). Not on the Current Account Switch Service.

### Revolut Business

Revolut's website refused our connection every time, so we could not check its fees or terms. Revolut Bank UK Ltd is on the Bank of England's [list of banks](https://www.bankofengland.co.uk/prudential-regulation/authorisations/which-firms-does-the-pra-regulate) as at 1 September 2026, and the switch service lists Revolut for personal accounts only. Before opening, check which Revolut company would hold your account and whether it is a bank or an e-money institution.

### ANNA

ANNA's account is e-money from PayrNet Limited, safeguarded and not FSCS protected ([pricing](https://anna.money/pricing/)).

- **Good:** Free plan with unlimited outgoing transfers. Invoicing, receipt capture, pots, Xero sync. No monthly fee in months you do not use it. 24/7 email support.
- **Bad:** E-money: not covered by the FSCS. Free plan charges 0.95% on incoming payments over £1,000 a month, so £3,000 in costs £19. Paid plans are plus VAT. Cash costs not published.

### Countingup

Accounts provided by Griffin Bank Ltd ([pricing](https://countingup.com/pricing/)).

- **Good:** Accounting, invoicing, receipts and tax pots included. 3-month free trial. Interest on tax pots. Support seven days a week.
- **Bad:** Price rises with deposits: £5 up to £750, £12 up to £7,500, £20 above. 3 free transfers in or out a month, then 27p. 3% on foreign card use. Cash costs unverified.

### TSB

[Business accounts](https://www.tsb.co.uk/business/accounts.html), [tariffs](https://www.tsb.co.uk/business/accounts/compare-business-tariffs.html).

- **Good:** 30 months free, then £5 a month with free electronic payments. Branches and Post Office.
- **Bad:** Only existing TSB business, mortgage or active personal account customers could apply when we checked. After 30 months cheques cost 70p each.

### Metro Bank

[Business Bank Account summary](https://www.metrobankonline.co.uk/globalassets/documents/customer_documents/business-and-commercial/businessbankaccount_importantinformationsummary.pdf) (July 2026).

- **Good:** 12 months free for new customers, 31 months for full switchers. Keep £6,000 in all month and the fee is waived with 30 free transactions. Local Business Managers in store.
- **Bad:** Otherwise everyday transactions, including payments in, cost 30p each. Cash in store 1%. Turnover cap £2 million. No credit interest.

### Co-operative Bank

[Business Bank Account](https://www.co-operativebank.co.uk/business/products/current-accounts/business-current-account/).

- **Good:** No monthly fee. Automated payments, card purchases and cash machine withdrawals free. Sole traders, partnerships, LLPs and companies accepted.
- **Bad:** Cash £1.50 per £100. The bank proposes transferring to Coventry Building Society on 1 January 2027, subject to court approval, after which the Bank, smile and the Society would share one £120,000 FSCS limit.

## Switching and opening, step by step

1. **Pick the right name**: yours as a sole trader, or the company's.
2. **Gather documents**: photo ID, proof of address, proof of what the business does, expected turnover and, for a company, its Companies House number ([Santander](https://www.santander.co.uk/business/current-accounts/business-current-account-classic), [Barclays](https://www.barclays.co.uk/business-banking/accounts/business-current-account/)). Apps usually want a selfie ([Tide](https://www.tide.co/business-current-account/)).
3. **Apply** online or in the app.
4. **Update payout details** on every platform yourself.
5. **Switch everything, if you want to.** The [Current Account Switch Service](https://www.currentaccountswitch.co.uk/common-questions-business/) moves payments and closes the old account in seven working days, for businesses with turnover up to £6.5 million and fewer than 50 staff. Business participants include Barclays, Co-operative Bank, HSBC UK, Lloyds, Metro Bank, Monzo, NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland, Santander, Starling, Tide, TSB and Zempler Bank ([CASS list](https://www.currentaccountswitch.co.uk/banks-building-societies/)). Mettle, ANNA and Countingup are not listed. Do not set up new payments on the old account in the final seven working days ([CASS](https://www.currentaccountswitch.co.uk/the-switching-process-business/)).

## Common mistakes

- **Using a personal account against its terms.** It can be closed with your payouts pointing at it.
- **Assuming every app is a bank.** Check the small print and the [FCA register](https://register.fca.org.uk/s/).
- **Forgetting the sole trader FSCS limit.** Personal and business money at one bank share £120,000.
- **Comparing month-one prices.** Price it after the free period, at your real volumes.
- **Small cash deposits on a minimum-fee account.** A £4 minimum on £50 is 8%.
- **Ignoring card fees.** Card acceptance is priced separately; see our [card payments comparison](/guides/taking-card-payments-compared).
