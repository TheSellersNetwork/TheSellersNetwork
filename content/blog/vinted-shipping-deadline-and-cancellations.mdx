This is for Vinted sellers who have made a sale and cannot get to the drop-off point in time, or who want to know the rules before it happens. By the end you will know the deadline for sending an order, what Vinted does when you miss it, how to ask the buyer for more time, when reserving an item is the better tool, and what cancelling an order costs you in feedback. Everything here comes from Vinted's own Help Centre as published in September 2026, and Vinted changes its pages often, so check the linked page before you rely on a detail. It is general information, not legal advice.

## The short version

- You have **5 business days** from the purchase to send a Vinted order ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/482-shipping-an-item)).
- If Vinted sees no tracking update and no confirmation that you sent it within those 5 business days, the order is cancelled the next business day and the buyer is refunded ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/740-i-cant-send-the-item-on-time)).
- A late shipment or a manual cancellation gives you automatic negative feedback ([Vinted feedback help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/14-feedback-policy)).
- You can ask the buyer for 3 or 5 extra business days. The buyer can accept or decline.
- Reserving holds an item for one buyer for 5 days before they pay. It is not a sale and does not start the shipping clock.
- If you know you cannot send orders for a while, turn on holiday mode before sales come in, not after.

## The deadline: 5 business days

Vinted's [shipping page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/482-shipping-an-item) says to send the item within 5 business days of the purchase. The in-app message you see after a sale describes the same window as 5 business days, Monday to Friday. Weekends do not count. Vinted's pages we read do not say how bank holidays are treated, so if a bank holiday falls inside your window, do not count on it as extra time.

The clock starts at the purchase, not when you first read the message. If a buyer pays on Friday evening, your window runs through the following week, but it has already started.

Two details catch people out:

- **You must use the label for that order.** After the sale, the carrier, label type, delivery address and parcel size cannot be changed. The only fix is to cancel and let the buyer order again ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/482-shipping-an-item)).
- **Using a different shipping method from the one the buyer chose** is itself a reason for automatic cancellation ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/674)).

For choosing carriers and parcel sizes before the sale, see our guide to [Vinted postage options](/guides/vinted-postage-options).

## What happens if you miss it

Vinted's help page on [orders that were cancelled](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/674) says an order is cancelled automatically if the seller:

- does not download the shipping label in time (5 business days);
- does not send the item within 5 business days;
- sends it with a different shipping method from the one the buyer chose;
- does not confirm shipment when using custom shipping.

The [I can't send the item on time](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/740-i-cant-send-the-item-on-time) page is more exact: if Vinted gets no tracking update and no confirmation that the order has been sent within 5 business days of the purchase, the order is cancelled the next business day. The buyer is refunded, and you can relist the item.

Vinted also says the automatic cancellation "may not happen immediately" after the deadline. That gap is not extra time. If you post the parcel after the deadline and the cancellation then goes through, you have a parcel on its way to someone who has had their money back.

### If you sent it but tracking has not updated

Vinted says tracking can take up to 48 hours to update. If you have sent the parcel and the tracking has not caught up, press **I confirm I've sent the order** in the conversation so the order is not cancelled automatically ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/740-i-cant-send-the-item-on-time)). Keep your drop-off receipt or the confirmation from the locker or shop. Only press the button when the parcel really has gone.

### If the order was cancelled but the parcel is already on its way

Vinted's page on [cancelled orders you have already sent](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/209) sets out two routes:

1. **The buyer has not received it yet.** Ask them not to collect it from the pick-up point, or to refuse home delivery. It comes back to you. Then press **Re-upload** in the conversation, ask the buyer to press **Buy now** and pay again, and send it with the new label.
2. **The buyer already has it.** Contact them and try to agree a solution. Vinted's page gives no other route.

Either way, the cost of the mistake falls on you, so the deadline is worth treating as firm.

## What it does to your feedback

Vinted's [feedback page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/14-feedback-policy) says:

- if the item is not shipped on time, the seller gets automatic negative feedback;
- if the seller or the buyer cancels the order manually, the one who cancels gets automatic negative feedback.

Vinted says it can only edit or remove automatic feedback, or feedback that breaks its Community Standards or the law. The [cancelling an order](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/58) page adds a practical route: tell the other member before you cancel and reach an agreement, and they can then edit or delete the feedback.

In our view, a short, polite message before any cancellation is the single most useful habit here. It turns a black mark into a conversation.

## Asking for more time

You can ask the buyer to extend the deadline from the conversation screen by choosing **Extend shipping deadline**. Vinted offers 3 or 5 extra business days, and you can add a reason ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/740-i-cant-send-the-item-on-time)). The buyer can accept or decline ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/482-shipping-an-item)).

The help page does not say whether you can ask more than once, so assume you get one go and pick the length you actually need.

A request is more likely to be accepted if it is specific. "I'm away until Tuesday and will post it at the shop on Wednesday morning" gives the buyer a date. "Sorry, busy week" does not.

If the buyer declines or does not answer, the original deadline stands.

## Reserving: when a buyer asks you to hold something

Buyers often ask a seller to "reserve" an item until payday. Vinted has a proper feature for this ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/59-reserving-an-item)):

- You can reserve from the conversation (the ⓘ symbol, then **Reserve**) or from the listing (**Mark as reserved**, then enter the member's name).
- The reservation lasts 5 days. You can unreserve at any time during that period.
- While reserved, the item is hidden from everyone else on the feed, in the catalogue and in your wardrobe. Only that member can see and buy it.
- You do not have to agree. Vinted's page says sellers are not required to accommodate reservation requests.

Reserving does not start the shipping clock, because there is no purchase yet. The 5 business days begin only when the buyer pays.

Some practical points, which are our opinion rather than Vinted's rules:

- **Reserve for a named date, not "until I can".** Tell the buyer you will unreserve after the 5 days if they have not bought.
- **Do not take payment outside Vinted to "secure" an item.** Vinted says that if payment is made without the **Buy now** button, the buyer is not covered and Vinted will be unable to offer its support ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/82-how-to-shop-pay-safely)). That leaves you both on your own if anything goes wrong.
- **Watch for repeat reservers.** If the same account reserves and never buys, you have lost days of visibility for nothing.

## Cancelling an order yourself

An order can only be cancelled, by either side, if the item has not been sent yet ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/58)). To cancel:

1. Open the conversation with the other member.
2. Press the ⓘ symbol in the top right.
3. Choose **Cancel order** and pick a reason, or choose **Other** and explain.

After a cancellation you can press **Re-upload item** to put it back on sale. Vinted also says it may cancel an order itself in some cases.

Good reasons to cancel yourself are real ones: the item is damaged, you cannot find it, or you realise it is not as described. If you find a flaw you did not list, cancelling with an explanation is usually better than sending it and facing a [not as described claim](/guides/vinted-fake-or-not-as-described-claims).

## When you cannot send orders for a while

If you are going away, ill or moving house, stop new sales before they happen. [Holiday mode](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/33-using-holiday-mode) hides all your listings for up to 90 days, and you can switch it on or off at any time. It does not extend any Bumps or Showcase you have paid for.

Vinted's page on missed deadlines also points to reserving and cancelling as alternatives. The order is:

| Situation | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| You will be away and have no open orders | Turn on holiday mode before you go |
| A sale has come in and you need a few more days | Ask the buyer to extend by 3 or 5 business days |
| The buyer declines, or you cannot send it at all | Message them, agree, then cancel |
| A buyer wants to pay later | Reserve for up to 5 days, or say no |
| You have sent it but tracking is not moving | Press I confirm I've sent the order |

## If you are selling regularly

If orders are arriving often enough that the 5 business days feels tight, it may be a sign you are selling at a scale that standard accounts are not for. Vinted says standard accounts are for selling your own things, and resellers need Vinted Pro. See [Vinted Pro and selling as a business](/guides/vinted-pro-and-selling-as-a-business). Repeated late shipments and cancellations also add up on your profile, and a restricted account is harder to fix than a late parcel; see [banned on Vinted: how to appeal](/blog/banned-on-vinted-how-to-appeal).

## What goes wrong

- **Counting from when you saw the sale.** The clock runs from the purchase.
- **Treating the delay before auto-cancellation as spare time.** A parcel sent after the deadline can arrive to a refunded buyer.
- **Pressing "I've sent it" before you have.** It is a statement to Vinted, and the tracking will show the truth.
- **Cancelling without a message.** You pick up automatic negative feedback that the buyer could have removed.
- **Using a different carrier or label.** It is a reason for automatic cancellation, and you cannot change the label after the sale.
- **Taking a deposit outside Vinted for a reservation.** Vinted says it cannot support payments made outside its system.
