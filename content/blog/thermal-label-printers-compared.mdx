This is for resellers who print enough postage labels that cutting A4 sheets and taping them on has started to cost real time, and who want to compare thermal label printers before buying one. It covers seven printers sellers commonly name: Zebra ZD421d, Brother QL-1110NWBc, DYMO LabelWriter 5XL, Munbyn RW411B, Rollo Wireless, Phomemo PM-241-BT and iDPRT SP410. Prices come only from each maker's own store or product page, and label compatibility comes from the help pages of Royal Mail Click & Drop, eBay, Vinted and Amazon. There are no affiliate links, and naming a printer is not a recommendation. This is general information, not financial advice.

## The short version

- A thermal printer uses heat-sensitive labels, so there is no ink or toner. The common shipping size is 4 x 6 inches (about 102mm x 152mm); Royal Mail calls it 6 x 4.
- Royal Mail Click & Drop can produce 6 x 4 and 4 x 3 labels for a thermal or label printer, and says it is compatible with Zebra and standard printers.
- Amazon's Print Connect tool prints Buy Shipping labels to thermal printers and uses native ZPL for Zebra. It runs on Windows only.
- eBay and Vinted both let you skip printing entirely at many drop-off points by showing a QR code.
- Of the makers here, only Brother and Munbyn showed a UK price in pounds on their own store when we checked: £266.32 and £89.99 (a sale price). Rollo showed US dollars and Phomemo euros. Zebra, DYMO UK and iDPRT did not show a price.
- The DYMO LabelWriter 5XL only works with DYMO-branded labels. Rollo says its printer takes any direct thermal label.

## What matters most for resellers

1. **4 x 6 inch support.** Most carrier and marketplace labels are built for it.
2. **How you print.** USB from a laptop, or Bluetooth or Wi-Fi from a phone.
3. **Label cost per 1,000.** Over a year this can cost more than the printer.
4. **Locked-in labels.** Whether the printer only accepts the maker's own rolls.
5. **Your software.** Click & Drop, eBay, Vinted and Amazon each produce labels differently.
6. **Price in pounds, including VAT, from a UK source.**

## Zebra ZD421d

Zebra's [ZD400 series specification sheet](https://www.zebra.com/us/en/products/spec-sheets/printers/desktop/zd400-series.html) lists a maximum print width of 104mm at 203 dpi and media from 15mm to 108mm wide. USB is standard. Ethernet and a combined Wi-Fi and Bluetooth option can be added.

**Good**

- Royal Mail's Click & Drop help names Zebra as compatible ([how to print Click & Drop labels](https://help.parcel.royalmail.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000811694-How-to-print-Click-Drop-labels)).
- Amazon Print Connect prints Buy Shipping labels to Zebra printers in native ZPL ([Amazon Print Connect](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/external/Q8WSSALNXZJEW3S)).
- Optional 300 dpi and field-installable connectivity, so you can add Wi-Fi later.

**Bad**

- Zebra's specification sheet gives no price, so you have to get quotes from resellers.
- Wi-Fi and Bluetooth are options, not standard.

## Brother QL-1110NWBc

Brother's [UK store](https://store.brother.co.uk/devices/label-printer/ql/ql1110nwbc) listed the QL-1110NWBc at £266.32 including VAT (£221.93 before VAT). It has a true 4-inch (101.6mm) print width and prints labels up to 103.6mm wide. Brother's [DK-11241 roll](https://store.brother.co.uk/catalogs/brotheruk/supplies/label-printers/labels/dk/dk11241) is 102mm x 152mm with 200 labels a roll.

**Good**

- The widest connection list here: USB, Ethernet, Wi-Fi and Bluetooth, with AirPrint and Apple MFi support, from a PC, Mac, phone or tablet.
- Brother says its "PDF Crop Print" driver can pick individual FBA labels out of an Amazon PDF and print each one.
- A built-in cutter for continuous rolls.

**Bad**

- The highest UK price among the printers with a published pound price.
- The DK-11241 roll page did not show a price when we checked, so we cannot give a cost per 1,000.
- 200 labels a roll means more roll changes than larger rolls or fan-fold stacks.

## DYMO LabelWriter 5XL

DYMO's [UK page for the 5XL](https://www.dymo.co.uk/label-makers-printers/labelwriter-label-printers/dymo-labelwriter-5xl-label-printer/SP_1373970.html) says it prints 4 x 6 shipping labels over USB or a LAN connection, with Automatic Label Recognition that shows the label size and how many are left. Its [XL shipping labels](https://www.dymo.co.uk/labels-tapes/labelwriter-labels/dymo-labelwriterxl--shipping-labels/SP_1364811.html) are 104mm x 159mm, 220 to a roll.

**Good**

- DYMO lists Royal Mail integrations, Amazon and eBay among the platforms it works with.
- Shows how many labels remain on the roll.
- USB and network connection for more than one user.

**Bad**

- DYMO says the 5XL works only with DYMO-branded labels made after April 2020.
- No price for the printer or labels on DYMO's UK pages when we checked.
- No Wi-Fi or Bluetooth listed, and the DYMO Connect software is for Windows and Mac.
- The older LabelWriter 4XL is no longer listed on DYMO UK's [LabelWriter range page](https://www.dymo.co.uk/label-makers-printers/labelwriter-label-printers/), which shows the 5XL, 550 and 550 Turbo.

## Munbyn RW411B

Munbyn's [UK store](https://uk.munbyn.com/products/bluetooth-thermal-label-printer-rw411b) listed the RW411B at £89.99, down from £149.99, with kits including labels at £119.99 and £109.99. The printer-only option showed as sold out when we checked. It connects by Bluetooth or USB at 203 dpi, and has a built-in tray holding 350 labels.

**Good**

- The lowest UK pound price here, and it ships from a UK warehouse according to the page.
- Prints from a phone, tablet or PC.
- Munbyn publishes its own label prices: a 500-label fan-fold stack of 4 x 6 labels was £19.99 (down from £35.99), which works out at about £40 per 1,000 ([Munbyn 4 x 6 fan-fold labels](https://uk.munbyn.com/products/4x6-fanfold-shipping-labels)).

**Bad**

- Both the printer and label prices were sale prices. The usual prices on the same pages were £149.99 and £35.99.
- The printer-only option was out of stock when we checked.

## Rollo Wireless

Rollo's [product page](https://www.rollo.com/product/rollo-wireless-printer/) lists the Wireless printer at US$279.99 with 500 labels included. It prints over Wi-Fi with AirPrint, and over USB with the included cable.

**Good**

- Wi-Fi and AirPrint are built in, so any phone, tablet or computer on your network can print.
- Rollo says it takes any direct thermal label and detects the size automatically.

**Bad**

- No UK price on Rollo's own site, only US dollars.
- Rollo's own shipping software is for sending from the US and Canada ([Rollo Ship](https://www.rollo.com/intro-ship/)), so UK sellers would print from Click & Drop, eBay or Amazon instead.

## Phomemo PM-241-BT

Phomemo's [EU store](https://eu.phomemo.com/en/products/phomemo-pm241-bt-bluetooth-shipping-label-printer-copy-1) listed the PM-241-BT at €129.59, with a UK version among the options. It connects by Bluetooth, using Phomemo's Labelife app on iOS and Android, and by USB to Windows, Mac, Chrome OS and Linux. It takes labels from 25.4mm to 117mm wide at 203 dpi.

**Good**

- Phone printing by Bluetooth and computer printing by USB.
- Wide label range, from small product labels to 4 x 6 shipping labels.

**Bad**

- No price in pounds on the store we checked; the euro price moves with the exchange rate.
- Phone printing goes through Phomemo's own app, and Chrome OS needs a browser extension.

## iDPRT SP410 and SP410BT

iDPRT's [SP410 page](https://www.idprt.com/Products/4x6-Shipping-Label-Printer.html) lists a 108mm maximum print width, labels from 50mm to 118mm wide and 203 dpi, with support for Windows, macOS, Linux and Chrome. The [SP410BT](https://www.idprt.com/Products/Bluetooth-Shipping-Label-Printer.html) adds Bluetooth for phones.

**Good**

- A USB-only model and a Bluetooth model, so you only pay for wireless if you need it.
- Wide operating system support listed.

**Bad**

- iDPRT's own site does not show a price or a UK store.
- No label prices published on the pages we checked.

## What each platform says about printing its labels

**Royal Mail Click & Drop.** Click & Drop offers A4, 6 x 4 and 4 x 3 label formats. The 6 x 4 and 4 x 3 formats "can be printed on a thermal or label printer", and despatch notes can also print at 6 x 4 ([choosing your label format](https://help.parcel.royalmail.com/hc/en-gb/articles/115004866953-Choosing-your-label-format)). Royal Mail says it cannot give technical support for printers, and that resizing labels or using custom contrast settings may stop barcodes scanning ([how to print](https://help.parcel.royalmail.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000811694-How-to-print-Click-Drop-labels)).

**eBay.** eBay's [label help](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/labels-packaging-tips/buying-printing-postage-labels?id=4157) says you can print from a laptop or desktop, on an ordinary printer, and fit labels on a single sheet of A4. It does not publish a thermal label size. For many services you also get a QR code, so the drop-off point prints the label for you. Simple Delivery gives Royal Mail and Evri a label and QR code, and InPost a QR code only ([Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575)).

**Vinted.** Vinted offers a printable label or a digital QR code, depending on the carrier. For InPost, DPD and Royal Mail, staff at the drop-off point print the label from the code ([InPost](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953), [DPD](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1101), [Royal Mail](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528)). Vinted's help does not publish a label size, so check the downloaded label before setting a printer to it.

**Amazon.** Amazon's [Print Connect](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/external/Q8WSSALNXZJEW3S) prints Buy Shipping labels directly to thermal and laser printers, with native ZPL for Zebra. It is Windows only, does not support one-click bulk Buy Shipping (Amazon points to QZ Tray for that), and does not support the EPL LP-2844 printer.

Our view: if most of your sales are on Vinted or eBay Simple Delivery, the QR code route may mean you rarely need a printer at all. A thermal printer earns its place when you buy your own labels in volume, through Click & Drop, eBay's label service or Amazon Buy Shipping.

## How to choose

Ask yourself:

1. Where do my labels come from? Click & Drop, Amazon Buy Shipping and eBay labels all print at 4 x 6; Vinted and Simple Delivery often need no printer.
2. Do I print from a phone or a computer? Bluetooth and Wi-Fi models suit phone printing; USB-only models need a computer.
3. What will labels cost me each year? Multiply your monthly parcels by 12 and price labels per 1,000.
4. Am I locked into one maker's labels? DYMO says the 5XL is.
5. Is there a UK price in pounds and a UK returns route?
6. Do I use Windows? Amazon Print Connect needs it.

## Common mistakes

- **Scaling a label to fit.** Royal Mail warns that resized labels may not scan.
- **Buying the wrong label type.** These printers need direct thermal labels; an ordinary sticky label will not print.
- **Comparing a sale price with a full price.** Munbyn's printer and labels were both on sale when we checked.
- **Forgetting the label cost.** At a few hundred parcels a month, labels matter more than the printer.
- **Printing when a QR code would do.** Our [Vinted postage guide](/guides/vinted-postage-options) and [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) cover which services offer print-free drop-off, and our [packaging guide](/guides/packaging-cheaply) covers the rest of the packing bench.
