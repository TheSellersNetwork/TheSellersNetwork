This is for resellers who drop off several parcels a week and want to know which lockers and counters will take them, when, and what happens if something goes missing. It compares InPost lockers and shops, Evri ParcelShops and lockers, Royal Mail's parcel lockers, parcel postboxes, Customer Service Points and shops (including Post Office branches), DPD Pickup, Yodel (now part of InPost) and Amazon's lockers and counters. Everything here comes from the carriers' and marketplaces' own help pages. Network sizes are given only where the carrier publishes one. We have no arrangement with any of them. This is general information, not legal advice.

## The short version

- InPost lockers take parcels up to 41cm x 38cm x 64cm and 15kg, and most are open 24/7. InPost's home page says it has 16,000 lockers in the UK.
- Evri has more than 9,000 ParcelShops plus 24/7 lockers. The largest Evri locker is 62cm x 57cm x 39cm; ParcelShops take longer parcels, up to 15kg.
- Royal Mail parcel lockers have grey and red types with different maximum sizes. Parcel postboxes (over 1,400) take prepaid items up to 2kg.
- Vinted says not to drop Royal Mail Vinted parcels in a postbox. If you do, the parcel may not be delivered and no compensation is offered.
- Yodel has moved to InPost. Yodel Direct accounts still work, and parcels go through InPost lockers and shops.
- Amazon lockers and counters are for Amazon buyers collecting and returning. Amazon's help says items shipped by third-party sellers cannot be sent to a pickup location.
- For most Vinted and eBay sellers we would choose InPost lockers: 16,000 in the UK, most open 24/7, QR codes instead of printed labels, and up to 15kg. See our verdict below.

## What matters most for resellers

1. **Size and weight limits.** A locker is a fixed box. If it does not fit, you have to go elsewhere.
2. **Hours.** 24/7 lockers suit a day job; shops suit bulky parcels.
3. **Which labels it takes.** A Vinted or eBay label only works with the carrier it was bought for.
4. **Printing.** Whether you need a printed label or can show a QR code.
5. **Proof of posting.** A receipt or scan you can use if the buyer says nothing arrived.
6. **Collection times.** When a locker is emptied decides when your parcel really starts moving.

## InPost lockers and shops

InPost's [locker guide](https://inpost.co.uk/parcel-lockers) lists four compartment sizes: extra small 4cm x 23cm x 40cm (not at every locker), small 8cm x 38cm x 64cm, medium 19cm x 38cm x 64cm and large 41cm x 38cm x 64cm. Its [price list](https://inpost.co.uk/send/parcel-prices) sets a 15kg limit on each size, and its [shop help page](https://inpost.co.uk/help/whats-the-maximum-size-and-weight-i-can-drop-off-at-a-shop) gives the same limits for shops.

**Good**

- Most lockers are open 24/7, and you can use a QR code instead of a printed label.
- Vinted sends InPost QR codes or printable labels, and shop staff print and stick the label for you ([Vinted InPost help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953)).
- eBay's Simple Delivery uses InPost with a QR code only, up to 41cm x 38cm x 64cm and 15kg ([eBay Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575)).
- InPost says lockers are emptied at least once a day, and the app shows which sizes are free near you.

**Bad**

- One parcel per compartment. If the locker is full, you try later or go to another one.
- On Vinted, InPost covers mainland England, Scotland and Wales only for UK orders.
- For labels bought through Vinted or another platform, InPost's [lost or damaged help](https://inpost.co.uk/help/my-parcel-has-been-lost-or-damaged--what-happens) says to contact that platform, not InPost.
- eBay's label help says QR codes are not available for InPost labels bought outside Simple Delivery ([eBay label help](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/labels-packaging-tips/buying-printing-postage-labels?id=4157)).

## Evri ParcelShops and lockers

Evri's [ParcelShops page](https://www.evri.com/parcelshops) says its shops are in more than 9,000 local businesses, most with longer opening hours and open 7 days. Its [lockers page](https://www.evri.com/parcelshops/lockers) says lockers are open 24/7 and the largest measures 62cm x 57cm x 39cm. Its [size guide](https://www.evri.com/send/parcel-size-and-weight-guide) sets a 15kg limit and a 120cm maximum length for ParcelShops.

**Good**

- Most ParcelShops have a Print In ParcelShop device, and you can print at a locker for free.
- You can also book, pay and print Evri at a Post Office branch.
- Drop off at a locker by 12pm for same-day collection.
- Vinted (Evri Home delivery and Evri ParcelShop) and eBay Simple Delivery both use Evri. eBay lists up to 15kg at a ParcelShop and 66cm x 41cm x 38cm at a locker ([eBay Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575)).

**Bad**

- ParcelShops now give digital receipts only. Evri says that if you do not give an email address you will not get a receipt or tracking, and suggests photographing the label.
- eBay's locker size for Evri differs from the one on Evri's own page. Our view: work to the smaller of the two.
- Evri's own included cover is £20 on most parcels ([Evri prices](https://www.evri.com/our-services/our-prices)).

## Royal Mail lockers, postboxes, Customer Service Points and shops

Royal Mail has four kinds of drop-off for prepaid items.

- **Parcel lockers.** Grey lockers take up to 34cm x 61cm x 58cm and red lockers up to 42cm x 43cm x 66cm. Most are open 24/7, with restricted hours where the locker is inside a building. Collections are from 16:00 Monday to Friday and 10:30 on Saturday, with none on Sundays or bank holidays ([parcel lockers](https://www.royalmail.com/locations/parcel-locker)).
- **Parcel postboxes.** Over 1,400 in the UK, for prepaid parcels up to 45cm x 35cm x 16cm and 2kg. The same page gives 44cm in its FAQs ([parcel postboxes](https://www.royalmail.com/locations/parcel-postbox)).
- **Customer Service Points.** Counters, often in delivery offices, that take prepaid items within Royal Mail's standard size and weight limits and can print labels ([Customer Service Points](https://www.royalmail.com/locations/customer-service-point)).
- **Royal Mail Shops.** These include Collect+ stores and all Post Office branches. Royal Mail says most are open 7 days, early till late ([Royal Mail Shops](https://www.royalmail.com/locations/royal-mail-shop)).

**Good**

- The widest mix of 24/7 and staffed options, and free label printing at lockers.
- Lockers take Tracked, 1st Class, 2nd Class, Signed For, international and Tracked Returns items.
- Your email receipt from a locker counts as basic evidence for a claim, and the Royal Mail app gives proof of posting at parcel postboxes.
- Vinted's Royal Mail labels can go from a Collect+ shop, Post Office, locker or Customer Service Point ([Vinted Royal Mail help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528)).

**Bad**

- Lockers do not take items with stamps, or Special Delivery.
- Vinted says not to use Royal Mail postboxes for Vinted parcels. If you do, Royal Mail cannot register the collection, the parcel may not be delivered, and no compensation is offered.
- Restricted items cannot go through a Customer Service Point; they have to go to a Post Office.
- A parcel left in a locker after the day's collection does not move until the next one.

## DPD Pickup shops and lockers

DPD's [FAQs](https://send.dpd.co.uk/FAQ) say it has over 11,000 shops and lockers, including Post Office and Sainsbury's locations. Shops take up to 20kg, with the longest two sides adding up to no more than 120cm. The biggest locker compartment is 37cm wide, 68cm high and 55cm deep, with a 20kg limit.

**Good**

- Higher weight limits than InPost or Evri for drop-off.
- On Vinted, you show a QR code and shop staff print the label ([Vinted DPD help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1101)).
- £50 of cover on DPD's own services, with extended cover up to £1,000.

**Bad**

- Vinted's DPD service is parcel shop to parcel shop only, and is not available to or from the Channel Islands, Scottish Islands, Scottish Highlands, Northern Ireland or the Isle of Man.
- DPD says only the contract holder can make a claim. For a marketplace label, that is the marketplace or booking service, not you.
- DPD's FAQs do not give opening hours. Check each shop in DPD's Pickup finder.

## Yodel, now InPost

Yodel's own site now says "Yodel by InPost has moved to InPost", and that Yodel Direct accounts can still be used to send ([Yodel](https://www.yodel.co.uk/)). Sending is through InPost Direct, which uses InPost lockers and InPost shops. Its [large parcel page](https://www.inpostdirect.co.uk/services/large-parcel-postage) sets 10kg and 60cm x 50cm x 50cm for Shop to Door and Shop to Shop, and 15kg for Locker to Door.

**Good**

- Shop drop-off takes bigger boxes than an InPost locker, up to 60cm x 50cm x 50cm.
- A Print In Shop option if you have no printer, and £50 cover as standard ([InPost Direct](https://www.inpostdirect.co.uk/)).

**Bad**

- The shop network is described as "thousands" of shops; no exact figure is published.
- Yodel is not in Vinted's list of UK shipping providers ([shipping on Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/234-shipping-methods)).

## Amazon lockers and counters

Amazon's help pages describe lockers and counters as places for buyers to collect Amazon orders and return items. The [pickup eligibility page](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GEV9KXN2VUVBKSR7) lists Amazon Locker limits of 42cm x 35cm x 32cm and 4.5kg, and says only items dispatched by Amazon, including Fulfilled by Amazon, can go to a pickup location. Items sold and shipped by third-party sellers are not eligible.

**Good**

- For FBA sellers, buyers can collect from a locker within 3 days or a counter within 7 days of delivery ([pickup point delivery](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GZTXM2L3YLZFSQ6W)).
- Buyers returning at an Amazon Counter show a QR code; staff bag the item and label it for them ([counter returns](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GL7EJJE5PXVGT6XS)).

**Bad**

- If you fulfil orders yourself, your buyers cannot choose an Amazon locker for your parcel.
- We found no option on Amazon's help pages for sellers to send their own parcels from an Amazon locker or counter.

## Claims and proof: what each network gives you

| Network | What you get at drop-off | Who you claim through on a marketplace label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| InPost | Tracking in the app or marketplace | The marketplace (Vinted, for example) |
| Evri | Digital receipt, if you give an email | The marketplace, or Evri if you booked direct |
| Royal Mail | Email receipt at lockers; proof of posting in the app | The marketplace, or Royal Mail if you bought direct |
| DPD | Tracking | The contract holder |

Vinted says it compensates sellers for lost or damaged parcels sent with its prepaid label, up to £500, if the parcel was packed properly ([Vinted compensation](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)). eBay says Simple Delivery protects you from the point the carrier scans the parcel until it is marked delivered. Keep your receipt or a photo of the label until the order is complete. Our post on [sealed parcel videos and evidence](/blog/sealed-parcel-videos-and-evidence) covers what else to keep.

## How to choose

Ask yourself:

1. What sizes do I send most? If most of your parcels fit an InPost small or medium compartment, a nearby 24/7 locker may cover most of your week.
2. What does the buyer's label say? The carrier is fixed when the label is bought. Our [Vinted postage guide](/guides/vinted-postage-options) covers the Vinted options.
3. Do I have a printer? InPost, Evri, Royal Mail and DPD all offer a QR code route somewhere.
4. When is the locker emptied? A parcel dropped after the last collection waits a day.
5. How will I prove I posted it? Give an email at Evri ParcelShops, and keep Royal Mail locker receipts.
6. Will it fit? Use our [postage finder](/tools/postage) to check the Royal Mail and Evri size bands before you pack.

## Our verdict

If we had to choose one drop-off network for a typical UK reseller, we would choose **InPost lockers**. By typical we mean someone selling clothes and small items on Vinted and eBay, dropping off several parcels a week around a day job.

Why:

- InPost's home page says it has 16,000 lockers in the UK, and most are open 24/7, so you can drop off before or after work.
- You can use a QR code instead of a printed label, so you do not need a printer.
- Each compartment takes up to 15kg, and the large one is 41cm x 38cm x 64cm, which covers most clothing parcels.
- Both Vinted and eBay Simple Delivery use InPost, and InPost says lockers are emptied at least once a day.

By situation:

- **If you send bulky or long parcels:** an Evri ParcelShop, which takes up to 120cm long and 15kg, and most are open 7 days.
- **If your boxes are heavy:** DPD Pickup, which takes up to 20kg at shops and lockers.
- **If you buy your own Royal Mail labels and want proof of posting:** a Royal Mail parcel locker, because Royal Mail says the email receipt counts as basic evidence for a claim, and lockers print labels for free.
- **If you send Evri parcels and have no printer:** an Evri locker, where you can print for free, or a ParcelShop with a Print In ParcelShop device. At a ParcelShop, give an email address so you get a receipt.

What we would avoid:

- **A Royal Mail parcel postbox for a Vinted parcel.** Vinted says the parcel may not be delivered and no compensation is offered.
- **Planning to send your own parcels from Amazon lockers.** Amazon's help pages describe them for buyers collecting and returning, and we found no option for sellers to send from them.

## Common mistakes

- **Posting a Vinted Royal Mail parcel in a postbox.** Vinted says this can leave the parcel undelivered and uncompensated.
- **Skipping the email at an Evri ParcelShop.** No email, no receipt.
- **Buying the wrong InPost size.** You cannot change it at the locker; you cancel and buy again.
- **Assuming every locker is 24/7.** Lockers inside shops and stations follow the building's hours.
- **Using a different carrier from the one on the label.** Vinted cancels orders sent with the wrong label.
