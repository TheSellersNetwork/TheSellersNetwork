This is for UK resellers who post their own parcels on eBay, their own site or anywhere they buy the label themselves, and who want to know what each carrier charges for the parcels they actually send. It prices three typical parcels across Royal Mail, Evri, InPost, DPD, Yodel (now part of InPost), Parcelforce and the booking sites Parcel2Go and Packlink, using only each carrier's own price pages. It also compares the compensation each includes and where you can drop off. Royal Mail changes its parcel prices on 5 October 2026, so both sets of Royal Mail prices are shown. We have no arrangement with any carrier or booking site. This is general information, not financial advice.

## The short version

- For a 500g or 2kg parcel that fits Royal Mail's small parcel size, Tracked 48 is the cheapest Royal Mail service, at £3.65 now and £3.75 from 5 October 2026. Untracked 2nd Class is dearer, at £3.95 now and £4.40 from 5 October.
- InPost prices by box size, not weight. Its locker-to-locker prices start at £1.99, and a large box up to 15kg is £3.99 to a locker or £5.99 to a home address.
- Evri's drop-off-to-home prices run from £3.04 (up to 1kg, small) to £6.68 (5kg to 10kg). Sending to an Evri ParcelShop is £2.62 for anything up to 5kg.
- For the 10kg box, published prices range from £3.99 (InPost locker to locker, if it fits the large compartment) to £14.20 (Parcelforce express48). Royal Mail Tracked 48 is £7.35, rising to £7.55.
- Included cover ranges from £20 (Royal Mail 1st and 2nd Class, Evri) to £150 (Parcelforce). Tracked 48 and 24 include £75.
- Booking sites show "from" prices without VAT. Parcel2Go's headline figures ran from £1.99 to £8.94 plus VAT in our sample, and Packlink's from £2.25 to £4.60 plus VAT. Both change daily.
- For an everyday clothing parcel we would choose Royal Mail Tracked 48: tracked, £75 of cover, and cheaper than untracked 2nd Class. See our verdict below.

## What matters most for resellers

1. **Size first, then weight.** Royal Mail and InPost price mainly by size band; Evri and DPD mainly by weight. A few centimetres can move you up a band.
2. **Tracking.** A tracked service gives you proof of delivery, which matters if a buyer says an item never arrived. Our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) covers when marketplaces will back you.
3. **Included cover.** What the carrier pays if it loses or damages the parcel, before you buy extra.
4. **Drop-off.** How far you travel and whether you need a printer.
5. **VAT.** Some prices include VAT, some add it, and Royal Mail 1st and 2nd Class are exempt. It matters if you are VAT registered.
6. **Where you buy the label.** eBay and Vinted labels are priced by the platform, not by the carrier's own page.

## The three parcels

We priced three parcels that cover most of what resellers send. Sizes are the packed outer dimensions.

- **Parcel A: 500g of clothing** in a mailing bag, 30cm x 22cm x 5cm. Too thick for a Royal Mail large letter (the limit is 2.5cm), so it goes as a small parcel.
- **Parcel B: 2kg box**, 40cm x 30cm x 15cm. Fits Royal Mail's small parcel limit of 45cm x 35cm x 16cm and 2kg.
- **Parcel C: 10kg box**, 50cm x 40cm x 30cm. A Royal Mail medium parcel (up to 61cm x 46cm x 46cm and 20kg).

## Royal Mail

Royal Mail's [online price guide valid from 3 August 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-07/online-price-guide-august-2026-v1.pdf) is the one in force now. Its [online price guide for 5 October 2026](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf) has the new prices. Our post on the [October 2026 price rise](/blog/royal-mail-prices-october-2026) covers every change.

| Royal Mail online price | Parcels A and B (small parcel, up to 2kg) | Parcel C (medium parcel, up to 10kg) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2nd Class | £3.95 now, £4.40 from 5 October | £8.05 now, £9.00 from 5 October |
| 1st Class | £5.15 now, £5.70 from 5 October | £9.35 now, £10.40 from 5 October |
| Tracked 48 | £3.65 now, £3.75 from 5 October | £7.35 now, £7.55 from 5 October |
| Tracked 24 | £4.65 now, £4.80 from 5 October | £8.55 now, £8.80 from 5 October |

If Parcel A could be folded to 2.5cm or thinner, it would go as a large letter instead: £2.40 by 2nd Class up to 500g in both guides, or £2.85 by Tracked 48 up to 1kg, rising to £2.90. That is the biggest single saving in this post, and it comes from packing, not from choosing a carrier.

**Good**

- Tracked 48 is cheaper than untracked 2nd Class for a small parcel, and the gap grows from 30p to 65p on 5 October.
- Tracked 48 and 24 include compensation up to £75. 1st and 2nd Class include £20.
- The widest drop-off choice: Post Office branches, Royal Mail Shops, Customer Service Points, parcel lockers, and [parcel postboxes](https://www.royalmail.com/locations/parcel-postbox) for prepaid items up to 45cm x 35cm x 16cm and 2kg.
- 1st and 2nd Class prices are exempt from VAT; Tracked prices include VAT at 20%, which a VAT-registered seller may be able to reclaim.

**Bad**

- Untracked parcels rise the most in October: 45p on a 2nd Class small parcel and 95p on a 2nd Class medium parcel up to 10kg.
- Medium parcels cost a lot more than small ones. Parcel C by Tracked 48 is £7.35 against £3.65 for Parcel B.
- The guide adds 50p per item if Royal Mail collects from you.
- Counter prices are higher than online prices.

## Evri

Evri's [price page](https://www.evri.com/our-services/our-prices) lists drop-off, Next Day and collection prices by weight. Its [size guide](https://www.evri.com/send/parcel-size-and-weight-guide) defines a Small parcel as up to 45cm x 35cm x 16cm and 2kg, and sets a 15kg limit for everything.

**Good**

- Sending to an Evri ParcelShop is £2.62 for any parcel up to 5kg, and £5.87 for 5kg to 10kg.
- Parcels A and B both qualify as Small, at £3.04 and £3.52 to a home address.
- Tracking and a photo on delivery are included. Evri says its prices include tax.
- More than 9,000 ParcelShops, most with a Print In ParcelShop device, plus lockers ([ParcelShops page](https://www.evri.com/parcelshops)).

**Bad**

- Included cover is £20, and none on Postable (letterbox) parcels.
- A £2.75 location charge may apply for some postcodes.
- Next Day is not always available, and you must drop off by 12pm.
- ParcelShops now give digital receipts only. If you do not give an email address you get no receipt or tracking.

## InPost

InPost's [price list](https://inpost.co.uk/send/parcel-prices) prices by the locker compartment your parcel fits, with a 15kg limit on every size: small 8cm x 38cm x 64cm, medium 19cm x 38cm x 64cm, large 41cm x 38cm x 64cm.

**Good**

- Among the lowest published prices here for Parcels A and C: £1.99 and £3.99 to a locker or shop.
- Weight does not change the price up to 15kg, so heavy but compact items are cheap.
- No printer needed: you buy a QR code in the app and have 30 days to drop off.
- £50 of cover included, with an upgrade to full value up to £500 ([cover help page](https://inpost.co.uk/help/can-i-buy-additional-coverage-for-my-parcel)).

**Bad**

- Delivery to a home address costs more: £2.89, £3.99 and £5.99.
- Size is fixed when you buy. If your parcel does not fit, you cancel and buy a new label.
- InPost's own pages say delivery to a locker or shop should take up to 3 working days, and to a home up to 4.
- Nothing bigger than the large compartment.

## InPost Direct (formerly Yodel Direct)

Yodel's website now says it has moved to InPost, and that Yodel Direct accounts still work for sending ([Yodel home page](https://www.yodel.co.uk/)). The booking service is now called InPost Direct. Its [Shop to Door page](https://www.inpostdirect.co.uk/services/store-to-door) lists prices by weight before VAT.

**Good**

- Low published prices for heavier parcels: £2.16 plus VAT at 2kg and £4.99 plus VAT at 10kg.
- £50 cover as standard, with a signature option ([InPost Direct home page](https://www.inpostdirect.co.uk/)).
- Tools for bulk uploads and links to eBay, Amazon, Etsy and other selling accounts.

**Bad**

- Prices are shown before VAT. Add 20% to compare them with the carriers above.
- Shop to Door has a 10kg and 60cm x 50cm x 50cm limit ([large parcel page](https://www.inpostdirect.co.uk/services/large-parcel-postage)).
- Standard delivery is typically within 2 working days, counting drop-off day as day zero.

## DPD

DPD sells to individuals through [DPD Online](https://send.dpd.co.uk/products-and-services/). Its headline price is "from £2.99", but the weight and size behind that figure are not stated, so a 500g or 2kg price needs a quote. Its [collection page](https://send.dpd.co.uk/products-and-services/parcel-collection/) does publish collected prices: £6.56 for 5kg and £7.81 for 10kg, including VAT.

**Good**

- Parcel C collected from your door is £7.81 including VAT, next working day.
- £50 of cover on every service, with extended cover up to £1,000 ([DPD FAQs](https://send.dpd.co.uk/FAQ)).
- Over 11,000 shops and lockers, including Post Office and Sainsbury's locations.

**Bad**

- No published drop-off price by weight. You need to run a quote.
- DPD says extra charges may apply if a parcel is bigger or heavier than you declared.
- Shop drop-off is limited to 20kg, with the longest two sides adding up to no more than 120cm.

## Parcelforce

Parcelforce is Royal Mail's express arm. Its [prices page](https://www.parcelforce.com/sending-parcel/our-prices) lists express48 from £11.95 and express24 from £12.50, and its [online price guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-04/pfw-uk-and-international-online-prices-april-2026-v2.pdf) gives £11.95 for up to 5kg and £14.20 for up to 10kg by express48.

**Good**

- £150 of compensation included on every UK express service, the highest here.
- Every UK express service takes parcels up to 30kg.
- Timed next-day options if a buyer pays for speed.

**Bad**

- The dearest option for Parcels A and B by a wide margin.
- Prices shown are for drop-off at a Post Office; Parcel Collect costs 30p more.

## Parcel2Go and Packlink

These are booking sites. They sell labels for several carriers and show you a list of quotes. Prices depend on the day, the route and the parcel, so we recorded the headline "from" prices each site showed in September 2026 rather than a quote for each parcel.

On Parcel2Go's carrier pages the headline prices were: Evri drop-off from £1.99, Royal Mail from £2.37, InPost from £2.69, DPD from £5.19 and Parcelforce from £8.94, all before VAT ([Parcel2Go couriers](https://www.parcel2go.com/couriers)). Its [UK delivery page](https://www.parcel2go.com/parcel-delivery/uk) says almost all services include £20 protection, some Royal Mail services up to £50, with extra protection up to £10,000 available.

Packlink's [UK home page](https://www.packlink.com/en-GB/) listed national rates from £2.25 up to 1kg, £2.80 up to 2kg, £4.06 up to 5kg and £4.60 up to 10kg, all before VAT, with insurance offered as an option at checkout.

**Good**

- One quote shows several carriers side by side.
- For heavier parcels, the "from" prices are below the carriers' own public prices.
- eBay's own label service is [powered by Packlink](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/labels-packaging-tips/buying-printing-postage-labels?id=4157).

**Bad**

- Headline prices are before VAT and are the cheapest available, not the price for your parcel.
- Prices vary by day, so today's cheapest carrier may not be tomorrow's.
- If something goes wrong, you deal with the booking site as well as the carrier.
- Our view: check which carrier and service you are actually buying before you pay, because the included cover and drop-off options follow the carrier.

## How to choose

Ask yourself:

1. Can I pack this flatter? A large letter beats every parcel price in this post.
2. Does it fit a Royal Mail small parcel? If so, compare Tracked 48 with InPost and Evri, and remember the October rise.
3. Is it heavy but compact? Size-priced services such as InPost lockers may be much cheaper than weight-priced ones.
4. What is it worth? If it is more than the included cover, add cover or pick a carrier that includes more.
5. Where will I drop off, and when? A 24/7 locker two minutes away may be worth more than a few pence saved.
6. Does my marketplace require its own label? On Vinted, and for eBay private sellers using Simple Delivery, you use the platform's label and carrier. Our [Vinted postage guide](/guides/vinted-postage-options) covers Vinted's options.

## Our verdict

If we had to choose one service for a typical UK reseller's everyday clothing parcel, we would choose **Royal Mail Tracked 48**. By typical we mean someone who buys their own labels and posts a few 500g mailing bags a week to buyers' home addresses.

Why:

- It is the cheapest Royal Mail service for Parcel A, at £3.65 now and £3.75 from 5 October 2026, which is less than untracked 2nd Class at £3.95 and £4.40.
- Tracking is included, and that is your proof of delivery if a buyer says an item never arrived.
- It includes £75 of cover, against £50 at InPost and £20 at Evri.
- It has the widest drop-off choice, including parcel postboxes for prepaid items up to 2kg, and the October rise is only 10p.

By parcel type:

- **Parcel A, 500g of clothing:** Royal Mail Tracked 48, for the reasons above. If you can fold it to 2.5cm or thinner, send it as a large letter instead, at £2.85 by Tracked 48, rising to £2.90.
- **Parcel B, 2kg box:** Royal Mail Tracked 48 again, at the same £3.65, rising to £3.75, with £75 of cover.
- **Parcel C, 10kg box:** InPost to a home address at £5.99, if it fits the large compartment (41cm x 38cm x 64cm), with £50 of cover. InPost says home delivery can take up to 4 working days. If it does not fit, Royal Mail Tracked 48 at £7.35, rising to £7.55.

By situation:

- **If your buyer is happy to collect from a locker:** InPost, from £1.99 for a small parcel, with no printer needed and 30 days to drop off.
- **If the item is worth more than £75:** Parcelforce express48, with £150 of cover included, or InPost with its upgrade to full value up to £500.
- **If you want a heavy box collected from your door:** DPD, at £7.81 including VAT for 10kg, next working day.
- **If you sell on Vinted or use eBay Simple Delivery:** the platform's own label, because the platform sets the carrier.

What we would avoid:

- **Untracked 2nd Class for parcels.** It costs more than Tracked 48, has no tracking and includes only £20 of cover.
- **Booking-site headline prices taken at face value.** They are before VAT, and most services include only £20 of cover.

## Common mistakes

- **Sending untracked when tracked is cheaper.** On the October prices, a 2nd Class small parcel costs 65p more than Tracked 48.
- **Measuring the item, not the packed parcel.** Carriers price by the outer box.
- **Comparing prices with and without VAT.** InPost Direct, Parcel2Go and Packlink show prices before VAT.
- **Assuming cover is automatic.** Evri Postable parcels carry none, and most booking-site services include £20.
- **Setting postage once and forgetting it.** Update your listings before 5 October 2026, and run each parcel through our [postage finder](/tools/postage) to check it is in the right size band.
