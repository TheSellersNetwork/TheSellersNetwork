This is for Vinted sellers who have been told there is a trick to the algorithm, whether that is relisting on a schedule, posting at a particular time, or using a tool that does it for you. We take the claims that come up most, test each against what Vinted itself publishes, and say clearly where Vinted publishes nothing at all. By the end you will know what Vinted says affects visibility, what its rules forbid, and why automated relisting puts your account at risk. It is general information about Vinted's published rules, not legal advice, and Vinted updates its pages often.

## The short version

- Vinted does publish the factors it uses to rank search results and feeds. How recently an item was uploaded is one of them, alongside price, brand, interactions and the buyer's own history.
- Vinted's terms forbid deleting and re-listing the same item multiple times, or multiple items in bulk. No count or time period is given, so there is no "safe" schedule.
- "Post at 7pm on a Sunday" and "3 to 5 times more views" are not Vinted figures. We found no official data on the best time to post, or on how many more views any tactic gets.
- Duplicate listings are against the Catalogue Rules and are removed. Repeat breaches can lead to a ban.
- Outside software, including bots, is banned unless Vinted has authorised it. That covers most auto-relisting tools.
- The approved way to pay for visibility is a Bump or Showcase.

## What Vinted says it actually ranks on

Start with the facts, because Vinted is more open about this than many people realise. Its page on [recommended content](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/409-recommended-content-on-vinted) lists the parameters it uses, including:

- time since the item was uploaded, which Vinted says "helps to evaluate the chances of an item being sold";
- the number of interactions an item has collected, including impressions, favourites and clicks;
- the item's price, condition, category, brand, size, colour, photos and description;
- listing frequency, used when a seller uploads a number of items within a specific timeframe, "showing those items to buyers in a proportionate way";
- seller type, meaning a regular member or a Pro seller;
- the buyer's filters, search terms, history, location, language and who they follow.

Vinted says the most important factors are the filters members apply and the filters its system applies, such as country availability, and that "promoted content (such as new listings, or listings as part of our paid promotion services) is also an important factor". Its [Our platform](https://www.vinted.co.uk/our-platform) page gives a shorter version of the same list.

Two things follow. Newness genuinely matters, which is why relisting myths are so persistent. And Vinted describes uploading lots of items at once as something it balances out, not something it rewards.

## Myth 1: "Relist every 3 days to stay at the top"

**What is behind it.** Time since upload is a real ranking factor, so a fresh listing can get a short-term lift.

**What Vinted's rules say.** Section 6 of Vinted's [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions), "What you must and must not do", says you agree not to "delete and re-list the same Item multiple times or multiple Items in bulk". There is no number of days and no count. A routine of deleting and reposting the same item every few days is exactly the behaviour the clause describes.

What Vinted does allow: editing a listing, lowering the price (Vinted says followers are notified when you do, on its [selling faster](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/57-selling-your-items-faster) page), re-uploading an item after an order is cancelled, and paying for a Bump. Our guide to [relisting and automation rules](/guides/vinted-relisting-and-automation-rules) goes through each.

**Verdict.** The mechanism is real; the tactic breaks the terms. There is no officially safe schedule.

## Myth 2: "There is a best time to post"

**What Vinted says.** Nothing. We found no Vinted page that recommends a time of day or a day of the week, and the ranking parameters above do not include one. The "selling faster" page covers photos, descriptions, discounts, offers, Bumps and Showcase, and says nothing about timing.

**Our view (opinion, labelled as such).** Because newness is a factor, it is plausible that listing when more buyers are browsing helps a little. The times sellers swap with each other vary widely, though, and they are reported experience, not a rule, and it will vary by category and by buyer. If you want to know, test it on your own items over several weeks rather than trusting someone else's chart.

**Verdict.** No official answer exists. Treat any specific time as a guess.

## Myth 3: "This trick gets 3 to 5 times more views"

**What Vinted says.** Vinted does not publish multipliers for any tactic, including its own paid features. Its [Bump page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/340-what-are-item-bumps) says a bumped item "appears higher in member newsfeeds and search results" for 3 or 7 days or until it sells. It does not say by how much. You can see the views and interactions for a bumped item in your own profile, which is the only honest figure you will get.

**Verdict.** Any number like this comes from someone else's items on someone else's day. It is not a Vinted figure.

## Myth 4: "Vinted flags duplicates"

**What Vinted says.** This one is true. The [Catalogue Rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say "you should only have one listing per item", and Vinted's page on [hidden or removed listings](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/62-why-your-listing-was-hidden-or-deleted) says "the same items can't be listed twice on Vinted". If you have several of the same thing, Vinted says to make one listing, say in the description how many you have, and show each size's label or each colour in the photos.

The same page warns that repeatedly breaking the Catalogue Rules can lead to a temporary or permanent ban, and Bumps and Showcase are not refunded on listings that are hidden or removed.

**Verdict.** True. Duplicates are removed, and repeats count against you.

## Myth 5: "Put popular brands in your description to get found"

**What Vinted says.** The [Catalogue Rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/catalog-rules) say that using other brands not related to your item in the brand field, title, description or hashtags is not allowed. "Inspired by" followed by a brand name is also banned.

**Verdict.** False as a tactic, and it can get the listing removed. Use the correct brand and accurate item details, which Vinted does list as ranking factors.

## Myth 6: "Likes and follows boost you, so a bot to do them is fine"

**What is behind it.** Interactions, including favourites, are a ranking factor, and followers are notified when you list new items or lower prices.

**What Vinted's rules say.** Section 6 of the [terms](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions) says you must not use "any kind of external software tools (including but not limited to: bots, scraping programs, crawling programs, spiders)" when using the site, including "for the purpose of promoting Items, adding Items to favourite", unless the use is "authorised, offered or in any other way allowed" by Vinted. Section 12 bans unsolicited or mass private messages sent to 5 or more users.

**Verdict.** The factor is real; automating it is against the terms.

## The ban risk from automated relisting tools

Tools that log into your account and relist, favourite, follow or send offers for you are sold on the promise of beating the algorithm. Put them next to the rules:

1. **Relisting.** Automatic delete-and-repost is the behaviour section 6 prohibits, and a tool will do it more regularly and in bigger batches than a person would.
2. **The software itself.** A tool acting in your account is an external software tool under section 6 unless Vinted has authorised it. We could not find any Vinted UK page listing authorised third-party tools for standard accounts. If a tool's makers say Vinted allows it, ask for Vinted's confirmation in writing.
3. **Listing frequency.** Vinted says it shows bursts of uploads "in a proportionate way", so a bulk repost may not deliver the lift the tool promises.
4. **What Vinted can do.** The terms say Vinted can take corrective action, including warnings, removing items, blocking a feature such as messages, and blocking an account temporarily or permanently. Vinted says it may use automated tools to detect breaches. In the version of the terms that applies from 8 October 2026, a blocked account's items are delisted and paid extras still running are not refunded. Vinted says it will tell you in writing and explain your right to appeal.
5. **Appeals are limited.** Vinted's [banned account page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/92-why-was-my-account-blocked) says you can appeal a decision once, within 6 months.

Sellers report restrictions after using these tools. Vinted does not publish how it detects them or how many accounts are affected, so we are not repeating figures.

One more point: if you are relisting stock often enough to want a tool for it, you may be selling commercially. Vinted's [commercial selling page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/1120-commercial-selling) says anyone selling to make a profit needs a Pro account, and its banned account page lists commercial selling on a standard account as a reason for a ban. See [Vinted Pro and selling as a business](/guides/vinted-pro-and-selling-as-a-business).

## What actually helps, according to Vinted

These all come from Vinted's own pages, and none of them risk the account.

- **Accurate item details.** Brand, size, colour, condition, category and description are ranking parameters. Fill them in properly.
- **Good photos and full descriptions,** which Vinted recommends on its [selling faster](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/57-selling-your-items-faster) page.
- **Price drops,** which notify your followers.
- **Offers** to members who favourite your item.
- **Bundle discounts** for buyers who take more than one item. See [bundles and discounts](/guides/vinted-bundles-and-discounts).
- **Paid visibility, if the sums work.** A Bump lasts 3 or 7 days and its price depends on the item's price and the duration; Showcase highlights up to 15 of your listings to each viewer for 7 days and needs at least 5 items listed ([Showcase](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/452), [price list](https://www.vinted.co.uk/pricelist)). The price is shown at checkout. Our guide to [bumps and Showcase](/guides/vinted-bumps-and-spotlight) has a break-even sum.
- **Listing new items regularly,** which Vinted says notifies your followers. New items, not the same items reposted.

## What goes wrong

- **Following a schedule from a video.** No Vinted page gives one, and the relisting clause has no safe number.
- **Paying for a tool before reading section 6** of the terms.
- **Bumping a listing that breaks the rules.** If it is removed, the Bump is not refunded.
- **Listing the same item twice** in different sizes or colours instead of one listing.
- **Treating reported experience as a rule,** in either direction. Something working for one seller last month is not Vinted policy.
