This is for UK sellers on Amazon.co.uk, and for anyone deciding whether to start, who want to see exactly what comes off a sale. It takes one product and sells it twice: once through Fulfilment by Amazon (FBA) and once posted by you (FBM). Every fee is applied in order with the working shown, and the fees that only apply in some months or to some stock are explained with the rule and a link to Amazon's own page. The figures come from Amazon's [Europe rate card effective 1 July 2026](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf) and its [UK pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing), both checked in September 2026. Amazon changes its fees at least once a year, so check both before you rely on a number. This is general information, not tax or financial advice.

## The short version

- Every sale pays a **referral fee**, a percentage of the total price set by category. On FBA you also pay a **fulfilment fee** set by size tier and weight, plus a **1.5% fuel and logistics surcharge** on that fulfilment fee.
- Amazon adds a **2% digital services fee** on its selling and FBA fees for UK-established sellers, and **VAT at 20%** on all its fees. If you are not VAT registered, that VAT is a cost you cannot reclaim.
- In the example below, a £24.99 toy sold through FBA in September pays about **£8.54** in fees. Posted by you, the same sale pays about **£4.59** before your own postage.
- The **Professional plan** is £25 a month plus VAT. It is not charged per sale, so it costs more per sale when you sell less.
- **Storage** is charged monthly and roughly doubles from October to December. Stock older than 240 days picks up an **aged inventory surcharge**.
- Two fees you may read about in US material, the inbound placement fee and the low-inventory-level fee, are not on the UK rate card we checked.

## The example product

Take a boxed toy. It weighs 600 g and the box measures 25 x 20 x 10 cm. You sell it for £24.99 in the Toys and Games category. You are not VAT registered, and you are on the Professional plan.

**Find the size tier first.** The rate card says parcels are charged on the greater of the unit weight and the dimensional weight, which is length x width x height in centimetres divided by 5,000. Here that is 25 x 20 x 10 / 5,000 = 1.0 kg. So although the toy weighs 600 g, it is charged as a 1.0 kg item. The box fits the small parcel tier (up to 35 x 25 x 12 cm), and 1.0 kg falls in the band up to 1.4 kg.

A light item in a big box pays for the box.

## The FBA sale, fee by fee

| Line | Working | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sale price | | £24.99 |
| Referral fee | Toys and Games, 15% of £24.99 | £3.75 |
| FBA fulfilment fee | Small parcel, up to 1.4 kg, non-peak | £3.05 |
| Fuel and logistics surcharge | 1.5% of £3.05 | £0.05 |
| Storage for one month | 0.18 cubic feet x £0.76 (January to September rate) | £0.13 |
| Fees before the digital services fee | | £6.98 |
| Digital services fee | 2% of £6.98 | £0.14 |
| VAT on fees | 20% of £7.12 | £1.42 |
| Total fees | | £8.54 |
| Left from the sale | Before what you paid for the toy | £16.45 |

Pennies are rounded. Here is where each line comes from.

### Referral fee

A percentage of the total price the buyer pays, including any delivery charge and gift wrap, with a £0.25 minimum in most categories ([Amazon pricing](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing)). Toys and Games is 15% on the [rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf). Some categories use price bands: Home Products is 8% up to £20 and 15% above, and Clothing and Accessories is 5% up to £15, 10% from £15 to £20 and 15% above. Books, music, DVDs, video games and software also pay a £0.50 closing fee per item. The cheaper bands came in during the [2026 fee changes](/blog/amazon-2026-fee-changes-fba-and-clothing) and the [low-price referral cuts](/blog/amazon-low-price-fba-and-referral-cuts-2026).

### Fulfilment fee

A flat fee per unit by size tier and shipping weight: from £1.83 for the lightest envelope to £3.58 for a standard parcel up to 11.9 kg, then more for oversize. Some categories, including clothing, footwear and grocery, use separate parcel tiers priced by the 100 g. Two things change the fee on the same item:

- **Peak season.** From 15 October 2026 to 14 January 2027 the rate card lists a festive peak fee. For our small parcel up to 1.4 kg it rises from £3.05 to £3.16. Clothing is excluded.
- **Low-Price FBA.** Small, light items priced at £20 or less (£10 or less in some categories, such as Beauty, Grocery, Books and Kitchen) get lower fulfilment fees. A small parcel up to 400 g is £2.70 instead of £3.00. Our toy is over £20, so it does not qualify.

### Fuel and logistics surcharge

Since 17 April 2026 Amazon adds 1.5% to FBA fulfilment fees in the UK. It is 1.5% of the fulfilment fee, not of your price. Our [change post on the surcharge](/blog/amazon-fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge) has the detail; Amazon calls it temporary but has given no end date.

### Monthly storage

Charged per cubic foot per month on your daily average volume, using the size of the packaged unit. The UK rates for standard-size products outside clothing are £0.76 from January to September and £1.51 from October to December. Standard-size clothing, footwear and bags are £0.62 and £0.82. Our toy's box is about 0.18 cubic feet, so one month costs £0.13 now and £0.27 in October.

On top of this, a **storage utilisation surcharge** can apply when your stock would take more than 22 weeks to sell at your recent rate. The rate card says it does not apply to new sellers (first shipment less than 52 weeks ago), Individual plan sellers, or sellers storing less than 25 cubic feet on average.

The FBA New Selection programme on the [pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) offers free storage, removals and returns processing on eligible new-to-FBA products, with conditions.

### Digital services fee

For sellers established in the UK, Amazon adds a 2% digital services fee as a percentage increase on its selling and FBA fees ([Amazon announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/ed2f04c2-afa0-4bd4-b0fb-08efa212b5a6)). It is separate from VAT. Amazon raised the rate to 3% from 20 March 2026 for sales that UK sellers make in the France, Italy and Spain stores ([announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/seller-forums/discussions/t/4fdf2816-9760-45d8-b763-26051640abb9)), not in the UK store.

### VAT on fees

Since 1 August 2024 UK-established sellers are invoiced by Amazon EU's UK branch, and "VAT will be charged on your Amazon fees" ([Amazon announcement](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/seller-forums/discussions/t/fe8e800e-d95c-42ae-a98b-e6e682547f90)). The fee figures on the rate card and pricing page exclude VAT. If you are VAT registered you reclaim it on your return. If you are not, it adds a fifth to every fee.

The gap is not the whole comparison: you now pay for the label, the box and your time, and handle customer service and returns yourself. If you charge the buyer for delivery, the referral fee is taken on that too. Our [fee calculator for orders you post yourself](/tools/calculator/amazon) does this sum for any category.

## The selling plan

The Professional plan is £25 a month excluding VAT, so £30 with VAT for most sellers. The Individual plan has no monthly fee and charges £0.75 plus VAT per item sold instead ([Amazon pricing](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing)). At 34 or more sales a month, Professional is the cheaper of the two on fees alone.

Spread across sales, the Professional plan costs £1.50 a sale at 20 sales a month and 30p a sale at 100. It is not on the per-sale table above because it does not change with each order, but it belongs in your monthly sums.

## Fees that turn up later

### Aged inventory surcharge

Charged monthly on stock that has been in Amazon's warehouses too long, on top of normal storage. The UK rates per cubic foot per month are:

| Age | Rate |
| --- | --- |
| 241 to 270 days | £1.18 (not clothing, footwear, bags, luggage, jewellery or watches) |
| 271 to 300 days | £3.14 |
| 301 to 330 days | £3.26 |
| 331 to 365 days | £3.41 |
| 365 to 455 days | £5.71 per cubic foot or £0.20 per unit, whichever is greater |
| Over 455 days | £6.14 per cubic foot or £0.25 per unit, whichever is greater |

For our toy at 271 to 300 days old, that is 0.18 x £3.14, about 55p a month, before the digital services fee and VAT, on top of storage. The 241 day start came in with the [2025 changes](/blog/amazon-returns-processing-fee-2025).

### Returns

When an FBA customer returns an item, the referral fee is refunded minus a refund administration fee of £5 or 20% of the referral fee, whichever is less ([Amazon pricing](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing)). On our toy that is 20% of £3.75, about 75p. The rate card says FBA fees and returns processing fees may still be charged.

A separate **returns processing fee** applies only to products whose return rate is above their category's threshold, and only to the returns above it. For our small parcel up to 1.4 kg it is £2.49 a unit. Clothing, accessories and footwear are different: they pay 50% of the fulfilment fee on every return. The [returns fee change post](/blog/amazon-returns-processing-fee-2025) walks through Amazon's own example.

### Getting stock back or disposing of it

Removal and disposal are charged per unit by weight. For standard-size items from 501 g to 1 kg, return-to-seller is £1.57 and disposal is £2.28 on the July 2026 rate card.

### Inbound, placement and low-inventory fees

You may read about an inbound placement fee and a low-inventory-level fee in material written for Amazon.com. Neither appears for the UK on the July 2026 rate card. What it does list:

- a **manual processing fee** of £0.15 a unit if you send boxes without box content information;
- a **low-inventory cost coverage fee** that applies only to Pan-European FBA stock in Germany, France, Italy and Spain, not to UK-only sellers;
- **optional prep and label services** if Amazon does the work, for example £0.38 a unit to label an envelope-size item and £0.78 for a parcel.

Shipping stock to the warehouse is its own cost. If you use the Amazon Partnered Carrier programme, Send to Amazon shows the price before you accept it, and Amazon's [pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) lists a discount on inbound shipping for new sellers.
