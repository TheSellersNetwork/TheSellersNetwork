This is for UK Amazon resellers doing retail arbitrage, online arbitrage or wholesale, who want to know which sourcing tool does which job before paying for one. It compares eight tools using only what each provider publishes on its own pricing, product and help pages: what the tool is for, whether it covers Amazon UK, what it checks (eligibility, intellectual property risk, VAT) and the trial. Our earlier post on [what Amazon seller tools cost](/blog/amazon-seller-tools-what-they-cost) covers the wider types of tool, from repricers to accounting; this one goes deeper on sourcing only. We have no arrangement with any provider, and our own pick, at the end, rests only on the published facts. This is general information, not financial or tax advice.

## The short version

- These tools do three different jobs. **Deal analysers** check one product you have found. **Scanners** search retailers or supplier lists for you. **Price history** tools show how price, rank and sellers have moved over time.
- Published prices run from £13.95 a month plus VAT to $199 a month for a single user, with agency and enterprise plans above that.
- Most of the analysers flag eligibility and intellectual property risk, but they do it differently: some check your own Seller Central account, some use their own or community databases. A warning is a prompt to check, not a guarantee either way.
- VAT handling varies. Some let you choose between not registered, standard and flat rate; others only mention standard and not registered.
- Trials range from 3 days to 30 days. Only some say no card is needed.
- Our free [FBA calculator](/tools/calculator/amazon-fba) works out fees, profit, ROI and the most you can pay, but only from figures you type in. It has no Buy Box, sales rank or eligibility data.
- For a typical arbitrage seller we would choose SellerAmp SAS: one of the lowest published prices, eligibility and IP warnings, and a VAT scheme setting. See our verdict below.

## What matters most for resellers

1. **Which job you need done.** Checking finds in a shop, scanning websites, or scanning a supplier's price list are different tools.
2. **Amazon UK support.** Every tool here says it covers the UK, but check that the features you want work on the UK marketplace.
3. **Eligibility checks.** Does it check your own account, or give a general flag?
4. **IP warnings.** Where the data comes from, and how it is described.
5. **Your VAT position.** Whether the tool can model not registered, standard and the Flat Rate Scheme correctly for you.
6. **Price in pounds including VAT.** Several are listed in dollars or euros plus VAT.

## SellerAmp SAS

A deal analyser you use on a product page, in a web app or on your phone. SellerAmp's [pricing page](https://selleramp.com/pricing/) lists three UK plans at £13.95, £21.95 and £34.95 a month plus VAT, or £139.50, £219.50 and £349.50 a year plus VAT. The cheapest plan allows 1,000 product analyses a month; the two higher plans are unlimited, subject to a fair use policy.

**Good**

- Its [features page](https://selleramp.com/features/) lists eligibility to sell the product, hazmat and dangerous goods notes, and "Private Label or IP warnings".
- A VAT panel lets you "Override the VAT scheme, VAT rate and use VAT inclusive or exclusive prices".
- It shows Buy Box, Amazon, lowest FBA and lowest FBM prices.
- UK prices in pounds, a 14-day free trial on every plan, and two months free on yearly billing.

**Bad**

- The cheapest plan caps you at 1,000 analyses a month and one sourcing profile.
- It analyses products you find. It is not a website or supplier list scanner, apart from its Advanced Search, which shows 10 results a search on the cheapest plan.

## BuyBotPro

A deal analyser aimed at online arbitrage. BuyBotPro's [site](https://www.buybotpro.com/) lists Basic at £24.95, Pro at £34.95 and Enterprise at £99.95 a month, each plus VAT, with lower monthly equivalents on annual billing.

**Good**

- A 30-day free trial, the longest here.
- It lists VAT options for not VAT registered (it adds VAT to Amazon fees for you), standard VAT registered and flat rate registered.
- It lists an IP check, eligibility, hazmat detection and a competition view of FBA, Amazon and merchant-fulfilled sellers.
- It names Amazon UK, US, Canada, Germany, France, Spain and Italy.

**Bad**

- The feature BuyBotPro calls Suspension Safeguard is not on Basic, only Pro and Enterprise.
- The lowest monthly price is above SellerAmp's and retailscout's.

## Seller Assistant

A browser extension plus bulk tools. Seller Assistant's [pricing page](https://www.sellerassistant.app/pricing) lists Pro at $29.99, Business at $79.99 and Business Plus at $189.99 a month, with an Agency plan from $999.99 a month on an annual contract. Yearly billing saves about 17%.

**Good**

- Pro includes IP-Alert and a Bulk Restriction Checker for 4,000 ASIN checks a month.
- Business adds a Price List Analyzer for 150,000 product scans a month, for wholesale lists.
- Its help centre lists Amazon.co.uk among its [fully supported marketplaces](https://help.sellerassistant.app/en/articles/7050380-what-amazon-marketplaces-does-seller-assistant-support).
- A 14-day free trial.

**Bad**

- Priced in US dollars, so the sterling cost moves with the exchange rate.
- Its [VAT settings article](https://help.sellerassistant.app/en/articles/7050856-how-to-configure-vat-settings) covers standard rate, no VAT and not registered. We found no mention of the Flat Rate Scheme there.
- Wholesale price list scanning is on Business, not the cheapest plan.

## Keepa

Keepa describes itself as an Amazon price tracker, and its site includes a Product Finder and a Product Viewer for searching and checking products. Its [API documentation](https://keepa.com/api-docs/) uses amazon.co.uk in its examples. Price history is how you judge whether today's price is normal or a spike.

**Good**

- Seller Assistant shows Keepa charts inside its own extension, so some sellers see Keepa data through another tool.
- Its subscription terms, shown on the site, allow cancellation at any time, ending at the end of the monthly term.

**Bad**

- The subscription price is shown only inside Keepa's site, which did not load a price for us when we checked, so we have not quoted one. Check [keepa.com](https://keepa.com/#!data) before you pay.
- It does not check eligibility or IP risk. It tells you whether something sells, not whether you can sell it.

## Tactical Arbitrage (Seller 365)

Tactical Arbitrage is now sold inside Threecolts' Seller 365 bundle. The [pricing page](https://www.threecolts.com/pricing?tab=seller365) lists Standard at $69 a month, Teams at $129 and Pro at $199, or $65, $119 and $183 a month billed yearly. Standard includes Tactical Arbitrage and other Threecolts apps, including ScoutIQ, Scoutify, InventoryLab and SmartRepricer.

**Good**

- Its [product page](https://www.threecolts.com/seller-365/tactical-arbitrage) says it scans 1,400 or more retail sites and supports 13 Amazon marketplaces, including the UK.
- It lists an IP Alert Filter and a Restriction Checker for whether your account can sell a product or brand.
- It says it applies category-specific VAT rates for the UK and some EU marketplaces.
- Its [help centre](https://threecolts.support/en/articles/12815903-tactical-arbitrage-overview) describes product searches, reverse searches and wholesale list searches.

**Bad**

- The highest entry price among the scanners here, in US dollars.
- The 14-day trial is 7 days, plus 7 more only after you connect an Amazon Seller or Walmart account.
- You pay for a bundle of apps whether or not you use the others.

## SourceMogul

An online arbitrage scanner. SourceMogul's [pricing page](https://www.sourcemogul.com/pricing) lists £79.99 a month or £699.99 a year.

**Good**

- It says it scans 700 or more UK and US retailers, with Amazon category search, stock availability search and wholesale list upload.
- A Chrome extension is included.
- A 3-day free trial with no card required.

**Bad**

- The pricing page does not say whether VAT is included or added.
- We found no mention of IP alerts or eligibility checks on the pricing page.
- The 3-day trial is short for judging a scanner.

## retailscout

An in-store sourcing app for the UK. retailscout's [site](https://retailscout.app/) lists £14.99 a month, or £99 for the first year and then £149 a year, each with a 7-day free trial. It is run by a company registered in England and Wales.

**Good**

- Built for UK retail arbitrage: it maps 15 UK retailers across more than 13,000 store locations and plans routes of up to 20 stops.
- Its deal analyser shows profit, ROI, sales data and the Buy Box, with IP and hazmat alerts.
- It says calculations use your own cost, prep and VAT settings.

**Bad**

- UK only, and aimed at shop sourcing; it is not a wholesale list scanner.
- The site does not say whether the price includes VAT.

## Profit Scanner

A wholesale price list scanner. Profit Scanner's [plans page](https://profit-scanner.com/plans) lists Starter at €19.99, Pro at €49.99 and Premium at €79.99 a month, each plus VAT, with 17% off yearly billing.

**Good**

- You upload a supplier's CSV or Excel file and it returns prices, fees and risk flags across 11 Amazon marketplaces, including the UK ([home page](https://profit-scanner.com/)).
- It lists a community database of IP complaints and brand restrictions, and an eligibility check through your Seller Central account.
- A free plan, and a 7-day free Premium trial with no card required.

**Bad**

- Priced in euros plus VAT.
- Starter allows 10 scans a month and 20 product lookups a day.
- It is for supplier lists, not shop or website sourcing.
- Our view: a community IP database is only as good as its reports. Treat a clear result as "no reports found", not as permission.

## What our free calculator covers, and what it cannot

Our [FBA calculator](/tools/calculator/amazon-fba) takes the selling price, your cost, inbound shipping, prep, category, size tier, storage months and size, and works out the referral fee, fulfilment fee, fuel surcharge, storage and VAT on fees. It shows profit per unit, ROI and the most you can pay to hit the ROI you want. It is free and needs no sign-up.

It cannot:

- look up a product, its Buy Box price, sales rank or sales history;
- tell you whether your account is allowed to sell it;
- flag brands with IP complaints or hazmat status;
- scan a shop, website or supplier list.

For an exact fulfilment fee, Amazon's own [revenue calculator](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) is linked from its pricing page. Amazon's free [Seller app](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/tools/amazon-seller-app) lets you scan a barcode to see whether a product is already in the Amazon store. For eligibility, the checks inside Seller Central are the final word; see [ungating on Amazon UK](/guides/amazon-ungating-uk).

Our view: a free calculator plus Amazon's own tools is enough to learn on. The paid tools save time when you are checking dozens of products in a session, and they add data you cannot type in yourself.

## How to choose

Ask yourself:

1. Where do I source: in shops, on retailer websites, or from supplier price lists? See our guides to [retail arbitrage](/guides/amazon-retail-arbitrage-uk) and [online arbitrage](/guides/amazon-online-arbitrage).
2. How many products do I check in a normal week? If it is a few dozen, a capped plan may be enough.
3. Does the tool check eligibility against my own account, or only give a general flag?
4. Does it model my VAT position, including the Flat Rate Scheme if I use it?
5. What is the monthly cost in pounds including VAT, set against my monthly profit, not my sales?
6. Can I test it on a real sourcing session within the trial?

## Our verdict

If we had to choose one tool for a typical UK Amazon reseller, we would choose **SellerAmp SAS**. By typical we mean a sole trader doing retail and online arbitrage on Amazon UK, checking products they have found one at a time rather than scanning whole websites or supplier lists.

Why:

- It has one of the lowest published entry prices here, £13.95 a month plus VAT, in pounds, with two months free on yearly billing.
- It flags eligibility to sell the product, hazmat and dangerous goods notes, and private label or IP warnings.
- Its VAT panel lets you override the VAT scheme and rate, so you can model your own position.
- A 14-day free trial on every plan, and the cheapest plan allows 1,000 analyses a month, with unlimited analyses on the higher plans, subject to fair use.

By situation:

- **If you source mainly in shops:** retailscout, at £14.99 a month, because it is built for UK retail arbitrage, maps 15 UK retailers across more than 13,000 stores, plans routes and shows IP and hazmat alerts.
- **If you use the Flat Rate Scheme and source online:** BuyBotPro, because it lists a flat rate VAT option and has the longest trial here, 30 days.
- **If you buy from wholesale price lists:** Profit Scanner, because it has a free plan, a 7-day Premium trial with no card, and an eligibility check through your Seller Central account.
- **If you want software to scan retailer websites for you at volume:** Tactical Arbitrage, because it scans 1,400 or more retail sites and lists an IP Alert Filter and a Restriction Checker, though it starts at $69 a month.
- **If you are just starting:** our free [FBA calculator](/tools/calculator/amazon-fba) with Amazon's own Seller app and revenue calculator, until you are checking dozens of products a session.

What we would avoid:

- **Paying for a scanner before you know you need one.** The scanners here start at £79.99 a month (SourceMogul) or $69 a month (Tactical Arbitrage), against £13.95 plus VAT for SellerAmp's cheapest plan.

*We are not paid by any provider named here and there are no affiliate links. This is our opinion based on the published prices and terms when we checked them in September 2026; check the provider's current terms before you sign up.*

## Common mistakes

- **Treating an IP warning as a green light when it is silent.** No warning means no data found, not no risk. Our guide to [brand complaints and inauthentic claims](/guides/amazon-brand-complaints-and-inauthentic-claims) covers what happens when a brand does complain.
- **Using the wrong VAT setting.** Profit on the same product can look very different depending on whether you are registered.
- **Paying for a scanner and an analyser that do the same job.** Several bundle both.
- **Comparing a euro or dollar price with a pound price.** Convert and add VAT.
- **Letting a trial roll over.** Note the end date the day you start.
