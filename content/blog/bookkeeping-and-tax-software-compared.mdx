This is for UK resellers who trade as sole traders on eBay, Vinted, Amazon, Etsy or anywhere else, and who are choosing software to keep their books and deal with HMRC. By the end you will know what each of the main products costs on its own price page, whether HMRC lists it as working with Making Tax Digital for Income Tax, what it connects to, and which ones can be free. We checked every price and listing in September 2026, and at the end we say which we would choose and why. This is general information, not tax or financial advice.

## The short version

- Making Tax Digital (MTD) for Income Tax applies from 6 April 2026 if your qualifying income is over £50,000, from 6 April 2027 over £30,000 and from 6 April 2028 over £20,000 ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/check-if-youre-eligible-for-making-tax-digital-for-income-tax)). Qualifying income is turnover from self-employment and property, before expenses, not profit.
- HMRC's software finder listed 146 products for sole traders and landlords when we checked. Xero, QuickBooks, FreeAgent, Sage, Coconut, ANNA and Pandle are on it. Hammock is listed for property income only. GoSimpleTax is not listed under its own name; its MTD product runs through Coconut.
- Several routes are free: Sage Sole Trader Free, Coconut free for 24 months with a new Revolut or Zempler account, FreeAgent free with a NatWest, RBS, Ulster Bank or Mettle business account, and ANNA's MTD offer for 2026 to 2027.
- Intro offers are large and short. Xero, QuickBooks and Sage all offer 90% off for six months, then full price.
- None of them imports eBay or Vinted sales on its own. FreeAgent has a paid Amazon UK integration; Xero and QuickBooks rely mainly on connector apps.
- Under MTD you must file your tax return through MTD software, not HMRC's own online service.
- Our pick for a typical reseller is Sage Sole Trader Free: it costs nothing and HMRC lists it for both quarterly updates and the tax return.

## What matters most for resellers

Before comparing, answer these for yourself:

- **Will MTD apply to you, and when?** Add up your turnover across every platform and any property income. If it is heading past £20,000, you need software on HMRC's list within the next few years.
- **Where does your money land?** If your payouts go into one bank account, a bank feed does most of the work. If they sit in PayPal or a marketplace balance, check the software can pull those in.
- **Do you need double-entry books?** Most resellers do not. A simple income and expenses app is often enough for a sole trader who is not VAT registered.
- **Will it file your return?** Some products file both MTD quarterly updates and the year-end return. Some only file quarterly updates. Some only prepare figures for you to copy.
- **What happens after the intro offer?** Budget on the full price, not the first six months.
- **Are you VAT registered?** Several of the cheapest plans are for non-VAT-registered sole traders only.

## Xero

Full accounting software. Plans run from Ignite upwards.

**Good**

- On HMRC's MTD list for quarterly updates and the tax return, for sole trader, UK property and foreign property income.
- Ignite includes Hubdoc receipt capture and bank reconciliation.
- Xero makes its own Shopify and Etsy apps, and its app store lists third-party connectors such as A2X and Link My Books for Amazon, eBay and other marketplaces ([Xero App Store](https://apps.xero.com/uk/function/ecommerce)).
- Also on HMRC's list of [Self Assessment commercial software suppliers](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/self-assessment-commercial-software-suppliers/self-assessment-online-commercial-software-suppliers).

**Bad**

- Ignite is £18 a month plus VAT after the first six months at 90% off ([Xero pricing](https://www.xero.com/uk/pricing-plans/)).
- Ignite is limited to 20 invoices; unlimited invoices start on Grow at £39 a month plus VAT.
- Marketplace connectors other than Shopify and Etsy are separate products with their own charges.
- No free route.

## QuickBooks

Intuit's accounting software, with a Sole Trader Plus plan below its business plans.

**Good**

- On HMRC's MTD list for quarterly updates and the tax return.
- Sole Trader Plus includes automated bank feeds, receipt capture and CIS, and is listed as "MTD for Income Tax ready" ([QuickBooks pricing](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/uk/pricing/)).
- Its sole trader page lists Shopify, eBay, Amazon and Etsy among connectors ([QuickBooks](https://quickbooks.intuit.com/uk/business-type/sole-trader/)).
- Cancel at any time with no cancellation fee.

**Bad**

- Sole Trader Plus is £10 a month plus VAT after six months at 90% off; Simple Start, which adds VAT returns, is £16 a month plus VAT.
- Sole Trader Plus has no VAT return feature, so a reseller who registers for VAT has to move plan.
- For non-MTD Self Assessment, the plan page says "Prepare for Self-Assessment". QuickBooks was not on HMRC's list of Self Assessment commercial software suppliers when we checked.

## FreeAgent

Accounting software for sole traders and small companies.

**Good**

- On HMRC's MTD list for quarterly updates and the tax return, and on the Self Assessment commercial software list.
- Free if you keep a business account with NatWest, Royal Bank of Scotland, Ulster Bank or Mettle with at least one transaction a month ([FreeAgent pricing](https://www.freeagent.com/pricing/)).
- An Amazon UK integration that connects your Seller Central account ([FreeAgent integrations](https://www.freeagent.com/integrations/)).
- Annual billing brings the price to £190 a year plus VAT after the first year's 50% discount.

**Bad**

- £19 a month plus VAT if you do not bank with one of those four.
- The Amazon integration costs £6 a month plus VAT on top.
- Smart Capture includes 10 receipts a month; unlimited is £5 a month plus VAT.
- The integrations page lists no eBay or Etsy connector.

## Sage Sole Trader

Sage's app for sole traders who are not VAT registered, with a free plan and a paid plan.

**Good**

- Sage Sole Trader & Landlord Free is on HMRC's MTD list as a free version for quarterly updates and the tax return.
- The free plan includes one bank connection, the SA103S self-employment summary, a mobile app and 5 sales invoices a month ([Sage](https://www.sage.com/en-gb/products/sage-sole-trader/)).
- The paid plan adds receipt scanning, several bank accounts and unlimited invoices for £7 a month plus VAT.

**Bad**

- Both plans are for non-VAT-registered sole traders. VAT registration means moving to Sage's business plans.
- The free plan only auto-categorises 25 transactions and records receipts manually.
- The £7 price applies after six months at 90% off.
- No marketplace connectors named on the page we checked.

## Coconut

A sole trader and landlord app. Coconut and GoSimpleTax are owned by the same directors ([Coconut](https://www.getcoconut.com/gosimpletax-mtd)).

**Good**

- On HMRC's MTD list (paid and free versions) for quarterly updates and the tax return, including a bridging option for people who keep spreadsheets.
- Plans priced by the year: MTD Bridging £49.99, Bookkeeping £99.99, MTD filing £129.99, +Self Assessment £129.99 and Full £159.99 ([Coconut pricing](https://www.getcoconut.com/pricing)).
- Bank feeds, receipt snapping and automatic PayPal income import, useful for sellers paid through PayPal.
- 24 months free for eligible new customers who open a Revolut or Zempler account through the app.

**Bad**

- Filing a non-MTD Self Assessment return from the Bookkeeping plan costs an extra £29.99.
- The MTD year-end submission on the Bridging plan costs an extra £49.99.
- Monthly billing costs more than the annual prices above.
- No marketplace connectors other than PayPal on the pricing page.

## GoSimpleTax

Self Assessment software rather than bookkeeping software. You enter your figures and it files the return.

**Good**

- On HMRC's Self Assessment commercial software list, covering the main return and schedules including SA103 and SA108.
- Individual plan £69.99 a tax year on subscription, or £113.99 without, prices including VAT ([GoSimpleTax pricing](https://www.gosimpletax.com/pricing)).
- Free trial without a card.

**Bad**

- Not on HMRC's MTD finder under its own name. Its MTD service is provided through Coconut at £12.99 a month.
- No bank feeds or receipt capture in the Self Assessment product itself.
- Subscriptions renew automatically on 1 April.

## Hammock

Landlord software. We include it because it is often named in MTD lists.

**Good**

- On HMRC's MTD list for quarterly updates and the tax return for UK and foreign property income.
- Unlimited bank feeds and receipt photos on every plan, from £8 a month plus VAT ([Hammock pricing](https://www.usehammock.com/pricing/)).

**Bad**

- HMRC's list does not include sole trader income for Hammock. It does not cover reselling income on its own.
- Priced by number of properties, which means nothing for a reseller.

## ANNA

A business current account with bookkeeping and tax built in.

**Good**

- ANNA Self Assessment is on HMRC's MTD list and on the Self Assessment commercial software list.
- Its current offer: 2026 to 2027 MTD done for free, and your 2025 to 2026 Self Assessment filed for free if you sign up for the MTD offer ([ANNA](https://anna.money/plus-taxes/self-assessment/)).
- Bank feeds from any UK bank account via Open Banking.

**Bad**

- ANNA does not say on that page what MTD will cost after 2026 to 2027.
- Paid account plans are £12.90, £22.90 and £59.90 a month plus VAT ([ANNA pricing](https://anna.money/pricing)).
- No marketplace connectors named.

## Pandle

Bookkeeping software with a free plan, added here because it appears on HMRC's list with a free version.

**Good**

- On HMRC's MTD list for quarterly updates and the tax return.
- The free plan includes bank feeds and receipt uploads.
- Pro is £5 a month plus VAT and adds PayPal and Stripe feeds and stock control ([Pandle pricing](https://www.pandle.com/pricing/)).

**Bad**

- Pandle's pricing page lists MTD income tax submissions under Pro, not the free plan.
- Monthly billing only; no annual plan.

## Free with a business bank

Some banks now bundle MTD software. HMRC's list includes free versions from Monzo (quarterly updates and tax return), and from Starling and Tide Accounting (quarterly updates only, on the list when we checked). A tool that only sends quarterly updates means you need something else for the year-end return. ## How to choose

Ask yourself:

1. What was my turnover last tax year, and when does that put me in MTD?
2. Am I VAT registered, or likely to be in the next year?
3. Do I want to change bank to get free software, or keep my bank and pay?
4. Where do my sales land: one bank account, PayPal, or a marketplace balance?
5. Do I need a marketplace connector, or is a bank feed and a monthly platform report enough?
6. Do I want one product that files both MTD updates and the year-end return?
7. What will this cost in month seven, once the intro offer ends?

Our view: most resellers under the MTD thresholds do not need full accounting software yet. A tidy spreadsheet plus the platform reports is enough until turnover heads towards £20,000, and our [sales reports tool](/tools/sales-reports) turns the reports you download from your selling accounts into profit figures.

## Our verdict

If we had to choose one for a typical UK reseller, we would choose **Sage Sole Trader Free**. By typical we mean a sole trader who is not VAT registered, sells on eBay and Vinted, has payouts landing in one bank account, and needs MTD software now or within the next two years.

Why:

- **It is free, with no intro offer to run out.** Most paid options here jump to full price after six months.
- **It covers the whole job.** HMRC lists the free version for both quarterly updates and the year-end tax return, so you are not caught out in January.
- **One bank feed is enough** when all your platform payouts land in one account.
- **It produces the SA103S** self-employment summary, the short self-employment page of the return.

Picks by situation:

- **If you bank with NatWest, RBS, Ulster Bank or Mettle:** FreeAgent, because it is free with those accounts and HMRC lists it for quarterly updates and the tax return.
- **If you sell on Amazon at volume:** FreeAgent, because its Amazon UK integration (£6 a month plus VAT) connects your Seller Central account directly.
- **If you are paid mainly through PayPal:** Pandle Pro, because at £5 a month plus VAT it adds a PayPal feed, and its MTD submissions sit on the Pro plan.
- **If you are VAT registered or about to be:** QuickBooks Simple Start at £16 a month plus VAT, because it adds VAT returns and lists eBay, Amazon and Etsy connectors, while Sage Sole Trader and QuickBooks Sole Trader Plus are for non-VAT-registered sellers.

What we would avoid, and why:

- **Relying on a quarterly-only tool** for MTD, because you would still need something else to file the year-end return.
- **Hammock for reselling income**, because HMRC lists it for property income only.

The free plan's limits are real: 25 auto-categorised transactions and 5 invoices a month. If you outgrow them, the paid plan is £7 a month plus VAT after the intro offer.

## Common mistakes

- **Counting profit, not turnover.** MTD qualifying income is before expenses.
- **Choosing on the intro price.** 90% off lasts six months.
- **Picking a quarterly-only tool** and finding in January that it cannot file your tax return.
- **Assuming a connector is included.** Several are separate products.
- **Dropping the business account that made the software free** and not noticing the new charge.
- **Leaving the books until January.** Software helps only if you reconcile monthly.
