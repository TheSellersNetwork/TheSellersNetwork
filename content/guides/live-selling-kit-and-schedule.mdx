This is for UK resellers who have run a few live shows and want them to look better, happen reliably and stay under control when the chat gets busy. After reading it you will have a kit list that does not need a big budget, a way to build a weekly schedule, a plan for moderators, and a set of responses for disruptive users and buyers who do not pay. Platform tools come from Whatnot's and eBay's own help pages; they change, so check the linked page before relying on a setting.

## The kit

Buyers forgive a plain background. They do not forgive a picture they cannot read or sound they cannot hear. Spend in this order.

| Item | What to look for | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phone | Any recent phone with a good rear camera and enough storage; the rear camera is better than the front one | It is the camera, the encoder and often the chat screen |
| Stand or tripod | A floor or desk tripod with a phone clamp; for cards and small items, an overhead arm that holds the phone looking down | A steady shot at a fixed height means every lot is framed the same way |
| Light | One or two LED panels or a ring light with adjustable brightness and colour temperature | Shiny items (slabs, holos, toploaders) show glare under ceiling lights. Light from the side, not straight on |
| Microphone | A clip-on wired or wireless microphone that works with your phone | Phone microphones pick up the room. A cheap clip-on makes speech clear |
| Second screen | An old phone, tablet or laptop for chat and the sold list | You cannot read chat on the phone that is filming you |
| Internet | A stable connection; wired or close to the router | Buffering loses bids |
| Scale and printer | A parcel scale and a 4 x 6 inch thermal label printer | Needed for accurate shipping profiles and fast label printing afterwards |

eBay's own advice for eBay Live is an LED ring light, a dedicated wireless microphone and the latest version of the eBay app, and you need a desktop or laptop to create and manage events ([eBay Live selling](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762)). You can also stream to eBay Live from a camera through third-party broadcast software, which is the next step up once the basics work.

We are not naming brands or models. Look for the features above, read the returns policy, and buy the light and microphone before a better phone.

### Setting up the shot

- **Frame the selling area, not your face.** What buyers need is the item, close up, and your hands handling it.
- **Mark the spot.** Tape on the desk shows where each item goes so it is always in focus.
- **Keep everything in frame during openings.** eBay's [eBay Live policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478) requires packs, breaks and openings to stay in the camera frame and be clearly visible throughout. Whatnot's [Card Breaks Policy](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/34107485220237-Card-Breaks-Policy) has the same rule and allows more than one camera.
- **No music you do not own.** eBay Live allows only music, sounds, images and video you own the rights to or have written permission to use. Background music from a streaming service is not that.
- **Do a test stream.** eBay lets you save a draft event with a shareable link for testing. Whatnot has Rehearsal Mode for practising auctions.

## Building a regular schedule

Live selling is closer to a radio slot than a listing. Buyers come back to shows they know will be on.

### Pick a slot you can keep for three months

Choose one or two evenings a week you can do every week, at the same time. Sellers we know found that a consistent slot matters more than finding the perfect one; the audience grows around it. Weekday evenings after dinner and weekend afternoons are the usual choices, but your stock and your buyers decide. Use the viewer and sales figures from each show to adjust after a month, not after one quiet night.

Plan the packing too. On Whatnot every order must have a drop-off scan within [two business days](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/43295059160973-Shipping-Policy-for-Sellers), so a show on a night before a day you cannot get to the post office is a problem. [Packing orders after a show](/guides/packing-orders-after-a-show) sets out the deadlines on each platform.

### Make the schedule visible

- **Whatnot.** Schedule shows in advance from Seller Hub, and use the repeat schedule option ([schedule, edit and start a show](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9778927885581-Schedule-edit-and-start-a-show)). Add a 9:16 thumbnail (Whatnot recommends 1080 x 1920 pixels), tags, show notes and listings so buyers can browse and pre-bid. Share the link early so people can bookmark it.
- **eBay Live.** Create and publish events in Live Studio with a title, description, start time, tags and an image (eBay recommends 1050 x 1575 pixels). Buyers can tap the bell for a notification when you go live, and follow your shop for alerts. You can duplicate an event to set up next week's quickly.
- **Everywhere else.** Put the weekly slot in your profile, pin it, and say it at the end of every show.

### Stay live and present

Both platforms expect you to be there. eBay [automatically ends a stream after 60 minutes of inactivity](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478) (except for case break sellers), and treats a host not visible or speaking for several minutes, or a placeholder screen, as inactive. Whatnot's [Community Guidelines](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061197472-Whatnot-Community-Guidelines) include an active show rule. Take a break by saying so and coming back quickly, not by leaving a still image up.

### Multistreaming

eBay Live events [may not be streamed at the same time on other live selling platforms](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478), but may be streamed simultaneously on non-auction platforms such as Twitch, YouTube or Instagram. Check each platform's own terms before streaming one show to two selling platforms; running two auctions for the same item at once is how you sell it twice.

## Moderators and chat

A moderator lets you sell while someone else reads the chat, answers questions and removes trouble. For a show with more than a handful of regular viewers, one is worth more than any piece of kit.

### On Whatnot

- **Adding a moderator.** When scheduling, open the Moderators section and add their username; during a show, tap their name in chat and choose Allow to Moderate ([Moderator Guidelines](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/6212550318477-Moderator-Guidelines)).
- **What they can do.** Remove users from the show and see muted chat messages.
- **Chat commands** for sellers and moderators ([livestream moderation commands](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/21988369770637-Livestream-moderation-commands)):

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| /announce message | Highlights a message in chat |
| /hide message | Sends a message only you and your moderators see |
| /slow seconds | Sets a cooldown between chat messages |
| /raid username | Sends your viewers to another show when you end yours |

- **Muted words.** Add them when scheduling the show so they are hidden automatically.

Whatnot's guidelines ask moderators to warn before removing and not to remove people for disagreeing. Brief yours on that, and on your shipping and auction rules so they can answer the common questions.

### On eBay Live

eBay's automated chat moderation blocks offensive messages before they appear. In the Stream manager you can reply, pin a message, set banned words, mute users and search chat ([eBay Live selling](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762)). Muting stops someone chatting but not bidding or buying; to stop them buying, add them to your Block Buyer list. With Team Access you can give another eBay member permission to moderate chat and manage the event without sharing your password.

### On TikTok Shop LIVE

We have not covered TikTok's moderation settings here. Check TikTok's own LIVE help before your first show. Note that buyers need an [active payment card on file](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2301024674400022&default_language=en-GB) to bid in TikTok LIVE auctions.

### What a moderator does in a show

1. Welcome regulars by name and answer shipping and condition questions from your show notes.
2. Keep the sold list: winner, lot, anything special about packing.
3. Warn once, then remove, anyone abusive, spamming or advertising.
4. Tell you, with a private message, about a failed payment or a question you need to answer.
5. Point people to the next show at the end.

## Disruptive users, blocking and buyers who do not pay

### Troublemakers in chat

Whatnot's advice on [dealing with disruptive users](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/32239228636557-Dealing-with-Disruptive-Users-in-your-show) starts with staying calm, because reactions are what disruptive users want. Then use the tools.

| Tool (Whatnot) | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| Remove from show | Immediately removes them; they cannot chat, bid or rejoin that show |
| Block | Stops them following you, messaging you, viewing your profile, joining your shows or buying from you. Orders placed before the block are still honoured ([block someone](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9816865522317-Block-someone-on-Whatnot)) |
| Require verified buyers | Only buyers with a purchase history or an authorised payment method can take part |
| Disable pre-bids | Stops one account placing unrealistic pre-bids on everything |

Blocked users are not notified. There is no list of people you have blocked, so keep your own note of usernames and why.

On eBay Live, mute for chat and the Block Buyer list for bidding. eBay also lets sellers set buyer requirements, for example to restrict buyers with a history of unpaid items, and says it may restrict users who are often blocked by sellers from eBay Live events.

### Bidders who do not pay

| Platform | What happens | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whatnot | A declined buyer is prompted to fix payment and has until the end of the show. If still unpaid, the order is cancelled automatically and you do not ship ([fix a payment issue](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/37362462405261-Fix-a-payment-issue-or-declined-payment)) | Give an honest buyer a minute, since a mistyped card is common. If it looks deliberate, re-run the lot and remove and block the bidder |
| eBay Live | Winners are charged automatically when the auction ends; an unsuccessful payment shows as awaiting in the Stream manager | Message the buyer, then cancel at your discretion ([eBay Live selling](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762)) |
| TikTok Shop LIVE | Bidders must have an active card on file before bidding | Check payment status in the Auction sold items tab |

Do not chase non-payers off the platform, and do not share your bank details to take payment another way. eBay's [eBay Live policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478) and Whatnot's [Community Guidelines](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061197472-Whatnot-Community-Guidelines) both prohibit off-platform dealing, and a buyer who will not pay through the app is not going to be easier to deal with outside it.

## What goes wrong

- **A better phone before a light and a microphone.** Picture and sound quality come mostly from the light and the microphone, not the camera.
- **Chat on the filming phone.** You cannot read it and the shot moves every time you touch it.
- **A new slot every week.** Nobody can find you twice.
- **No moderator once the room grows.** One troll takes over the show while you are selling.
- **Arguing with trolls on stream.** Remove, block, move on.
- **Re-running a lot the moment a payment fails.** Honest buyers mistype cards; give them a moment.
- **Copyrighted background music.** eBay can remove the stream or the replay.
