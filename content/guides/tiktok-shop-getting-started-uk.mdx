This is for UK resellers thinking about opening a TikTok Shop, or halfway through the sign-up and stuck. After reading it you will know whether you qualify, which seller type to pick, what documents to have ready so verification passes first time, and the one question most resellers need answered before anything else: whether TikTok Shop will let you sell used stock. Every rule links to TikTok's own Seller Academy page. TikTok changes these pages often, so check the page before relying on it.

## Can you sell on TikTok Shop UK at all

TikTok's [registration policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099606786&default_language=en-GB) (last updated 8 September 2026) sets out two seller types.

**Sole traders** must be at least 18, hold a valid passport or driving licence, give a UK home address with proof, keep their stock in the UK, ship UK orders from the UK and accept returns in the UK.

**Corporate sellers** must be a business legally incorporated in the UK, with a UK business address, stock held in the UK, and a business representative who holds a valid passport or driving licence and is a local resident with UK proof of address.

Anyone currently under investigation or subject to enforcement action by a competent authority cannot register until that is resolved in their favour or the sanction has expired.

Two points worth checking before you start, because they catch people out.

- **Your ID must be UK issued.** The [ID verification page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/course?learning_id=4561669859378977&content_id=4561669859116833&lang=en-GB) accepts only a UK passport (signed) or a UK driving licence, and says the document cannot come from outside the UK. An older [eligibility page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/course?learning_id=1099176185202434&content_id=7753844603963137&default_language=en-GB) goes further and says only UK citizens can apply as individual sellers. If you live here on a non-UK passport and have no UK driving licence, expect the individual route to fail, and ask TikTok Shop support before you spend time on it.
- **Your bank account must match.** The [registration policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099606786&default_language=en-GB) asks for an unredacted first page of a statement from a UK or EU bank, and the account holder's name must match your ID (or, for a company, the registered company name).

## Sole trader or corporate seller

This is the decision people agonise over. It is simpler than it looks: register as what you already are.

| You are | Register as | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reselling on your own, not incorporated | Sole trader | The corporate route needs a Companies House certificate you do not have. |
| Trading through a limited company | Corporate seller | The shop and the bank account must match the company, and the income belongs in the company's books. |
| A partnership or two people selling together | Ask support before registering | The published pages cover sole traders and incorporated businesses; they do not describe a partnership route. |

Do not set up a limited company just to open a TikTok Shop. The platform does not require it, and a company brings accounts, filing and costs that only make sense for other reasons. Our [bookkeeping and tax guide](/guides/bookkeeping-and-tax-for-resellers) covers that decision properly. None of this is tax, legal or financial advice.

Whichever you choose, TikTok reports seller income to HMRC under the digital platform reporting rules, so keep records from the first sale. See [HMRC's platform reporting rules](/blog/hmrc-platform-reporting-rules).

## What to have ready

Gather these before you open the sign-up. Verification that stalls halfway is the most common reason new shops sit unused for weeks.

### Sole trader

- A TikTok account, a phone number or an email to sign up with ([how to register](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753849800820481&lang=en-GB)).
- Your original UK passport or UK driving licence, physically in front of you. The [ID verification page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/course?learning_id=4561669859378977&content_id=4561669859116833&lang=en-GB) says you cannot use a photo or copy at the verification stage.
- A proof of address dated within the last 3 months: bank statement or utility bill, unredacted.
- Your UK bank details and a statement first page in the same name.
- Your date of birth exactly as it appears on the ID.
- A warehouse address (for most resellers, home) with proof.
- A shop name that follows the rules below.

### Corporate seller

The [documents page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753857149323009&default_language=en-GB) lists: the certificate of incorporation from Companies House, company number and registration date, proof of the business or warehouse address, ID and UK proof of residential address for the director or primary authorised user, an authorisation letter if the person registering is not the director, and business bank details in the company's name. The [registration policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099606786&default_language=en-GB) also asks for a VAT number where you have one.

### Getting the selfie and ID photos accepted

TikTok's [ID verification page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/course?learning_id=4561669859378977&content_id=4561669859116833&lang=en-GB) lists the usual reasons photos are declined: a blurry ID, only part of your face visible, the ID covering your face, or part of the ID cut off. Edited photos are refused. In practice:

- Use daylight or a bright room, not a flash that glares off the card.
- Hold the ID below your chin, not in front of your face.
- Get the whole card and your whole face in the frame, then check the photo before submitting.
- Upload both sides of the ID where asked. The [registration policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099606786&default_language=en-GB) asks for front and back, in colour, unobstructed, as PNG or JPG.

### Choosing a shop name

The [registration policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099606786&default_language=en-GB) says a shop name must not exceed 40 characters, must not include "TikTok", someone else's brand without permission, or contact details, must not be only numbers or special characters, and must not be a generic word such as "Shop". You can change it once every 30 days, but not in the first 30. Pick something you will still be happy with in a year, because buyers who follow you will know it by that name.

## Pre-owned stock: the question that decides everything

Most UK resellers sell used goods. Read this section before you register.

TikTok's [Restricted Products Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753822475273986&lang=en-GB) (last updated 27 August 2026) says all second-hand products can only be sold by sellers onboarded through a limited, invite-only process. A newly opened shop cannot simply list a used jacket or a played-with toy. You need an invitation or an approved application for the second-hand category, and the application asks for your business background, category list, warehouse images and links showing your e-commerce performance elsewhere.

Where you are approved, each second-hand category has its own rules. The main ones for resellers:

| Category | Key rules from the policy |
| --- | --- |
| Second-hand fashion | Title must say "second-hand", "pre-owned" or "pre-loved"; at least 2 images, one showing flaws; maximum £500 per listing; underwear, socks and swimwear must be new or new with tags |
| Second-hand trading cards | Invite-only; graded cards only from listed grading companies and no more than £1,000 per listing; ungraded cards need at least 2 images and a condition description; repacked cards must say so |
| Second-hand books | At least 2 images (front and back covers); condition graded as like new, very good, good or fair, with defects described |
| Second-hand luxury watches and jewellery | At least 2 images; condition good, very good or excellent only |
| Pre-owned electricals | Phones, tablets, smart watches and laptops only, refurbished; needs a registered UK business, certification, UKCA or CE marking and at least a 1 year warranty |
| Second-hand golf equipment | Minimum images of the item and its condition |

Second-hand products cannot be sold as mystery or surprise items. The policy also lists new categories that need documents (toys, electricals, beauty and others) and a separate [Prohibited Products Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408307457). Our guide to [account health and restricted products](/guides/tiktok-shop-account-health-and-restricted-products) goes through those.

### What that means in practice

- **If you mainly sell new stock** (clearance, retail arbitrage, new with tags), TikTok Shop is open to you from day one, subject to category documents.
- **If you mainly sell used clothing, cards or books**, register only if you are prepared to apply for the second-hand invite and wait. Until you are approved, the shop has nothing to sell.
- **If you sell used electricals from home**, TikTok Shop is not a realistic channel. The requirements assume a registered refurbishment business.

Do not list used items as new to get round the rule. It breaches the condition requirements in the same policy, and it is the kind of listing violation that costs account health points quickly.

## Your first weeks: probation and limits

New shops start in TikTok's [Shop Probation Program](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753849800492801). Early tiers cap how many orders you can take per day and how many products you can list, and you move up by completing a probation quiz, reaching order and time milestones, and keeping your shop performance up. The exact caps and steps are on that page and in your Seller Center. Plan your first stock around them: there is no point photographing 300 items if the shop can only list a fraction of them.

New sellers are also paid more slowly. TikTok's [settlement period page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099475714&default_language=en-GB) says introductory and probation sellers are paid 31 days from delivery, against 8 days on the standard period. Budget for a month or more before the first money arrives. The [statements and settlements guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-statements-and-settlements) explains the tiers.

## What it costs

The standard commission is 9% including VAT, and it is charged on the order total including the shipping the buyer pays, not just the item price ([commission policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3337893683398432&lang=en-GB)). Some electronics and beauty categories are reduced to an effective 5%. New sellers can get a lower rate for a period; TikTok does not publish it, so check your Seller Center. We covered the change in detail in [TikTok Shop UK commission rose from 5% to 9%](/blog/tiktok-shop-uk-commission-9-percent), and compared the total cost with other live platforms in [live selling fees compared](/guides/live-selling-fees-compared).

Add affiliate commission if creators sell for you (see [affiliates and samples](/guides/tiktok-shop-affiliates-and-samples)), and shipping, which depends on the method you choose (see [shipping and returns](/guides/tiktok-shop-shipping-and-returns)).

## A sensible order to do things

1. Decide what you will sell first. If it is used stock, find out whether you can get the second-hand invite before anything else.
2. Gather the documents above, including a recent proof of address.
3. Register as what you already are: sole trader or company.
4. Link your personal TikTok account to the shop so you can post shoppable videos and go LIVE.
5. List a small number of products that sell well elsewhere, with honest condition and clear photos.
6. Ship the first orders fast and with tracking. Early performance shapes your score and your payout speed.
7. Read your first statement line by line before you scale.

## What goes wrong

- **Registering with the wrong ID.** A non-UK passport, a provisional licence photo that does not match, or a date of birth typed differently from the ID.
- **Proof of address older than 3 months** or with numbers blacked out. The policy asks for unredacted documents.
- **Bank account in a different name.** A partner's account, or a personal account for a company shop.
- **Opening a shop full of used stock without the invite.** Listings are removed and the shop picks up violations.
- **Setting prices from the item price alone.** Commission is on the total including shipping.
- **Ignoring the probation caps.** Promoting heavily before the shop can take the orders leads to cancellations, which hurt you twice.
- **Expecting money in a week.** New sellers wait around a month after delivery.
