This is for UK resellers deciding where to run live shows, or checking whether the platform they use is still the cheapest for what they sell. It sets out each platform's fee from its own UK page, shows what the fee is charged on, and works through a £10 and a £40 sale on all three. Every figure links to the page it came from. Whatnot changed its commission structure on 21 September 2026 and eBay Live has a promotion running to the end of 2026, so check the pages before you rely on the numbers. This is general information, not tax or financial advice.

## The fees on one page

| | Whatnot (UK) | eBay Live (UK) | TikTok Shop UK |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commission | 6.67% plus VAT for a new seller in the standard tier; lower in higher tiers; Coins 4% | 6% of the total sale, capped at £100 per item; 4% for Coins, Bullion and athletic trainers | 9% including VAT |

| Payment processing | 2.42% plus £0.25 per transaction, plus VAT, on the total the buyer pays including shipping | 2.42% of the total plus £0.30 per order | No separate processing fee listed |
| Fixed fee per order | £0.25 within processing | £0.30 within processing; £0.10 on orders of £10 or less until 31 December 2026 | None listed |
| VAT | Commission and processing are subject to VAT | Fees shown exclude applicable taxes | Rate includes VAT |

## Each platform in more detail

### Whatnot

Whatnot now charges commission on a sliding scale. Your total sales across all categories in a four-week period set your tier for the next four weeks, and each tier has a rate per category. The UK rate card on the [seller fees page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4847069165965-Whatnot-seller-fees) starts every category except Coins at 6.67% in the standard tier (£0 to £9,999 of sales in the period), with the first step down at £10,000. Coins start at 4%. All rates are subject to VAT. Live sales are charged at the rate for the show's category; marketplace sales at the rate for the product's category.

| UK tier (sales in four weeks) | Sports, TCG, Other Collectibles | Fashion | Coins | Other |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standard, £0 to £9,999 | 6.67% | 6.67% | 4.00% | 6.67% |
| Tier 1, £10,000 to £19,999 | 6.50% | 6.25% | 3.90% | 6.25% |
| Tier 2, £20,000 to £34,999 | 6.25% | 5.75% | 3.80% | 5.50% |

Higher tiers are on the page. Most sellers reading this will be in the standard tier for some time, so the worked examples use 6.67%.

Commission is charged on the item price only. Payment processing is separate: 2.42% plus £0.25 per transaction, plus VAT, on everything the buyer pays including shipping. Whatnot's page does not say in so many words whether several wins bundled into one parcel count as one transaction or several; check the earnings breakdown on your first few orders in Seller Hub.

VAT on the fees matters if you are not VAT registered, because you cannot reclaim it. Whatnot's page on [taxes on commissions and fees](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/32083195713421-Taxes-on-Whatnot-commissions-and-fees) explains how it treats VAT-registered sellers. The UK standard rate is [20%](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates).

Postage is paid by the buyer and Whatnot provides the label, so on a standard profile the postage line nets to nothing for you.

### eBay Live

eBay Live has its own fees, separate from the normal eBay final value fees. The [eBay Live selling fees page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/ebay-live-selling-fees?id=5870) splits them into a commission (6% in most categories, 4% for Coins, Bullion and athletic trainers, capped at £100 per item) and payment processing (2.42% plus £0.30 per order). The two are charged together as one final value fee of 8.42% plus £0.30, or 6.42% plus £0.30 in the lower categories. Both are charged on the total amount of the sale, which includes the postage and any tax the buyer pays.

Other points from the same page.

- Until 31 December 2026 the per-order fee is £0.10 on orders of £10 or less. The page does not say whether the £10 is the item price or the total including postage. On a £10 item with postage, the total is above £10, so do not count on the lower fee until you have seen it on an invoice.
- Each purchase in an event is a separate order with its own per-order fee, even if you later combine the orders into one parcel.
- There is no insertion fee for eBay Live listings, and eBay does not charge Promoted Listings general ad fees on items that sell through eBay Live.
- The fees shown exclude applicable taxes. Check your eBay invoice to see whether VAT is added to yours.

eBay Live is open to [eligible business sellers and sole proprietors](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762) who meet eBay's requirements, in a list of categories on that page. The free selling that UK private sellers get on the main eBay site is a different scheme and does not describe eBay Live.

### TikTok Shop UK

TikTok Shop UK's commission is [9%, including VAT](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408913665&default_language=en-GB), calculated on net sales plus customer-paid shipping plus any platform discount, less refunds. The same rate applies to LIVE sales; we found no separate LIVE fee on TikTok's UK pages. TikTok's [introduction to seller fees](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522154754&lang=en-GB) lists commission, shipping fees and affiliate commission, and no payment processing fee.

Things that change the figure.

- **Affiliate commission.** If an affiliate creator sells your product, you pay the commission rate you agreed with them on top, on the product price less discounts.
- **Category reductions.** Some Electronics and Beauty and Personal Care categories get an effective 5%, listed on TikTok's [commission policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3337893683398432&lang=en-GB).
- **New seller rates.** TikTok offers new sellers a reduced rate for a period, but does not publish the rate or length on its public pages. Check your Seller Centre.
- **Shipping.** If you use Ship by TikTok, TikTok charges you a shipping fee and credits the customer-paid shipping, so your net postage cost depends on the rate TikTok gives you.

Our breakdown of the [TikTok Shop commission rise to 9%](/blog/tiktok-shop-uk-commission-9-percent) has more on the formula.

## Worked example: a £10 sale and a £40 sale

To compare like with like, the examples assume the buyer pays £3.27 postage on every platform. That is what Whatnot charges a UK buyer for a small parcel on its [buyer shipping page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/21559276628621-Shipping-for-buyers-in-the-UK). Each is a single item in a non-coin category, sold by a seller who is not VAT registered and is in Whatnot's standard tier. Figures are rounded to the penny.

### £10 item, £3.27 postage (£13.27 paid by the buyer)

| | Whatnot | eBay Live, fees as shown | eBay Live, if VAT is added at 20% | TikTok Shop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commission | 6.67% of £10 = £0.67, plus VAT = £0.80 | 8.42% of £13.27 = £1.12 (commission and processing combined) | | 9% of £13.27 = £1.19 |
| Processing and fixed fee | 2.42% of £13.27 + £0.25 = £0.57, plus VAT = £0.69 | £0.30 | | none |
| Total fees | £1.49 | £1.42 | £1.70 | £1.19 |
| You keep from the £10 | £8.51 | £8.58 | £8.30 | £8.81 |

If eBay's £0.10 promotional per-order fee applies to the order, the eBay Live totals fall to £1.22 before tax and £1.46 with VAT.

### £40 item, £3.27 postage (£43.27 paid by the buyer)

| | Whatnot | eBay Live, fees as shown | eBay Live, if VAT is added at 20% | TikTok Shop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Commission | 6.67% of £40 = £2.67, plus VAT = £3.20 | 8.42% of £43.27 = £3.64 (commission and processing combined) | | 9% of £43.27 = £3.89 |
| Processing and fixed fee | 2.42% of £43.27 + £0.25 = £1.30, plus VAT = £1.56 | £0.30 | | none |
| Total fees | £4.76 | £3.94 | £4.73 | £3.89 |
| You keep from the £40 | £35.24 | £36.06 | £35.27 | £36.11 |

"You keep" is before the cost of the item, packaging and, on eBay Live and TikTok, the label. On Whatnot the £3.27 pays for the label. On eBay Live and TikTok the £3.27 is yours and the label comes out of it. A Tracked 48 small parcel bought online from Royal Mail costs £3.75 from 5 October 2026 ([online price guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf)), so charging £3.27 on those platforms would leave you 48p short on every small parcel unless you post more cheaply. Our [Royal Mail October 2026 breakdown](/blog/royal-mail-prices-october-2026) has the other prices.

### What the examples show

- **On fees alone the three are close.** On these assumptions the gap between cheapest and dearest is 30p on a £10 sale and under 90p on a £40 sale. The postage arrangement and the audience matter more than the fee table.
- **VAT on fees is the swing factor for eBay Live.** Before tax it is the middle of the three on a £10 item; with 20% added it is the dearest.
- **Whatnot's commission ignores postage; the others do not.** The heavier the parcel, the more that helps Whatnot's figure.
- **Fixed fees hurt small lots.** On a £3 lot, a 25p or 30p fixed fee is 8 to 10% of the price before any percentage. Group cheap items into bundles or multi-item lots rather than running them one by one.
- **Coins are cheaper on both Whatnot and eBay Live.** Both charge 4% commission rather than the standard rate.
- **Whatnot gets cheaper as you grow.** At £10,000 of sales in four weeks the commission steps down, category by category.

## Figures we could not confirm

- **Whatnot's fixed fee on bundles.** Whether several wins shipped together count as one £0.25 transaction or several.
- **Whatnot's lower fixed fee on small UK orders.** A limited-time UK promotion that cut the fixed part of the processing fee on sales of £10 or less appeared in search results, but its help page no longer exists. Treat it as ended.
- **eBay Live VAT.** eBay's page says the fees exclude applicable taxes but does not say how VAT is charged on them for UK sellers. Your invoice will show it.
- **eBay Live's £10 promotion threshold.** Whether it is measured on the item price or the order total.
- **TikTok's new seller rate.** Not published publicly.
- **TikTok category spreadsheet.** The category rates are in a downloadable file on the [category rates page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3315312175236897&default_language=en-GB), which we did not rely on for trading cards or collectables. Check your category there.

## How to choose

Fees rarely decide it. A rough order of questions, from sellers we know who run on more than one platform.

1. **Where are your buyers?** Trading cards, sports cards and collectables have large live audiences on Whatnot; eBay Live brings eBay's existing buyers; TikTok's audience finds you through video.
2. **Can you post it for what the buyer pays?** Whatnot's buyer-paid label removes the postage risk. On the others, check your label price against what you charge.
3. **What does your average lot sell for?** Under about £5, fixed fees dominate and bundling matters more than the percentage.
4. **Are you VAT registered?** If you are not, VAT on Whatnot's fees, and on eBay's if it applies, is a real cost.
5. **Can you meet the dispatch rules?** Whatnot wants a drop-off scan within two business days; eBay Live's handling time starts when the event's eight-hour shopping window closes; TikTok's timeframes depend on the shipping type. [Packing orders after a show](/guides/packing-orders-after-a-show) covers each.

## What goes wrong

- **Comparing headline percentages.** 6.67% on the item alone and 9% on item plus postage are not the same base.
- **Forgetting VAT on fees.** It adds a fifth to Whatnot's fees if you are not VAT registered.
- **Charging buyers less postage than the label.** On eBay Live and TikTok the difference comes out of your margin on every order.
- **Running single cheap lots.** The fixed fee eats the sale.
- **Relying on promotions.** They end, sometimes without the help page saying so.
