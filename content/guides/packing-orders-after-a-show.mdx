This is for UK sellers whose shows are going well enough that packing has become the bottleneck. After reading it you will know how each platform combines a buyer's wins into one parcel, how to set up a packing run that gets 50 orders out in an evening without sending the wrong card to the wrong person, and exactly how long you have on each platform. Deadlines and tools come from each platform's own pages; they change, so check the linked page before relying on them.

## The deadlines

| Platform | When the clock starts | Deadline | What counts as shipped | If you miss it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Whatnot | The sale | Two business days, excluding weekends and public holidays | A drop-off scan: Royal Mail acceptance, a kiosk scan, the Whatnot in-app scanner or a Whatnot Manifest | Late shipments hurt your ship time and on-time scan rate; buyers can cancel or be refunded at your cost; repeat lateness can cost Early Payout or your account ([shipping policy](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/43295059160973-Shipping-Policy-for-Sellers)) |
| eBay Live | The end of the event's shopping window, eight hours after the scheduled start | Your handling time | As for any eBay order | As for any eBay order ([eBay Live selling](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762)) |
| TikTok Shop, Ship by TikTok | Receipt of the order | Hand to the carrier within 2 working days | Carrier scan | Counts against on-time delivery; the order auto-cancels at 7 working days ([shipping policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099426562&default_language=en-GB)) |
| TikTok Shop, Ship by Seller | Receipt of the order | Delivered within 4 working days | Carrier tracking | Counts against on-time delivery rate, which can lead to late delivery fees; auto-cancels if not handed to the carrier within 7 working days |

A Friday evening show on Whatnot must be scanned by the end of Tuesday. TikTok counts working days as Monday to Friday excluding public holidays in the customer's country.

## How combined shipping works on each platform

### Whatnot: Smart Bundling

Whatnot bundles a buyer's orders from the same livestream into one package automatically. For the first item the buyer pays the cheapest package that fits; for later wins in the same show, the item goes into that package if it fits, and if a bigger package is needed the buyer is only charged the difference. Whatnot's example on the [UK buyer shipping page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/21559276628621-Shipping-for-buyers-in-the-UK) is a large letter at £2.70 becoming a small parcel at £3.39, an extra £0.69. (The same page's current price table shows £2.62 and £3.27; the example uses older figures.) In the UK the bundling works on weight and dimensions, moving up a package size when either limit is passed.

Two things you control.

- **Custom profiles with a maximum bundle size.** Set it to 1 for anything fragile or awkward so it always ships alone, as [choose shipping profiles](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407962164621-Choose-shipping-profiles-for-your-listings) describes.
- **Incremental weight.** For small items that share a mailer, set a lower weight for each additional item so a buyer who wins ten cards is not charged for ten mailers.

After the show, check each bundle on the Shipments page before printing. [Pack and ship your order](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406544597773-Pack-and-ship-your-order) explains how to bundle or unbundle and how to edit the weight and size.

### eBay Live: the shopping window and combined postage rules

Each purchase in an eBay Live event is a separate order at checkout, with its own per-order fee ([eBay Live selling fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/ebay-live-selling-fees?id=5870)). Paid orders sit as "Paid, wait to ship" for eight hours after the event's scheduled start, even if you started late, so buyers can keep adding items. When the window closes they move to Awaiting Shipment, where you can combine a buyer's orders and print one label. If you have combined postage rules and postage discounts set up in your eBay account, buyers get the discount automatically on multiple purchases during that window. Set them up before the event; there is no way to apply them after.

### TikTok Shop: auto-combined orders

TikTok's [LIVE Auction page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2301024674400022&default_language=en-GB) describes a LIVE auto-combined order feature that merges several orders from the same buyer within a set time window. Check in your Seller Centre how the window is set for your shop before the show, and tell buyers in the stream.

### Tell buyers the rule

Whatever the platform, say at the start of every show how postage works: "one postage charge per show, extra wins go in the same parcel where they fit". It is the most common question in chat, and buyers who know they have already paid postage bid on more.

## Packing 50 orders in an evening

The aim is simple: every parcel goes to the right person with the right items, weighs what the label says, and is scanned before the deadline. The method below is what sellers we know settled on after sending the wrong item once.

### Before the show

1. **Put a SKU on every listing.** On Whatnot the SKU prints on the packing slip ([create product listings](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779149424269-Create-product-listings)). A short code, such as the tray number and slot, tells you where the item is.
2. **Lay stock out in show order.** Numbered trays, boxes or card sleeves in the order you will sell them. What is sold stays in its slot until packing.
3. **Pre-make the common packs.** A card in a penny sleeve and toploader, taped into a card mailer; a folded T-shirt in a poly mailer. Do the folding and sleeving before the show, not after.
4. **Check your supplies against the stock.** Count mailers, boxes, labels and tape against the number of lots. Running out of 4 x 6 labels at order 30 costs a trip and an evening. [Packaging cheaply](/guides/packaging-cheaply) covers what to buy.

### During the show

5. **Mark each sold item with the buyer's username.** A sticky note on the item or its slot. A moderator can do this for you from the sold list while you keep selling.

### After the show

6. **Clear cancellation requests first.** On Whatnot, you cannot generate a label while a request is pending ([accept or decline a cancellation request](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9810585575693-Accept-or-decline-a-cancellation-request)).
7. **Check the bundles.** Filter the Shipments page by the show. For each buyer, check the grouped items, weight and package size against what will actually go in the parcel.
8. **Print everything in one batch.** On Whatnot, bulk labels and packing slips are on the website, not the app ([generate and print labels](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/39141311489421-Generate-and-print-shipping-labels)). Sort the export by buyer so the labels and slips come out in the same order. Labels are 4 x 6 inches for a thermal printer or 8.5 x 11 inches for a normal printer.
9. **Pick by slip, pack by slip.** Take one packing slip, pull every item on it, tick each one off, then pack. Only then take the next slip. Never pull all the items for the whole show first.
10. **Weigh before sealing.** Whatnot's own advice is to leave the package open, confirm the label, and weigh the finished parcel. If a bundle ended up heavier or thicker than the label, adjust the shipment before you post. A large letter over 2.5cm thick will not go as a large letter; our [parcel size checker](/tools/postage?tab=size) does the test.
11. **Label and seal.** Label flat, barcode fully visible, on the largest face.
12. **Scan at hand-over.** On Whatnot, the in-app scanner photographs the parcel and gives the buyer an instant update. Scan with the label attached, at the drop-off point, and show only the parcel in the photo, as the [shipping policy](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/43295059160973-Shipping-Policy-for-Sellers) asks.

### Time it

A rough target: two to three minutes per simple order once the stock is laid out and the labels are printed, longer for fragile or multi-item parcels. Fifty orders is two to three hours for one person. Time your first packing run and plan the next show around the real figure, not a hopeful one.

### Getting parcels to Royal Mail

| Option | Who it suits | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drop off at a Post Office, Royal Mail shop or delivery office | Most sellers | Get an acceptance scan or use the in-app scanner |
| Royal Mail collection booked through its app | Sellers without a business account, up to 25 Whatnot packages a day | [Shipping for sellers in the UK](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/27641952241037-Shipping-for-sellers-in-the-UK) |
| Collection with a Royal Mail Online Business Account | Sellers already posting through a business account | Tell Whatnot support your account details so Whatnot packages are included |
| Whatnot's free scheduled collections | Selected sellers posting at least 250 Whatnot packages a quarter | By invitation; collection windows are usually 30 minutes between 13:00 and 18:00 ([collections for UK sellers](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/30174350002061-Collections-on-Whatnot-for-UK-sellers)) |

On Whatnot you must use its labels. On eBay Live and TikTok with your own carrier, you buy the labels yourself; our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) covers the choice.

## When you cannot make the deadline

Illness, a printer failure or a snowed-in post office happen. On Whatnot, turn on Vacation Mode to stop new shop orders, message each affected buyer with a date, and email Whatnot's Trust and Safety team with your username, the reason, the expected ship date and the show, as [if you can't ship an order on time](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9541177911565-If-you-can-t-ship-an-order-on-time) describes. Your average ship time is a 30-day rolling figure, so one late batch recovers if the next ones are on time. On eBay and TikTok, message buyers first and use each platform's own delay tools.

Do not buy your own labels to catch up on Whatnot orders. Whatnot's UK page says it voids buyer protection and delivery problems are then charged to you.

## What goes wrong

- **Pulling everything at once.** Picking by slip is slower in theory and much faster than resending a wrong item.
- **Not checking bundles before printing.** A bundle that outgrew its package means an underpaid label.
- **Selling more than you can pack.** The show ended at 10pm on Friday; the deadline is still Tuesday.
- **Scanning from a screen or before the label is on.** Whatnot's scanner rules say the label must be on the parcel and the scan made close to hand-over.
- **Forgetting giveaway parcels.** They have the same two business day deadline and you have already paid the postage.
- **No combined postage rules on eBay.** Buyers of several lots pay postage several times and stop buying after the first.
