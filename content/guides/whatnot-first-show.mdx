This is for UK resellers who have watched a few Whatnot shows and want to run their own. After reading it you will know how the application works, how to list stock so the Royal Mail labels come out right, how to plan a first show that is short enough to manage, and what has to happen in the two business days after it. Whatnot changes its help pages often, and it changed its fee structure on 21 September 2026, so check each linked page before you rely on a figure.

## The numbers you will need

Bank account must be in the same country and currency | [Seller location and currency requirements](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061195812-Seller-location-currency-requirements) |

## Getting seller access

The application is done in the app. You tap Sell, answer a few questions about your experience and what you plan to sell, agree to the Community Guidelines, choose a primary category, and enter your legal name as it appears on your ID and the address you will post from. Whatnot says [most sellers get access within minutes](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/8442834600333-Apply-to-sell-on-Whatnot); some are asked for more information by email. A few categories, such as Luxury Bags and Accessories and Wholesale, need a separate approval after you have seller access.

Three things to decide before you apply.

- **The return address.** It is used to work out shipping and it is printed on your labels. You can change it later to a business name or a PO Box, but you cannot change it on a label once the label is generated. If you would rather not print your home address on every parcel, sort that out first.
- **The primary category.** Your show category decides which buyers see it and, under the new fee structure, which commission rate applies to live sales. Pick the one that matches most of what you will sell. Whatnot's fee page says deliberately choosing a wrong category for a lower rate is treated as fee avoidance.
- **The bank account.** It must be a UK account in pounds. A euro account or a card from an overseas fintech registered elsewhere can make payouts fail.

You can only go live while you are physically in the country you are approved to sell from. If you travel, the shows stop.

## Listing stock so the labels come out right

Every listing needs a format, a price or starting bid, and a shipping profile. The shipping profile is where new sellers lose money, so start there.

### Shipping profiles

A shipping profile tells Whatnot the packed weight and, in the UK, the size of the parcel. From it, Whatnot works out what the buyer pays, which Royal Mail service is used, and how items from one buyer are bundled. [Whatnot recommends the standard UK profiles](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/27641952241037-Shipping-for-sellers-in-the-UK) wherever one fits, and says its system adds a buffer weight for packaging on UK parcels, so you do not add the mailer yourself on a standard profile.

The UK package types and what the buyer pays for the first item are on the [buyer shipping page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/21559276628621-Shipping-for-buyers-in-the-UK).

| Package | Maximum weight | Maximum size | Buyer pays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large letter | 1kg | 35.3 x 25 x 2.5cm | £2.62 |
| Small parcel | 2kg | 45 x 35 x 16cm | £3.27 |
| Medium parcel | 2kg | 61 x 46 x 46cm | £5.03 |
| Medium parcel | 5kg | 61 x 46 x 46cm | £6.31 |
| Medium parcel | 10kg | 61 x 46 x 46cm | £6.50 |
| Medium parcel | 20kg | 61 x 46 x 46cm | £10.02 |

By default [shipping costs the seller nothing](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/27641952241037-Shipping-for-sellers-in-the-UK): the buyer pays and Whatnot gives you a prepaid label. The catch is accuracy. If the profile is too light or too small, the label is underpaid and you may have to adjust the shipment and pay the difference; if it is too heavy, buyers overpay for postage and stop bidding on your cheap lots. Weigh each item type packed, on a kitchen or parcel scale, and check its thickness against 2.5cm. A card in a toploader, a slim paperback or a folded T-shirt can go as a large letter; a boxed figure cannot. Our [parcel size checker](/tools/postage?tab=size) does the size test for you.

Use a [custom profile](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4407962164621-Choose-shipping-profiles-for-your-listings) when nothing standard fits, when an item is fragile and must travel alone (set the maximum bundle size to 1), or when many small items share one mailer and you want a lower incremental weight for each extra item.

You must use Whatnot's labels. Its UK page says buying your own label, for example a Special Delivery label, invalidates buyer protection and delivery problems are charged to you.

### Listing formats

| Format | What it does | Use it for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Auction | Buyers bid live; you set the starting bid and timer when you run it | Collectables, one-offs, anything where competition sets the price |
| Buy It Now, reserved for live | Fixed price, only buyable during the show | Multiples of the same item, fixed-price bundles, anything you will not sell below a set price |
| Buy It Now in your shop | Fixed price, buyable any time | Stock between shows |
| Giveaway | Free item, winner drawn by Whatnot, you pay the postage | Building an audience; read [giveaways and the law](/guides/giveaways-and-the-law) first |

[Create product listings](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779149424269-Create-product-listings) explains the difference between a Quality Listing (photos, description, reusable across shows) and a Temporary Listing (quick, only in that show). For a first show, use Quality Listings for everything you can. Buyers browse the show before it starts, can place pre-bids on auctions listed in advance, and a listing with a photo and an honest condition note gets bids that a line of text does not.

Fill in the SKU and cost fields. The SKU prints on the packing slip, which makes packing faster, and the cost field gives you per-item profit in the weekly orders report.

## Planning the first show

### How much stock

Sellers we know who started well kept the first show to 30 to 60 lots and 60 to 90 minutes. That is enough to learn the controls without creating more parcels than you can pack in the two business days that follow. It is easy to sell 80 orders to 30 buyers and then discover it takes an evening to pack them.

### Pricing the lots

Before the show, write the lowest price you would accept for each lot, after fees and after the cost of the mailer. Auctions on Whatnot cannot be stopped once started, there is no reserve, and the [highest bid wins](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779931101837-Start-an-auction-during-your-show). The starting bid is your floor. [Live auctions and starting bids](/guides/live-auctions-and-starting-bids) has the method for setting it.

### Running order

A simple order that works for most first shows.

| Slot | What | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First 5 to 10 minutes | Say hello, explain what is in the show, shipping and how you run auctions. Show the first few lots | People arrive late. Nothing sells well to an empty room |
| Early | A few strong lots at sensible starting bids | Gets bidding going and shows the room that things sell at fair prices |
| Middle | The bulk of the stock, alternating stronger and weaker lots | Keeps people watching for the next good one |
| Late middle | Your best lots | By now the room is at its largest and buyers have already paid shipping on earlier wins, so extra items bundle into the same parcel |
| Last 10 minutes | Remaining lots, Buy It Now multiples, thanks and the date of the next show | Tells followers when to come back |

The bundling point is real money for buyers. Whatnot's Smart Bundling puts a buyer's further wins from the same show into the parcel they already paid for, and charges only the difference if a bigger package is needed. A buyer who has won one lot is cheaper to sell to than a new one, so put the lots that appeal to the same people close together.

### Schedule it, then rehearse it

[Schedule the show](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9778927885581-Schedule-edit-and-start-a-show) from Seller Hub several days ahead. You set a name, date, time, category and primary selling format, and can add a 9:16 thumbnail, tags, moderators, muted words and show notes. Put your shipping and auction rules in the show notes. Share the link so people can set a reminder.

Then use Rehearsal Mode, which the [auction help page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779931101837-Start-an-auction-during-your-show) mentions, to practise starting auctions and pinning items. Finding the counter-bid setting for the first time with 20 people watching is avoidable.

Kit and a regular schedule are covered in [live selling kit and schedule](/guides/live-selling-kit-and-schedule).

## During the show

- **Ask a friend to moderate.** A moderator can remove disruptive users and see muted chat. You add them when scheduling or by tapping their name in chat. More on this in the kit guide.
- **Say the starting bid and the condition out loud for each lot.** What you say on stream should match the listing.
- **Watch for failed payments.** If a winner's card declines, they have until the end of the show to fix it, and [you may relist the item](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/37362462405261-Fix-a-payment-issue-or-declined-payment). If it is still unpaid when the show ends, the order is cancelled automatically.
- **Do not cancel lots that went cheap.** All bids are binding under the [Community Guidelines](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061197472-Whatnot-Community-Guidelines), and the cancellation page lists "changing your mind about the sale price" among the reasons that attract a fee. The fix for a cheap sale is a higher starting bid next time.
- **End the show properly.** Closing the app only pauses it.

## After the show

Everything sold has to receive a drop-off scan within [two business days](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/43295059160973-Shipping-Policy-for-Sellers). A Friday show must be scanned by the end of Tuesday.

1. **Open the Shipments page on whatnot.com, not the app.** The app generates one label at a time; bulk labels and packing slips are on the website ([generate and print labels](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/39141311489421-Generate-and-print-shipping-labels)).
2. **Deal with cancellation requests first.** Buyers can ask to cancel within 24 hours; you have [48 hours to accept or decline](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9810585575693-Accept-or-decline-a-cancellation-request), and you cannot generate a label while one is pending.
3. **Check the bundles.** Make sure each buyer's items are grouped correctly and the weight and size on the shipment match the real parcel.
4. **Print labels and packing slips.** Labels come as 4 x 6 inch for a thermal printer or 8.5 x 11 inch for an ordinary printer. QR code labels are not supported, so you need a printer or a print shop.
5. **Pack, label and drop off.** Scan with the in-app scanner at the drop-off point, with the label attached. Royal Mail acceptance scans count too.

Without a Royal Mail Online Business Account you can book [collections of up to 25 packages a day](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/27641952241037-Shipping-for-sellers-in-the-UK) through the Royal Mail app. Whatnot also runs [free scheduled collections](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/30174350002061-Collections-on-Whatnot-for-UK-sellers) for selected sellers who keep up at least 250 packages a quarter.

The full packing method is in [packing orders after a show](/guides/packing-orders-after-a-show).

If something stops you posting on time, turn on Vacation Mode, message the buyers, and email Whatnot's Trust and Safety team as described in [if you can't ship an order on time](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9541177911565-If-you-can-t-ship-an-order-on-time). Late shipments count against your ship time and on-time scan rate, can be refunded to the buyer at your cost, and repeated lateness can cost you Early Payout or your account.

Earnings are typically available after the carrier confirms delivery, according to [pack and ship your order](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4406544597773-Pack-and-ship-your-order). Plan your cash flow for a gap of a week or more between the show and the money.

Whatnot sales are income like any other and are reported to HMRC under the [digital platform reporting rules](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/reporting-rules-for-digital-platforms). [Bookkeeping and tax for resellers](/guides/bookkeeping-and-tax-for-resellers) covers the records. This is general information, not tax or financial advice.

## What goes wrong

- **Guessing weights.** An underweight profile gives you a shipping adjustment after the sale; an overweight one kills bidding on cheap lots.
- **Too much stock for a first show.** The show goes fine and the packing overruns the deadline.
- **Starting bids below cost.** Whatnot will not let you stop an auction or cancel for price without a fee.
- **Buying your own labels.** It voids buyer protection on that order.
- **No printer.** You cannot use a QR code at the Post Office for Whatnot labels.
- **Forgetting giveaway postage.** It is deducted from your balance when the giveaway runs, even if your balance goes negative.
- **Using the app for fulfilment.** One label at a time is fine for five orders and miserable for fifty.
