This is for Vinted sellers who want to know when the money will actually be in the bank, and what to do when it is not. After reading it you will know how the pending and available balance work, the real timeline from sale to bank for standard and Pro sellers, why withdrawals fail or get paused, and the steps to take for each problem. It describes Vinted's published rules; it is not financial advice, and Vinted's payment pages change, so check the links.

## How the money flows

When a buyer pays, the money does not go to you or to Vinted. It is held in an escrow account controlled by a regulated payment provider until the order is completed ([terms, section 9](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions)). Then the item price and any shipping the buyer paid other than prepaid Vinted shipping go to your Vinted Balance, and the Vinted fee goes to Vinted.

Your Vinted Balance has two parts ([pending balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/460-pending-balance)):

| Part | What it is | What you can do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pending balance | Money for orders not yet completed | Nothing yet. It cannot be withdrawn or spent. |
| Available balance | Money from completed orders | Withdraw to your bank, or spend on Vinted |

You cannot top up your balance; it only holds earnings ([Vinted Balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/437-vinted-balance-how-it-works)).

### You must activate your balance

You need an active Vinted Balance to sell. If you have not activated it before your first sale, you must do so within 5 calendar days of that sale, or you cannot ship and the order is cancelled ([terms, section 8](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions)). Activation asks for your real full name and address, and possibly date of birth, nationality, phone number and bank details. Use your legal name exactly as your bank has it; it matters later.

## When an order completes

**Standard sellers.** After Vinted tells the buyer the item should have been delivered (or seems lost), the buyer has 2 days to press Everything is OK or I have an issue. The order completes when they confirm, or 2 days after delivery if they do nothing ([getting paid](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/235-getting-paid-for-a-completed-sale)). If they raise an issue, the order is suspended until it is resolved.

**Pro sellers.** The buyer has 14 days to raise an issue, there is no confirmation button, and the order completes automatically 14 days after delivery. It cannot complete earlier, because of the buyer's right of withdrawal ([getting paid](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/235-getting-paid-for-a-completed-sale)).

**Then, for both:** once the order completes, the payment moves to your available balance within 2 days ([pending balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/460-pending-balance)).

## Withdrawing to your bank

From Vinted's [withdrawal page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/68-how-to-withdraw-earnings):

- Add your bank details first, then go to your Vinted Balance and press Withdraw to bank account.
- Withdrawals can take up to 5 business days (Monday to Friday, not bank holidays).
- Minimum withdrawal is £0.10.
- Pounds sterling only, to a UK account with an account number and sort code. The terms say payouts are only in your local currency and within your home country.
- You cannot use the same bank account for more than one Vinted account; withdrawals fail if you do.
- You will be asked to set up biometric authentication, or verify your phone number if your device does not support it.
- Your history (Vinted Balance, then History) shows each withdrawal, when it started and when it should arrive.

### Name checks

Some banks use Confirmation of Payee, which checks the name on the bank account against your Vinted Balance details. Vinted says that for withdrawals processed by Vinted Pay, a name mismatch may block the withdrawal, and that with Vinted Pay you can only withdraw to an account in your own name ([withdrawal issues](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/73-money-withdrawal-issues)). If your Vinted Balance name is a nickname or has a typo, contact Vinted to correct it before you need the money.

## The move to Vinted Pay

Vinted's UK wallet provider is changing to Vinted Pay Limited, an electronic money institution regulated by the Financial Conduct Authority ([terms, section 8](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions)). Vinted's [Vinted Pay page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1548) says:

- a new wallet is created and your available and pending balance, payment history and wallet details move across automatically;
- you need to accept the updated terms; if you do not, you are limited to withdrawing funds, contacting support, downloading your data and deleting your account, and accounts that never accept are eventually closed;
- a few members may be asked to complete identity checks;
- the move itself is quick, but withdrawing or spending during it can cause errors; if that happens, try again in an hour;
- you get an email when the new balance is in use.

Accept the terms promptly, and avoid starting a large withdrawal in the middle of the move. Our [summary of the October 2026 terms](/blog/vinted-terms-october-2026) covers what else changed.

## A realistic timeline, sale to bank

Adding up Vinted's published maximums gives the planning figure. Your sales will often be faster.

| Stage | Standard seller | Pro seller |
| --- | --- | --- |
| You ship | Up to 5 business days after the sale | Up to 5 business days |
| In transit | Usually days; Vinted allows up to 25 business days before treating it as lost | Same |
| Order completes | When the buyer confirms, or 2 days after delivery | 14 days after delivery |
| Moves to available | Within 2 days | Within 2 days |
| Reaches your bank | Up to 5 business days | Up to 5 business days |

So a standard sale shipped the day after purchase and delivered in two or three days can reasonably be in your bank within about two weeks of the sale, sometimes less. A Pro sale typically takes around three weeks, because of the 14 day wait after delivery. A disputed order is paused until it is resolved, and a lost parcel investigation can take several weeks ([refund policy](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465)).

Two ways to speed things up that are entirely in your hands: ship the same or next day, and politely ask buyers to press Everything is OK once they have the item.

## Watch out: your balance pays for your purchases

Vinted uses your available balance automatically as the first payment method when you buy something on Vinted ([Vinted Balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/437-vinted-balance-how-it-works)). If you buy on Vinted, your earnings may already be spent when you go to withdraw. If you keep business money separate from personal spending, withdraw before you shop.

## When something goes wrong

| Problem | Likely reason | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Money still pending days after delivery | The buyer has not confirmed and 2 days have not passed since Vinted's delivered notice; or tracking has not updated; or the buyer raised an issue | Check the order status. If the buyer has the item but tracking has not updated, ask them to press Track parcel then Mark as received ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/100)). |
| Order completed but no money in available balance | Still within the 2 days; or already spent on a purchase; or already withdrawn | Check your payment history ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/235-getting-paid-for-a-completed-sale)). |
| Withdrawal failed | Below the minimum; name mismatch; wrong format (account number must be exactly 8 digits, sort code exactly 6); account blocked by the provider; account already linked to another profile | The money stays in your balance. Fix the details and try again ([withdrawal issues](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/4/73-money-withdrawal-issues)). |
| Withdrawal not arrived after 5 business days | Bank delay or a problem at the provider | Check statements for references such as Vinted, Vinted Pay, Adyen or Mangopay, then contact Vinted with the withdrawal date and screenshots of your statement from that date, showing your name, account number and statement date. |
| Sent to the wrong or a closed account | Details entered wrongly | Withdrawals cannot be cancelled. A closed or non-existent account is usually reverted within 14 calendar days; an account that is not yours may be reverted within 15 business days. Contact your bank too. |
| Withdrawals paused, asked for ID | A Know Your Customer check by the payment provider | Complete it. Until you do, withdrawals are blocked and your balance cannot be used to pay ([why you may be asked for ID](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/111)). |
| Account blocked | Rules breach, security or fraud concern | You can usually still complete pending orders and withdraw, but payouts can be restricted where there are fraud or security concerns ([terms, section 7](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions)). Use the appeal link in the notice. See [relisting and automation rules](/guides/vinted-relisting-and-automation-rules). |

### Identity checks

The payment provider may ask for documents when you open your balance, when certain transaction or payout thresholds are reached, when there is unusual activity, or periodically for compliance ([terms, section 8](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions); [Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/111)). Vinted Pay may ask for a passport, residence permit, selfie, bank statement, driving licence or proof of address. Vinted says it does not see the documents; they go to the provider. Only upload through the official form in the app or site. Vinted says it never asks for personal details during a Buy now sale, so treat any message or email asking for them as a likely scam ([Vinted Balance](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/437-vinted-balance-how-it-works)).

## Records and tax

Your Vinted Balance history shows each sale and withdrawal by year. Download or note it monthly; it is the record you will reconcile against your bank and your stock list. Vinted reports sellers above the platform thresholds to HMRC each January for the calendar year, which does not line up with the tax year; see [HMRC platform reporting](/blog/hmrc-platform-reporting-rules) and [bookkeeping and tax for resellers](/guides/bookkeeping-and-tax-for-resellers). If you buy stock to resell, Vinted's rules say you need a Pro account, which changes your payout timing; see [Vinted Pro and selling as a business](/guides/vinted-pro-and-selling-as-a-business).

## What goes wrong

- **Not activating the balance within 5 days of the first sale.** The order is cancelled.
- **A Vinted Balance name that does not match the bank.** Withdrawals can be blocked.
- **One bank account on two Vinted accounts.** Withdrawals fail.
- **Expecting Pro money in days.** Pro orders complete 14 days after delivery.
- **Shopping on Vinted before withdrawing.** Your earnings pay for it automatically.
- **Ignoring the updated terms.** Your account is limited until you accept.
- **Leaving an ID check unanswered.** Withdrawals stay blocked.
- **Planning cash flow on best cases.** Use the maximums in the table and treat faster payouts as a bonus.
