For sellers who pick up second-hand books and want to sell them on Amazon without losing the margin to fees, postage or complaints. After reading you will be able to attach a used book to the right listing, grade it against Amazon's condition rules, work out what a sale pays after the fixed closing fee, and choose between FBA and posting it yourself. It assumes the basics from [the working manual](/guides/how-to-start-amazon-fba-uk).

Fees come from Amazon's [July 2026 rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf) and [UK pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing), which quote them excluding VAT. They change at least yearly; check before relying on a figure.

## How a used book sale works on Amazon

Nearly every book with an ISBN already has a detail page, and you are adding one more offer to it. What you control is the condition grade, a short condition note, the price and the fulfilment method. Buyers compare offers on those four things and little else, so the work is in buying the right titles, grading them accurately and knowing your costs to the penny.

## Listing against the ISBN

### Find the right edition

Search by the ISBN on the back cover or copyright page, not by the title. A popular novel can have a dozen editions (hardback, paperback, large print, film tie-in, book club), each a different detail page with a different price and sales rank. The ISBN takes you to the one in your hand.

Older ten digit ISBNs search the same way. Books with no ISBN sometimes still have a detail page, found by title and publisher; check the cover, publisher and page count before attaching. If nothing matches, creating a new page is slow and it will have no sales history, so for a cheap book it is rarely worth it. The general steps for attaching an offer are in [adding products and creating shipments](/guides/amazon-adding-products-and-creating-shipments#find-the-exact-listing).

### One SKU per copy, or per grade

A used book is graded individually, so two copies of the same title in different condition are two SKUs. Amazon's [condition guidelines](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/rainier/help/legal/Condition_Guidelines_EN_161220.pdf) state that once you have chosen the condition of a listing you cannot change it later. If you graded a copy wrongly, close that offer and list it again under a new SKU with the right grade.

### The condition note

Facts, not sales talk: a name on the flyleaf, a creased spine, pencil notes in one chapter. A short, specific note heads off "not as described" returns.

## Amazon's condition grades for used books

The downloadable [condition guidelines document](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/rainier/help/legal/Condition_Guidelines_EN_161220.pdf) is dated 2016; the live version is in Seller Central under Help, [condition guidelines](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/G200339950?locale=en-GB). Read the live page before you rely on the wording below.

| Grade | What Amazon allows |
| --- | --- |
| Used, Like New | Apparently unread, perfect condition. Dust jacket intact with no nicks or tears, no spine creasing, clean pages with no notes or folds. A remainder mark on an outside edge is allowed but must be noted. Any CD, DVD or access code must be present and unredeemed |
| Used, Very Good | Read, but in excellent condition. Pages intact with no notes or highlighting, spine undamaged. Any CD, DVD or access code must be present and unredeemed |
| Used, Good | Read, but clean. All pages and the cover intact, including the dust jacket if there was one. Spine may show wear. Limited notes and highlighting allowed, and "From the library of" labels. Access codes may be missing or used |
| Used, Acceptable | A readable copy. All pages and the cover intact, but the dust jacket may be missing. Considerable notes in pen or highlighter allowed, as long as they do not obscure the text. Access codes may be missing or used |

### What must always be disclosed

The same document says a remainder mark should always be noted in the comments, and strongly encourages noting any obvious cosmetic flaw. Read with the grades, that means noting: remainder marks at any grade; a missing dust jacket (which also rules out Good and above); notes and highlighting, and how much; library stamps, labels and pockets; a missing or used access code, CD or DVD; and inscriptions, which a gift buyer wants to know about.

Amazon's guidelines give ex-library books no grade of their own. Describe the markings and grade on everything else. In practice a book with a library pocket and stamped page edges will not satisfy a buyer who ordered Very Good.

### What cannot be sold at all

Amazon will not accept mouldy, badly stained or unclean copies, books with missing pages or text that cannot be read, promotional copies including advance reading copies, or uncorrected proofs ([condition guidelines](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/rainier/help/legal/Condition_Guidelines_EN_161220.pdf)). Books marked "Bargain" or "Remainder", or with any markings or labels, cannot be listed as New, and listing a used book as New is prohibited. Boxes from clearances often contain proofs and review copies; put them aside before you scan.

### Collectible grades

Signed, limited, first edition, scarce or out of print copies can be listed as Collectible, with a note on why. The same document excludes former library books, remaindered books, book club editions and proofs of in-print books, and says Good or Acceptable copies are generally not collectible grade. If Amazon asks for approval, see [ungating](/guides/amazon-ungating-uk). Check sold prices on other platforms first; a valuable book often does better with a specialist audience.

## What a sale actually pays

### The fees on every book

| Fee | Amount | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Referral fee, Books | 15% of the total price, including any delivery charge the buyer pays | [UK pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing), [rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf) |
| Minimum referral fee, Books | Not applicable | [UK pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) |
| Closing fee, per media item sold | £0.50 | [rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf), referral fee notes |
| Individual plan, per item sold | £0.75 | [UK pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) |
| FBM delivery charge on a book, UK standard | £2.80, set by Amazon and passed to you as a shipping credit | [UK pricing page](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing), shipping credits table |

Media means Books, Music, DVD, Video, Video Games and Software. The closing fee is flat: 50p on a £1 book and on a £40 one, which is why it decides which books are worth listing.

The delivery charge on books is not yours to set. Amazon's pricing page says it charges buyers fixed rates on books, music, video and DVD for Individual and Professional sellers alike, passes the same amount to you as a credit, and takes the referral fee on the item price plus that credit.

### Worked numbers, FBM

Professional plan, posted from home by Royal Mail 2nd Class large letter bought online. The [October 2026 online price guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf) keeps a 2nd Class large letter at £1.90 up to 250g and £2.70 up to 750g (our [summary of the October prices](/blog/royal-mail-prices-october-2026)).

| | 1p book | £3.99 paperback | £12 hardback |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer pays | £2.81 | £6.79 | £14.80 |
| Referral fee (15%) | £0.42 | £1.02 | £2.22 |
| Closing fee | £0.50 | £0.50 | £0.50 |
| Amazon pays you | £1.89 | £5.27 | £12.08 |
| Postage | £1.90 (up to 250g) | £1.90 (up to 250g) | £2.70 (up to 750g) |
| Left before the mailer and the book | minus £0.01 | £3.37 | £9.38 |

On the Individual plan, take another 75p off each sale.

### Worked numbers, FBA

FBA fees depend on size and weight. Books priced at £10 or less (including VAT) qualify for Low-Price FBA rates; above that, standard rates apply ([rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf), Low-Price FBA and standard fulfilment pages). A 1.5% fuel and logistics surcharge has been added to fulfilment fees since 17 April 2026 ([our breakdown](/blog/amazon-fba-fuel-logistics-surcharge)); it is included below, rounded to the penny.

| | £3.99 paperback, standard envelope up to 210g | £12 paperback, standard envelope up to 460g |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fulfilment fee | £1.73 Low-Price FBA, £1.76 with surcharge | £2.16 standard, £2.19 with surcharge |
| Referral fee (15%) | £0.60 | £1.80 |
| Closing fee | £0.50 | £0.50 |
| Left before storage, shipping in, labels and the book | £1.13 | £7.51 |

A standard envelope is up to 33 x 23 x 2.5 cm. A thick hardback up to 4 cm and 960g is a large envelope at £2.72, or £2.79 in the festive peak from 15 October 2026 to 14 January 2027; standard envelope rates do not change in the peak. Check the real ASIN in Amazon's [revenue calculator](https://sell.amazon.co.uk/pricing) and measure with the [parcel size checker](/tools/postage?tab=size).

### The penny book problem

The FBM table shows why so many books sit at 1p. That seller is pricing the delivery credit, not the book. On these figures a light 1p book barely breaks even before the mailer, and anything over 250g loses money. It only works at volume, with near-free stock and discounted postage contracts, which is a different business from buying a box at a car boot.

**If the lowest used offer in your grade is a few pence, do not list it.** Matching it pays you to post books. Sell those titles in a mixed lot elsewhere or leave them on the shelf. The same applies to FBA at the bottom end: at £3.99 the flat fees take over half the price before the book has cost you anything.

## Pricing a used book

### Start from the offers in your grade

Filter the offers to used and compare yourself with the same or better grade and the same fulfilment method. A Good FBA copy is not competing with an Acceptable FBM copy at 1p; a buyer who wants Prime or a clean copy pays more. Price against the lowest offer a buyer choosing yours would realistically consider.

### Sales rank as a speed signal

Amazon's [help page](https://www.amazon.co.uk/gp/help/customer/display.html?nodeId=GGGMZK378RQPATDJ) says Best Sellers Rank is based on Amazon sales and updated hourly to reflect recent and historical sales. It tells you speed, not price, and nothing about how many sellers share those sales.

- One day's rank is noise. A single sale lifts a book, then it drifts back.
- A rank history, from any price and rank history tool, shows how often it really sells. Count the jumps over three to six months.
- Divide by the number of used offers. Weekly sales shared by twenty sellers are not a weekly sale for you.
- Set texts, revision guides and Christmas cookbooks move in waves. Buy for the wave, not after it.

Amazon does not publish what a rank means in sales, so learn it from your own sales. A fast title at a thin margin often beats a slow one at a fat margin, because the money comes back to buy more stock.

## Storage drag on slow titles

A paperback of about 20 x 13 x 2.5 cm is roughly 0.023 cubic feet. At Amazon's UK standard size storage rates of £0.76 per cubic foot per month from January to September and £1.51 from October to December ([rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf), monthly storage), that is under 2p a month for most of the year and about 3.5p in the last quarter. Storage alone rarely kills a book.

The aged inventory surcharge does. From 366 days the UK charge is the greater of a cubic foot rate or £0.20 per unit a month, rising to £0.25 after 455 days ([rate card](https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/02/sell/images/260630-FBA-Rate-Card-EN1.pdf)). For a small book the per unit amount is always greater, so a year-old paperback costs 20p a month on top. On a £3.99 book leaving £1.13 after fees, a few months of that is the profit gone. The earlier bands and how to remove stock before it is charged are in [inventory health](/guides/amazon-inventory-health#aged-inventory-surcharge).

The rule: reprice or remove any FBA book approaching nine months, and do not send in books you expect to take more than a few months to sell.

## FBA or FBM for books

| Factor | Favours FBA | Favours FBM |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | £10 and up, where the fees are a smaller share | Under about £5, where every fee matters |
| Speed of sale | Sells within a few months | Slow or occasional sellers |
| Competition | Most offers are FBM, so Prime stands out | Plenty of Prime offers already listed |
| Size | Fits an envelope tier | Heavy or oversized, where FBA tiers rise fast |
| Your time | Want to post nothing day to day | Already posting daily for other platforms |
| Value | Ordinary copies | Collectible copies you want to pack yourself |

**Our recommendation:** FBA for books priced at £10 or more that sell within a few months, where the Prime badge earns a better price than the FBM offers around it. FBM for everything else you choose to list, and nothing at all for books where the lowest offer is pennies.

Two cautions on FBM. First, Amazon holds you to its delivery metrics, including a [valid tracking rate](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/G201817070?locale=en-GB) of 95%. Untracked large letters are only exempt when bought through Buy Shipping, and a Tracked 48 large letter at £2.90 online from 5 October 2026 ([Royal Mail online price guide](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf)) costs more than the £2.80 credit. Check the Buy Shipping rate for your weight before working out any margin; the metrics are in [FBM: when it makes sense](/guides/amazon-fbm-when-it-makes-sense). Second, the credit does not rise with weight, so weigh before you list.

For FBA, every unit needs an Amazon barcode label ([FBA barcode requirements](https://sellercentral.amazon.co.uk/help/hub/reference/external/G201100910?locale=en-GB)), covering the ISBN barcode, with the removable adhesive Amazon requires so the cover survives; the label rules are in [adding products and creating shipments](/guides/amazon-adding-products-and-creating-shipments).

## Where used books come from

The sources are covered in detail in [sourcing for resale](/guides/sourcing-for-resale). For books specifically:

| Source type | What to expect | What to watch |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Charity shops | Steady supply on a weekly route | Mostly common titles; scan, do not guess |
| Car boots and jumble sales | Cheapest per book, very mixed | Damp, smoke and proofs |
| House clearances and estate lots | Whole collections, sometimes specialist | Boxes of unsellable books to dispose of |
| Library and institutional sales | Cheap non-fiction and reference | Library markings to describe |
| Bulk lots | Low cost per unit | You buy the average, not the best |

Scan every book against the live offers before you pay, and keep receipts, even for 50p.

## VAT

Books are zero-rated for VAT in the UK ([VAT Notice 701/10](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/zero-rating-books-and-printed-matter-for-vat-notice-70110)). If you are VAT registered, set the right product tax code on every book SKU so Amazon does not charge 20% on a zero-rated sale; how to do that, and the traps with items sold alongside a book, are in [VAT, invoices and business accounts](/guides/amazon-vat-invoices-and-business-accounts#listing-zero-rated-and-reduced-rate-products-correctly). This is general information, not tax advice.

## What goes wrong

- **Listing against the wrong edition.** Same title, different ISBN. A return follows.
- **Grading up.** A Good copy sold as Very Good is a refund and possibly negative feedback. Grade down when unsure.
- **Undisclosed faults.** A missing dust jacket or a used access code, not mentioned in the note.
- **Matching 1p.** You pay to post someone else's book.
- **Forgetting the closing fee.** 50p on every sale, whatever the price.
- **Heavy books on FBM.** The £2.80 credit does not move with weight; your postage does.
- **Slow titles in FBA.** Pennies a month at first, then at least 20p a month on top after a year.
- **Selling proofs and review copies.** Prohibited. Take them out at the sorting stage.
