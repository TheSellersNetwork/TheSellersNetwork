This is for UK TikTok Shop sellers who look at a payout and cannot tell how it was arrived at. After reading it you will know where statements live, what each line means, which settlement period you are on and how to move to a faster one, and a monthly routine for getting TikTok's figures into your books. Rules link to TikTok's Seller Academy. They change, so check the page before relying on it. This is general information about bookkeeping, not tax, legal or financial advice.

## When you are paid

TikTok pays for an order after it is delivered, not when it is placed. How long after depends on your settlement period ([Payment Settlement Period](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099475714&default_language=en-GB)).

| Period | Paid | Who is on it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Introductory | 31 days after delivery | New shops and shops in probation |
| Standard | 8 days after delivery | Most established shops |
| Accelerated | 3 days after delivery | Shops with a Shop Performance Score of 3.0 to below 3.5, a minimum sales level, out of probation |
| Express | 1 day after delivery | Shops with a score of 3.5 or more, a higher sales level, out of probation |
| Deferred | 31 days after delivery | Shops under security review |

Eligibility is reviewed monthly on the 1st, and you can check yours on the Shop Health page in Seller Center. The sales levels for accelerated and express are on the [settlement period page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099475714&default_language=en-GB). Platform-funded shipping subsidies are paid within 3 days whatever your period.

After the period ends, if your balance is positive and your bank details are valid, TikTok starts the transfer, and it usually arrives within 3 business days ([Settlement on TikTok Shop](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5082281622406945)).

### What it means for cash flow

A new shop waits 31 days after delivery, plus delivery time, plus bank time. Stock you buy in week one may not be paid for by TikTok until week six or seven. Plan for that before you scale. Getting out of probation and keeping your [Shop Performance Score](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2437046845359874) up is what shortens the wait, which is one more reason to ship on time. Our [account health guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-account-health-and-restricted-products) covers both.

### When money is held back

- **Reserve.** Shops that fail the seller-fault cancellation rate criteria can have funds held for 30 calendar days after delivery to cover refunds and disputes ([Settlement on TikTok Shop](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5082281622406945)). It shows as on-hold in the Finance module.
- **Account Health milestones.** A low Account Health Rating suspends withdrawals for 7, 14 or 30 days depending on the score ([AHR](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3374697174435586)).
- **Investigations.** TikTok can withhold funds for up to 90 calendar days, or until an investigation ends ([Seller Performance Evaluation Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408274689)).
- **Negative balance.** If fees exceed revenue, your balance goes below zero. TikTok's [Negative Balance page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5286902741042976) lists escalating actions the longer it stays negative, from a warning and a longer settlement period to frozen withdrawals and, eventually, deactivation. Common causes it names: wrong prices, wrong parcel weights, promotion mistakes, too many creator samples and high refund rates. You can pay it off in the Finance module.

## Reading a statement

Go to Seller Center, Finance, Statements. You can view by statement or by order ([Finance Report Guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522744578)). Each statement shows the statement date, an ID, the number of orders and adjustments, the settlement amount, a status (pending, processing, paid or failed) and a payout ID.

The statement is a sum: revenue, less fees, plus or minus adjustments, equals settlement.

### Revenue lines

| Line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| Gross sales | Item prices before discounts |
| Seller discounts | Discounts you funded; these reduce your revenue |
| Net sales | What the buyer paid for the items after your discounts and refunds |
| Customer-paid shipping fee | The shipping the buyer paid |
| Platform discounts | Discounts TikTok funded; you are compensated for these, and commission is still charged as if they were paid |

### Fee lines

| Line | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok Shop commission | The 9% (or reduced) platform commission, including VAT, on net sales plus shipping plus platform discount, less refunds |
| Affiliate commission | What creators earned on orders they referred; see [affiliates and samples](/guides/tiktok-shop-affiliates-and-samples) |
| Affiliate partner commission | Payments to affiliate partner agencies, if you use them |
| TikTok Shop shipping fee | The cost of platform shipping labels |
| FBT shipping fee | Shipping charged under Fulfilled by TikTok |

The names come from TikTok's [Finance Report Guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522744578) and [seller fees page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522154754&lang=en-GB); your statement may group or name them slightly differently.

### Adjustments

Adjustments sit outside individual orders: logistics adjustments (for example a weight correction), reimbursements (for example a lost parcel TikTok pays you for), deductions for policy violations, chargebacks and promotion adjustments. Read every one. An unexplained deduction is worth a support ticket.

A single order can show a negative settlement. TikTok explains this happens when the order's fees exceed its revenue, typically when the shipping cost is more than the buyer paid. If you see several, your shipping charges are too low.

### Checking one order

Pick one order and work it through by hand once. Take net sales plus customer-paid shipping, apply 9% (or your rate), and compare with the commission line. Take the creator's rate on the product price after discounts and compare with the affiliate line. Add the shipping label cost. If the settlement matches, you understand the statement; if not, you have found either a mistake or a line you did not know about.

## Exporting for your records

Statements export to a spreadsheet with tabs for order details, statements, reports, payments, reserve and fee explanations ([Finance Report Guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522744578)). Export monthly and keep the files. TikTok's fee invoices are separate: download them from Finance, Invoices. They are usually issued around the 7th of each month; platform fees are invoiced by TikTok Information Technologies UK Limited and logistics fees by Perceiver Limited ([Check Tax Invoices](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=6837879071016706&lang=en-GB)). Make sure your tax details are complete in Seller Center, or the invoices will be incomplete.

## Reconciling TikTok for your books

The common mistake is recording the payout as your income. It is not: the payout is sales less fees. HMRC wants your income and your expenses shown properly, and TikTok sends HMRC figures about your sales under the [platform reporting rules](/blog/hmrc-platform-reporting-rules), so your records need gross sales and fees shown separately, not just the net payout.

A monthly routine:

1. **Export the month's statements** and the order detail.
2. **Record sales** as the total the buyers paid for items and shipping (net sales plus customer-paid shipping). Platform-funded discounts are paid to you by TikTok, so include them in income.
3. **Record each fee type as an expense**: commission, affiliate commission, shipping labels, FBT fees, chargeback fees, violation deductions. Keep them separate; you will want to see what affiliates cost you.
4. **Record refunds** against sales, not as an expense.
5. **Match payouts to the bank.** Each payout ID on a statement should match a deposit. The difference between sales less fees and money received is timing (orders delivered but not yet settled) or money held (reserve, negative balance).
6. **File the fee invoices** with the month's records.
7. **Track what is owed to you**: delivered orders not yet paid, and reserve. On a 31 day period that can be a large figure at a year end.

A spreadsheet with one row per statement and a column per line type is enough for most resellers. ### VAT

TikTok says its 9% commission is inclusive of applicable taxes including VAT ([seller fees](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522154754&lang=en-GB)). If you are not VAT registered, the whole fee is your cost. If you are registered, the invoices show the VAT separately, and whether and how you reclaim it, and how your own sales are treated under your scheme (including the margin scheme for second-hand goods), is a question for your accountant. Do not guess from the statement.

## What goes wrong

- **Recording payouts as income.** Understates sales and hides the fees.
- **Not budgeting for the introductory period.** A month or more with no money in.
- **Ignoring adjustments.** Weight corrections and violation deductions add up.
- **Undercharging shipping.** Negative settlements on individual orders.
- **Letting samples run away.** The balance goes negative and payouts stop.
- **Not downloading invoices.** You need them for your records and for any VAT reclaim.
- **Forgetting money owed at the year end.** Delivered orders not yet settled are still your sales.
