This is for UK TikTok Shop sellers who want to know what can close the shop and how to stay well clear of it. After reading it you will understand the two scores TikTok uses, the violations that most often catch resellers, and the product rules: what needs documents, what needs an invitation, and what is banned outright. Every rule links to TikTok's own Seller Academy page. TikTok revises these policies frequently, so check the page before relying on it.

## Two scores, two jobs

TikTok measures a shop in two ways, and resellers often confuse them.

| | Account Health Rating (AHR) | Shop Performance Score (SPS) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| What it measures | Policy compliance: violations cost points | Service quality compared with similar shops |
| Scale | 0 to 1,000 | 0 to 5 |
| What a bad score does | Withdrawal suspensions, then deactivation | Loss of access to programmes such as auctions, affiliates and campaigns, and slower settlement |
| Where to find it | Account Health in Seller Center | Shop Health in Seller Center |

### The Account Health Rating

TikTok's [AHR page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3374697174435586) (last updated 23 January 2026) replaced the older milestone system for UK sellers. Every new seller starts at 200 points, which is the bottom of the healthy zone. Violations take points off according to severity and frequency. Points count for 180 days and then drop off.

The milestones are where it bites. At 150 points your withdrawals are suspended for 7 days; at 100, for 14 days; at 50, for 30 days and you cannot trade; at 0, the shop is permanently deactivated after a 30 day withdrawal suspension.

The important thing to notice is that you start at 200 with no cushion. One serious violation in your first month can take you straight into the at-risk zone. You earn points back by fulfilling orders perfectly: 4 points per 200 completed orders, capped at 20 points a week. Orders that were late, returned, refunded, cancelled or defective, and sample orders, do not count. For a small reseller doing a few hundred orders a quarter, that is a slow climb. The practical lesson is that avoiding violations is far easier than earning your way out of them.

You cannot appeal an AHR enforcement itself, but you can appeal the violation that caused it. A successful appeal removes the enforcement and restores the points ([AHR](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3374697174435586)). The [Seller Performance Evaluation Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408274689) says you are notified by email and in Seller Center and can submit one appeal within the stated timeframe. Make that one appeal count: evidence, not argument.

### The Shop Performance Score

The [SPS guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2437046845359874) (effective 22 July 2026) scores you from 0 to 5 once you have at least 30 delivered orders in the last 60 days. It combines three areas, weighted by category:

- **Product satisfaction:** negative review rate and seller-fault return or refund rate.
- **Fulfilment and logistics:** seller-fault cancellation rate and on-time delivery rate.
- **Customer service:** dissatisfaction with your messages and how long you take to handle after-sales requests.

Each metric is scored against other sellers in your subcategory, so a score is relative. TikTok treats 4 or more as excellent. A low score restricts access to auctions, affiliate marketing, campaigns and co-funded returns support. SPS also feeds into how quickly you are paid: see the [statements and settlements guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-statements-and-settlements).

## The violations that catch resellers

The AHR page groups violations into product compliance, product safety, listing quality, intellectual property, fair trading, fulfilment, after-sales, account management and review manipulation. For resellers, these are the ones that come up again and again.

| Violation | How resellers trip it | How to avoid it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Selling used goods without approval | Listing second-hand stock on a shop not invited into the second-hand category | Apply for the invite first; list nothing used until approved |
| Condition misrepresented | Used or open-box items listed as new; flaws not shown | Put the condition in the title where required; photograph every flaw |
| Restricted product without documents | Toys without UKCA or CE mark images; electricals without manuals; beauty without ingredient images | Upload the documents the category asks for before listing |
| Intellectual property | Fakes, unbranded items listed under a brand name, brand logos in the shop name | Sell only what you can prove is genuine; see [spotting fakes](/guides/spotting-fakes-before-you-buy) |
| Off-platform selling | Telling buyers to message you elsewhere, showing a phone number or social handle in a video | Keep every sale and refund inside TikTok Shop |
| Late or no dispatch | Taking orders you cannot ship in time; stock sold elsewhere | Keep quantities accurate; ship within the stated time |
| Seller-fault cancellations | Cancelling because the item sold on another platform | Sync stock across channels or keep separate stock |
| Misleading content | Claims you cannot support, showing a product in a video that is not the one listed | Make only claims you can prove; show the actual item |
| Review manipulation | Asking friends for reviews, offering refunds for good reviews | Never, in any form |

The off-platform rules are strict. The [content policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8913678280345345) (last updated 1 September 2026) bans links or references to other ways of ordering, QR codes, and any call to action that encourages customers to leave TikTok Shop. Resellers who cross-list sometimes say "also on my other shop" in a video. Do not.

The fulfilment rules are in our [shipping and returns guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-shipping-and-returns).

## Products: restricted, invite-only and prohibited

TikTok splits products into three bands. Know which band your stock is in before you list it.

### Restricted: allowed with documents

The [Restricted Products Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753822475273986&lang=en-GB) (last updated 27 August 2026) lists categories you can sell if you provide supporting documents and product attributes. Those most relevant to resellers:

| Category | What you need |
| --- | --- |
| Toys (including figurines, board games, soft toys) | UKCA or CE marking images and age suitability information |
| Electrical products and electronics | UKCA or CE marking images, user manual, warranty and safety information |
| Beauty products | Packaging images showing ingredients, country of origin and usage instructions |
| Media (CDs, DVDs, vinyl, VHS, cassettes) | Front and back cover images; DVDs and Blu-rays limited to age rating 12 and under |
| Dangerous goods (batteries, aerosols, flammable liquids) | Safety Data Sheet and product attributes |
| Surprise-based products (mystery boxes, trading card packs) | Contents shown clearly with quantities; total retail value stated; listed under £100; not marketed as gambling |
| Safety workwear | Manufacturer details, CE or UKCA marks, Declaration of Conformity |

The media rule matters to resellers of DVDs: anything rated 15 or 18 is out. The toy rule matters to anyone selling used or boxed toys: without the marking images, the listing is a violation even if the toy is perfectly safe.

### Invite-only: you must apply

The same policy lists categories that need an application and approval. For resellers the big ones are:

- **All second-hand products**, with caps of £500 per listing for second-hand fashion and £1,000 for graded trading cards. Our [getting started guide](/guides/tiktok-shop-getting-started-uk) covers the second-hand rules in detail.
- **Jewellery and watches** in precious metals and diamonds, which need business details and test reports.
- **Coins and bullion**, which need a registered UK business and a compliance record; graded coins must use named grading services.
- **Collectable plushies**, which must carry a warning that they are not toys and are unsuitable for children under 14.
- **Pre-packaged food, drinks and supplements**, with detailed labelling rules.
- **Power tools, e-bikes and lawnmowers**, each with strict technical requirements.

### Prohibited: never

The [Prohibited Products Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408307457) (last updated 25 August 2026) bans these outright. The categories that surprise resellers:

- **Bladed products**: knives, scissors and sharply pointed tools. That includes vintage pocket knives and craft scissors.
- **Financial products**: currency, cryptocurrency and financial instruments. Banknote collections fall here.
- **Government and military items**: official uniforms, documents and replicas. Some militaria collections are caught.
- **Cultural property and archaeological items.**
- **Single-use plastics** such as straws and cutlery.
- **Protected wildlife**: anything from endangered species, which catches some antiques.
- **Adult products, drugs and paraphernalia, weapons, gambling, hazardous materials** and the other categories you would expect.

If you are not sure which band an item is in, do not list it until you have checked the policy and, where needed, asked support. A removed listing is a violation; an unasked question is not.

## Keeping the shop healthy: a weekly routine

1. **Check Account Health** in Seller Center. Read any new violation the day it appears. If it is wrong, appeal with evidence while the facts are fresh.
2. **Check Shop Health** for SPS and the individual metrics. A slipping on-time delivery rate shows up here before it costs you.
3. **Review listings added this week** against the restricted products policy: documents uploaded, condition clear, nothing prohibited.
4. **Reconcile stock across channels.** Every cancellation because an item sold elsewhere is a seller-fault cancellation.
5. **Answer messages and after-sales requests** within the required times. Slow handling feeds the customer service score.

## What goes wrong

- **Treating 200 points as safe.** It is the starting line with no margin.
- **Listing used stock before the invite.** The most common reseller violation.
- **Toys and electricals without marking images.** Legal to sell, a violation to list without the documents.
- **Selling 15 and 18 rated films.** Not allowed on TikTok Shop UK.
- **Mentioning another platform in a video.** Off-platform promotion.
- **Cancelling because the item sold elsewhere.** A seller-fault cancellation every time.
- **Appealing with anger instead of evidence.** You usually get one chance.
- **Ignoring a small violation.** Points last 180 days, and they stack.
