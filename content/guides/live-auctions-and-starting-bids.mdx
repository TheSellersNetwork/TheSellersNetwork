This is for UK sellers running live shows who want to stop selling good stock for pennies at the end of a quiet auction. After reading it you will know when an auction is the right format and when a fixed price is, how each platform's auction timer actually behaves, and how to work out a starting bid that covers your cost, fees and packaging whatever happens. Timer rules and fees come from each platform's own pages and change, so check the linked page before relying on them.

## The rule that matters most

On every live platform, a started auction is a sale at the highest bid. Whatnot says an auction [cannot be cancelled or stopped once it starts](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779931101837-Start-an-auction-during-your-show), and its [cancellation page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061603971-Cancel-an-order-before-you-ship) lists "changing your mind about the sale price" among the reasons that attract a seller cancellation fee. eBay Live listings [cannot use a reserve price](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762). In practice, the starting bid is the only price protection you have. Set it as the lowest price you are genuinely happy to take, never lower in the hope that bidding will run.

## Auction or Buy It Now

| Stock | Format | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| One-offs, collectables, anything where the right price is uncertain | Auction | Competition finds the price, and live bidding is what keeps viewers watching |
| Items with a steady, known market price and plenty of sold comps | Buy It Now, reserved for the show | An auction can only match the market or undershoot it. A fixed price holds the value |
| Multiples of the same item | Buy It Now with a quantity | Ten auctions of the same thing teach the room to wait for the tenth |
| Cheap items under a few pounds | Bundle them into one lot, or Buy It Now | Fixed per-order fees take a large share of small sales |
| Your best lot of the night | Auction, late in the show, at a starting bid you are happy with | The room is at its biggest and buyers have already paid postage on earlier wins |

Most shows that work well are mostly auctions with a few fixed-price lines. Use the pricing method in [pricing from sold comps](/guides/pricing-from-sold-comps) to decide which items have a known price.

## How the auction types work on each platform

### Whatnot

When you start an auction you set three things, according to [start an auction during your show](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9779931101837-Start-an-auction-during-your-show).

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Starting bid | The opening price. Whatnot's own advice is to make it the lowest amount you are comfortable accepting |
| Auction time | The minimum length. Bids can extend it |
| Counter bid time | When fewer than 10 seconds remain, each new bid resets the timer to this amount |
| Sudden death (optional) | Ends the auction at 00:01 to the final bidder, with no extension |

A standard Whatnot auction therefore keeps going while bids keep coming in the last few seconds. Sudden death stops that: whoever is highest when the clock runs out wins. Whatnot notes that many auctions last 30 seconds or less.

If you list an auction before the show, buyers can pre-bid, and those bids are applied when the auction starts. Pre-bids help on strong lots. They are also the tool disruptive bidders use: a single account can place high pre-bids on everything and let the payments fail. Whatnot lets you [disable pre-bids](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/32239228636557-Dealing-with-Disruptive-Users-in-your-show) on the web version of the show settings, and restrict the show to verified buyers.

### eBay Live

eBay Live auctions are set up in Live Studio and run from the Stream manager. The [eBay Live selling page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762) describes the controls.

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Starting price and duration | Editable before and during the event. For an auction under one minute, enter 0 in the minutes field |
| Extend auction | If a new bid arrives in the final 5 seconds, the auction extends by 5 seconds |
| Sudden death | A toggle that starts a countdown timer for final bids |
| Best Offer and reserve | Not allowed on eBay Live listings |

To auction a Buy It Now listing from your eBay shop, you have to revise it to auction format in Seller Hub first. Each event can hold up to 750 listings.

### TikTok Shop LIVE

TikTok's [LIVE Auction page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2301024674400022&default_language=en-GB) says the feature is available for selected product categories, confirmed through your account manager. It is an English auction (highest bid wins), your starting bid must be at least £1, and TikTok recommends timers of 15 to 30 seconds. Buyers need an active payment card on file before they can bid. You can re-run an auction that ends with no bids. The page mentions setting a time extension on temporary auction listings but does not describe a sudden death option or how the extension behaves, so test it in a short show before relying on it.

## Setting a starting bid that does not lose money

### Step 1: work out what you need back

For each lot, add up:

- what the item cost you;
- the packaging for that item (mailer, toploader, bubble wrap, tape);
- the smallest profit you are willing to accept for the time it takes.

Call this the amount you need. If you do not know the cost, because it came from a job lot, divide the job lot price by the number of sellable items.

### Step 2: add the fees back on

Fees are partly a percentage of the price and partly fixed. The starting bid that leaves you exactly the amount you need is:

starting bid = (amount you need + fixed costs) ÷ (1 minus the percentage taken from the price)

For the three platforms, using the fees on their UK pages for a seller who is not VAT registered, in a standard category:

| Platform | Assumptions | Formula |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Whatnot | 6.67% commission plus VAT on the item; 2.42% plus £0.25 processing plus VAT on item and postage; buyer pays £3.27 postage for a small parcel and Whatnot supplies the label ([fees](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/4847069165965-Whatnot-seller-fees), [UK buyer shipping](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/21559276628621-Shipping-for-buyers-in-the-UK)) | (amount you need + £0.40) ÷ 0.891 |
| eBay Live | 8.42% of item plus postage, plus £0.30 per order, fees as shown before any tax; you charge £3.75 postage and pay £3.75 for a Tracked 48 small parcel label ([eBay Live fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/ebay-live-selling-fees?id=5870), [Royal Mail online prices](https://www.royalmail.com/sites/royalmail.com/files/2026-09/online-price-guide-october-2026-v1-ta.pdf)) | (amount you need + £0.62) ÷ 0.916 |
| TikTok Shop | 9% including VAT on item plus postage; you charge £3.75 postage and pay £3.75 for the label ([commission fee](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753824408913665&default_language=en-GB)) | (amount you need + £0.34) ÷ 0.91 |

Worked through:

| Amount you need | Whatnot | eBay Live | TikTok Shop |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| £3 | £3.81 | £3.95 | £3.67 |
| £5 | £6.06 | £6.13 | £5.87 |
| £10 | £11.67 | £11.59 | £11.36 |
| £20 | £22.89 | £22.51 | £22.35 |

Round up to a figure buyers are used to: £4, £6.50, £12, £23. If VAT is added to your eBay fees, add roughly another 25p at £5 and 56p at £20. The worked examples in [live selling fees compared](/guides/live-selling-fees-compared) show where these percentages come from.

### Step 3: compare with what the item actually sells for

The floor from step 2 is not a price, it is a limit. Compare it with the recent sold prices for the item.

- **Floor well below the sold price.** Start at the floor or a round number just above it. Leave room for bidding; a low but safe start gets more people involved than a high one.
- **Floor close to the sold price.** Sell it at a fixed price, or start the auction at the floor and accept that it will often sell there.
- **Floor above the sold price.** Do not auction it. You bought it too dear, or it belongs in a bundle. [Stale stock](/guides/stale-stock) covers what to do instead.

### Why not start everything at £1

A £1 start draws bids and can end well above the floor on a busy night. On a quiet one, the same lot sells for £1, and you pay the postage-related fees and the fixed fee on top of the loss. Sellers we know who run £1 starts do it on a handful of lots where the room reliably drives the price, not on the whole show, and only once they know how many regular bidders they have. For a first show, use the floor.

## Running auctions well

- **Say the starting bid and condition out loud.** What you say must match the listing. eBay's [eBay Live policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478) bans misleading claims about value, rarity or condition, and Whatnot's rules are similar.
- **Use sudden death for cheap, fast lots.** It keeps the show moving and stops long tails of 10p increments.
- **Use extended bidding for your best lots.** It gives the underbidder a chance to respond, which is where the higher price comes from.
- **Never bid on your own lots, or let friends do it to push the price.** That is shill bidding, prohibited under eBay's [shill bidding policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/selling-policies/selling-practices-policy/shill-bidding-policy?id=4353) and Whatnot's [Community Guidelines](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/360061197472-Whatnot-Community-Guidelines). The starting bid is the honest way to protect a price.
- **Handle failed payments calmly.** On Whatnot, a buyer whose card declines has until the end of the show to fix it and you may [re-run the item](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/37362462405261-Fix-a-payment-issue-or-declined-payment). On eBay Live, a sale showing as awaiting payment [can be cancelled at your discretion](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762), ideally after messaging the buyer.

## Auctions and the buyer's right to cancel

If you sell as a business, most of your online sales to consumers come with a 14-day right to cancel under the Consumer Contracts Regulations 2013. The regulations exclude contracts made at a "public auction" ([regulation 28](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/28)), but [regulation 5](https://www.legislation.gov.uk/uksi/2013/3134/regulation/5) defines a public auction as one run by an auctioneer where consumers attend, or can attend, in person. A live stream that buyers can only watch online does not obviously meet that definition, so do not assume your live auction wins are exempt. eBay's own [eBay Live policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478) tells buyers that most purchases from business sellers are covered by the 14-day right, with limits for opened rip-and-ship packs and case breaks. Whatnot's [cancellation request page](https://help.whatnot.com/hc/en-us/articles/9810585575693-Accept-or-decline-a-cancellation-request) says UK buyers may have a legal right of withdrawal. Price your floor on the basis that some items will come back. This is general information, not legal advice; [returns without losing money](/guides/returns-without-losing-money) covers the rules in more detail.

## What goes wrong

- **Starting below the floor to "get things going".** On a quiet night the whole show sells at the floor you did not set.
- **Auctioning items with a known price.** An auction cannot beat the market; it can only miss it.
- **One floor for every lot.** Each item has its own cost and its own packaging.
- **Forgetting fixed fees on cheap lots.** A 25p or 30p fixed fee is a tenth of a £3 sale.
- **Cancelling cheap sales.** It costs a fee on Whatnot and damages trust with the room.
- **Leaving pre-bids open when a disruptive bidder turns up.** Turn them off and restrict to verified buyers.
- **Shill bidding.** It is against every platform's rules and can end your account.
