This is for UK eBay sellers, private or business, who have sold something and cannot yet touch the money. After reading it you will know which of eBay's hold types you are looking at, the timelines eBay itself gives for each, and the handful of habits that shorten them. Every timing below links to eBay's own help page; eBay changes these rules, so check the page before you plan cash flow around them.

## First, is it actually a hold

A lot of "eBay is holding my money" posts are not holds at all. They are the normal payout timing. Before you chase anything, look at the Payments tab in Seller Hub (or Payments in My eBay) and read the status on the order.

- **Business sellers** see sales proceeds as Processing, then Available for payout, typically [24 hours after the buyer's payment is confirmed](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814). Payouts then go out on your schedule: daily by default, or weekly, fortnightly or monthly if you changed it. Once sent, a payout can take up to 4 business days to reach your account.
- **Private sellers** see funds as Pending until delivery is confirmed. That is not a hold; it is how private sellers are paid unless they qualify for faster payment. Since [4 February 2025](/blog/ebay-buyer-protection-private-sellers), private sellers have been paid after delivery.

If the order shows a hold, the Payments tab has a "Check payment holds" option that gives the expected date the money becomes available, according to [eBay's holds page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/payouts-work-managed-payments-sellers/payments-hold-managed-payments-seller?id=4816). Write that date down. It is the one eBay has committed to, and it is what you quote if you contact them.

## The three kinds of hold

eBay's [Payments on hold](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/payouts-work-managed-payments-sellers/payments-hold-managed-payments-seller?id=4816) page splits holds into three types. They behave very differently, so work out which one you have.

| Hold type | What it covers | What usually lifts it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Transaction hold | Money from one specific sale | Delivery being confirmed, or the hold period ending |
| Payment dispute hold | A sale where the buyer has reported a problem, through eBay or through their bank | The request or case closing in your favour |
| Payout hold | Your whole account, not one sale | You fixing whatever eBay has flagged (details, compliance, payment method) |

### Transaction holds

This is the common one. eBay keeps the money from a single order until it is satisfied the item arrived, and says the funds may be used to refund the buyer for that same transaction if something goes wrong. The help page lists these reasons:

- **New or infrequent business sellers.** Funds typically arrive within 31 days, sometimes longer, while eBay sees how you sell.
- **Sellers who were previously restricted.** Held while eBay confirms delivery.
- **High-priced items.** Held for up to 31 days.
- **Unusual selling patterns or a big jump in order volume.** Funds are typically available 2 days after delivery confirmation or 31 days after the buyer pays.
- **Authenticity Guarantee items.** Typically held for up to 31 days while the item goes through authentication. See our [Authenticity Guarantee guide](/guides/ebay-authenticity-guarantee).

The page is clear that holds apply in every category, whether or not the sale is covered by the eBay Money Back Guarantee, and that "in most cases, holds are lifted when we can confirm an order has arrived to the buyer". So the fastest route out of a transaction hold is a tracking scan that says delivered.

### Payment dispute holds

When a buyer reports an item not received or not as described, the money for that order is held until it is resolved. If the buyer goes to their bank or card issuer instead of eBay, the page warns it can take 90 days or more, and eBay may hold the funds for up to 90 days from when the bank dispute starts. If you are found responsible for a chargeback, business sellers pay a [£14 dispute fee](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-business-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4809) and private sellers [£16.80](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/fees-credits-invoices/fees-private-sellers-activated-managed-payments?id=4822). For the case itself, see [item not received and cases](/guides/item-not-received-and-cases).

### Payout holds

These stop all payouts, not one sale. eBay's reasons include registration details that do not match, compliance checks, performance concerns and an invalid payment method. None of these lift by waiting. Check your messages and the banner in Seller Hub, then do what it asks: confirm identity or business details, update the bank account, or pay what you owe eBay. If a payout shows as Returned or Blocked, the [getting paid page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814) says your bank details may be wrong or you may need to verify more information.

## Private sellers: how you get paid now

Private sellers have their own rules, set out on [Getting paid for items you've sold](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/getting-paid/getting-paid-items-youve-sold?id=4814).

| How you sent it | When the money is available |
| --- | --- |
| Tracked, delivery confirmed | Typically 2 calendar days after the delivery scan |
| Tracked, subtotal £750 or less, buyer leaves positive feedback after delivery | Instantly on that feedback |
| Subtotal £10 or less, buyer leaves positive feedback | Instantly on that feedback (not Authenticity Guarantee or Global Shipping Programme orders) |
| Untracked, or tracked with no delivery scan | 14 calendar days from the order |
| Local collection | Within 24 hours of you entering the buyer's 6-digit code or scanning their QR code |

Then there is faster payment. If you have at least 10 completed sales totalling £150 or more in the last 5 years, with no refund, return, chargeback, cancellation or case on those sales, and no more than 2 open requests, cases or transaction defects in the last 12 months, funds typically arrive within 24 hours of the buyer paying. eBay evaluates each order for 15 days before adding it to your tracker, and you can watch your progress in the Payments tab.

Money held as Pending is not dead: the page says you can use pending funds to buy postage labels and issue refunds.

Two practical points follow. Untracked postage costs a private seller up to 12 extra days of waiting, so a cheap tracked service often pays for itself in cash flow. And a quick, polite "please leave feedback when it arrives" note in the parcel is worth more to a private seller than to anyone, because feedback can release money on low-value and sub-£750 orders the moment it lands. See [asking for feedback](/guides/asking-for-feedback) for how to ask without breaking eBay's rules.

## How to get funds released sooner

The official list is short: dispatch within your handling time, upload tracking, and use Simple Delivery so tracking goes on automatically. In practice that breaks down into these habits.

1. **Post the day the buyer pays, or the next working day.** Every day before the first scan is a day added to the hold.
2. **Use a service with a delivery scan.** For held orders, delivery confirmation is what eBay is waiting for. A signed-for service gives the strongest confirmation on high-value items.
3. **Upload tracking straight away, and check it is the right number.** A mistyped number is the same as no tracking. [Uploading tracking](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/tracking-items-youve-sold?id=4088) covers how.
4. **Consider eBay labels or [Simple Delivery](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/posting-items/setting-postage-options/simple-delivery?id=5575).** Tracking uploads itself, so nothing depends on you remembering.
5. **Describe items accurately.** Holds lift when cases close in your favour. The fewer not-as-described requests you get, the fewer disputes freeze money, and the sooner a new account stops being treated as new. [Descriptions that sell](/guides/descriptions-that-sell) covers the listing side.
6. **Grow steadily if you are new.** A sudden jump in volume is one of eBay's named triggers. Ten sales a week rising to thirty is fine; nothing for a month and then two hundred listings of new electronics is the pattern that gets held.
7. **Keep your account details current.** Payout holds for mismatched details are entirely avoidable.

### What does not help

- Asking the buyer to mark the item received. eBay uses tracking, not the buyer's word, for most holds.
- Sending a replacement tracking number from a different parcel. If eBay spots it, you have a bigger problem than a hold.
- Ringing eBay before the date shown in "Check payment holds". Unless something is wrong, the answer will be that date.

## When a hold goes past its date

The help page says most transaction holds are resolved within 30 days, but "in rare circumstances it may take longer". If a date passes and the money has not moved:

1. Check the order for an open request, return or case you missed. That turns a transaction hold into a dispute hold.
2. Check the tracking actually shows delivered, not "out for delivery" or "attempted".
3. Check for a payout hold banner on the account.
4. Only then contact eBay through the help pages, with the order number and the date you were given.

## Business sellers: planning cash flow around holds

If you are a new business seller, assume every sale is money you will see in about a month for the first stretch. Build that into buying: a first sourcing trip funded from savings, the second funded from the first's proceeds a month later, not a week later. Sellers we know who hit trouble early usually bought their second batch of stock on money that was still held.

Once you are established, holds become rare and payouts daily. At that point, if you changed to weekly or monthly payouts for bookkeeping, you can still take available funds early with Withdraw in the Payments tab; that option is not there on daily payouts because daily is already the fastest.

None of this is financial advice; it is how eBay's published rules work and how sellers we know plan around them.

## What goes wrong

- **Selling untracked as a private seller.** Fourteen days from the order, every time.
- **Forgetting to upload tracking.** The parcel was delivered, but eBay does not know.
- **Treating Pending as a hold.** For private sellers without faster payment, Pending until delivery is normal.
- **Buying stock against held money.** It usually arrives, but not always on your timetable.
- **Ignoring a payout hold banner.** It will not lift on its own.
- **Letting a buyer's bank dispute run without a response.** Up to 90 days of held money, then a fee if you lose.
- **A volume spike on a new account.** Grow in steps.

If a hold has gone past its date, post the order type, postage service and the date eBay gave you in [Account health and policy](/community/c/ebay-account-health-and-policy). If a case or chargeback is holding the money, [Buyers and disputes](/community/c/ebay-buyers-and-disputes) is the place.
