This is for UK TikTok Shop sellers who have never sold on a LIVE and want the first one to work. After reading it you will know whether you can go live, how to get products into the LIVE and pin them, which rules new hosts break without knowing, a running order for the first hour, and an honest view of when a reseller should use short videos instead. Rules link to TikTok's own pages. Features and rules change, so check the page before relying on it.

## Can you go LIVE

You need three things.

1. **A TikTok Shop that is approved.** If you have not got that far, start with [getting started on TikTok Shop UK](/guides/tiktok-shop-getting-started-uk).
2. **Your TikTok account linked to the shop.** TikTok's [LIVE selling course](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/course?learning_id=1903924999358241&content_id=1802491717863201&lang=en-GB) says to link it in Seller Center under My Account, Linked TikTok Accounts, and that you can then go LIVE with 0 followers.
3. **To be 18 or over.** TikTok's [age requirements page](https://www.tiktok.com/support/faq_detail?id=7581820708762753548) says you must be 18 to go LIVE, and it may ask you to confirm your age with ID, a selfie or another method before your first stream.

If the LIVE button does not appear, or products cannot be added, check your shop is out of any restriction on the Account Health page. A shop under an Account Health milestone can lose features; see [account health and restricted products](/guides/tiktok-shop-account-health-and-restricted-products).

## Setting up products

Every product you sell in the LIVE must already be a live listing in your shop. You add them to the LIVE's shopping bag, either in the app while setting up the LIVE or in LIVE Manager in Seller Center. TikTok's [LIVE Moderator page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=5550849816348417&default_language=en-GB) says moderators have the same shopping rights as the host, so a second person can manage products while you keep talking. A moderator must be 18 or over, in the same country, and follow your account.

For a reseller, this is the main set-up job. A one-off item needs its own listing with its own photos and condition before the LIVE. A LIVE with twenty one-offs is twenty listings made in advance.

Practical rules for the product list:

- **Order the shopping bag in running order.** Viewers scroll it; the order they see should match what you show.
- **Price each listing at the price you will say out loud**, including any LIVE discount you have set up. The [content policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8913678280345345) requires the total price and the delivery cost to be clear.
- **Check the condition in the listing matches the item.** If you are approved for second-hand, the title must say second-hand, pre-owned or pre-loved.
- **Set the shipping charge before you go live.** Commission is charged on shipping too, and a mistake here repeats on every order.

### Pinning

The [LIVE Product Pin page](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8535330761557793) explains that pinning puts one product on screen so viewers can buy it without opening the shopping bag. Only one product can be pinned at a time, and only while you are live. On the phone, tap the Shop icon during the LIVE, then Pin next to the item. On desktop, use the product tools in the LIVE console.

Pin the item you are holding, every time you move to a new item. It is the single biggest habit that separates LIVEs that sell from those that do not. If you have a moderator, make pinning their job.

### Auctions

TikTok Shop UK has [LIVE auctions](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2301024674400022&lang=en-GB) for selected categories. The starting bid must be at least £1, the highest bid wins, and buyers need a card on file to bid. TikTok suggests a short timer. Your shop needs good health to use them, and the [SPS guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2437046845359874) says a low Shop Performance Score restricts access to auctions. Ask your account manager or check Seller Center to see whether your category qualifies. For a first LIVE, use fixed prices; add auctions once you know the format.

## The rules new hosts break

The [TikTok Shop UK Content Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8913678280345345) (last updated 1 September 2026) applies to every LIVE. The rules that catch first-time hosts:

- **No pre-recorded material.** You cannot play a pre-recorded video or audio during a LIVE, or switch between live and recorded footage.
- **You have to keep talking.** Streams without real-time, sustained verbal engagement with viewers fail the policy. A camera on a pile of stock with music is not a LIVE.
- **Show the product you are selling.** Content showing a product that is not the one listed, or is no longer available, breaches the policy. Unpin sold items and move on.
- **No sending people elsewhere.** No links, handles, phone numbers, QR codes or mentions of your other shops. Everything happens on TikTok Shop.
- **Claims must be true and provable.** "Genuine" means you can prove it; "never worn" means never worn.
- **Prices and delivery costs must be clear.** Say the price and point to the delivery charge.

Two product rules matter on LIVE in particular. Mystery and surprise-based products are restricted, must show possible contents and must be listed under £100 ([Restricted Products Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753822475273986&lang=en-GB)). And trading cards opened live are excluded from change-of-mind returns ([Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753822475355906)), which is good for card sellers but only if the opening is genuinely live.

## A running order for the first hour

TikTok's own course says consistency matters more than any single stream. Your first LIVE is practice for the tenth. Keep it short and structured.

| Time | What you do | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before | Check lighting, sound and the shopping bag; do a short test stream at a quiet time if you can | Most first LIVEs fail on sound or a missing product |
| 0 to 5 minutes | Say who you are, what you sell and what is coming; pin the first item | People join in the first minutes; tell them why to stay |
| 5 to 45 minutes | One item at a time: show it, show flaws, say the price and delivery, pin it, answer questions, move on | A steady rhythm; about two or three minutes per item |
| Every 10 minutes | Recap for new viewers: who you are and what is still to come | Viewers arrive throughout |
| 45 to 60 minutes | Best items last, then a thank you and when the next LIVE is | Rewards the people who stayed and sets the habit |
| After | Pack every order that evening or next morning | The dispatch clock started when they paid |

Set-up that helps: a phone on a stand at eye level, light in front of you not behind, a plain background, the items in order within arm's reach, and a printed list of prices. A second person as moderator to pin, answer comments and remove abuse is worth more than any equipment.

After the LIVE, the orders are ordinary TikTok Shop orders with the normal dispatch deadlines. A good LIVE can produce more orders in an hour than you usually get in a week. See [shipping and returns](/guides/tiktok-shop-shipping-and-returns) before you go live, not after.

## Short videos or LIVE for a reseller

This is the real decision, and for many resellers the answer is not LIVE.

| Your stock | Better format | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Many units of the same new item | Short videos, and affiliates | One video can sell for weeks; creators can sell it for you |
| One-off used items in a niche (cards, vintage, collectables) | LIVE | Each item gets its moment; buyers come for the variety and the host |
| Mixed one-offs with no theme | Neither, until you find a theme | Viewers need a reason to stay |
| High-value single items | Short video plus a scheduled LIVE | The video builds interest; the LIVE closes |

Short videos keep working after you post them; a LIVE ends when you stop. But a short video for a one-off item has a problem: once the item sells, the video points at nothing, and the content policy does not like content for products no longer available. LIVE suits one-offs because the item and the moment end together.

Our recommendation: if your stock is one-offs, try a weekly LIVE on a fixed day and time for six weeks before judging it, and use short clips from each LIVE to announce the next one. If your stock repeats, put the effort into short videos and [affiliates](/guides/tiktok-shop-affiliates-and-samples) and use LIVE occasionally.

## What goes wrong

- **Products not listed before going live.** Twenty minutes of the LIVE spent making listings.
- **Forgetting to pin.** Viewers cannot find what you are holding.
- **Silence.** Streams without sustained talking fail the content policy.
- **Mentioning your other shop.** Off-platform promotion.
- **Selling an item twice.** It sold on another platform during the LIVE. Keep LIVE stock off other channels for the day.
- **Wrong shipping charge.** Every order loses money.
- **Orders you cannot dispatch in time.** A big LIVE the night before a weekend away.
- **Judging it on one stream.** The first few are small for everyone.
