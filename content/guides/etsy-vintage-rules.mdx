This is for UK resellers who buy second-hand stock and want to know which of it Etsy will accept. After reading it you will be able to sort a haul into "Etsy", "not Etsy" and "not yet", date an item with enough confidence to list it as vintage, and keep the kind of record that answers Etsy if it asks. Etsy's rules are linked where they are used; they change, so check the page before relying on this. Background on when and why the rules were written down is in our breakdown of [Etsy's Creativity Standards](/blog/etsy-creativity-standards).

## The short version

| Question | Answer | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| How old must vintage be? | 20 years or more | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) |
| Can I list a newer item that looks vintage? | No. Etsy names commercially available items newer than 20 years as not allowed, even if designed to look vintage | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) |
| Can I resell new craft supplies? | Yes, if their main purpose is making something by hand | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) |
| Can I resell new ready-to-use goods? | No. Blank t-shirts, hats, tumblers, generic kitchen, DIY, office and garden supplies and ready-to-wear clothing are named as not allowed | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) |
| Can I use stock photos for vintage? | No. Handpicked items, which includes vintage, must show the real item | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity), [Listing Image Requirements](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005) |
| Will Etsy ask for proof? | It may ask for extra photos or information on source, age, designer or materials | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) |
| What if a listing is removed? | Etsy can remove it and you still owe the fees already charged | [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) |

## How Etsy sorts what it allows

Etsy's [Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity) allow four kinds of item: made by the seller, designed by the seller, handpicked by the seller and sourced by the seller. A reseller who does not make or design anything lives in the last two.

**Handpicked** covers vintage, natural items you found yourself, and themed gift baskets you put together from different types of item. **Sourced** covers craft supplies, party decorations for a specific event, and items personalised with a buyer's own text or image by a production partner.

Everything else a general reseller buys (new branded goods, returns pallets, clearance stock, modern second-hand clothing) falls outside both, and Etsy is not the place for it. That is the whole of the rule in one line: on Etsy, a reseller sells age, or sells materials for making.

## Vintage: the 20-year rule in practice

The rule is a plain number. An item made in 2006 became eligible in 2026; an item made in 2007 is not eligible until 2027. There is no allowance for "vintage style", "retro", "Y2K look" or "vintage inspired": if it is under 20 years old and commercially made, it is out, however it looks.

Three things resellers get wrong.

**The date is when it was made, not when you bought it or when the brand started.** A brand founded in 1980 still made plenty of stock in 2015. The label tells you the brand; you need something that tells you the year.

**Reissues and reproductions are not vintage.** A 2019 reissue of a 1970s design is a 2019 item. The same goes for reprinted books, re-released records and modern castings from old moulds. The listing should describe the item you have, not the design it copies.

**"Around 20 years" is not 20 years.** If your best evidence puts an item somewhere between 2004 and 2009, you cannot honestly list it as vintage yet. Wait, or sell it elsewhere.

### What counts as handpicked vintage

The item has to be one you chose, and Etsy's examples are single items: a dress from the 1950s, a 1940s copy of a book. A bulk lot of identical items bought to flip does not fit the idea of "personally curated", even if each piece is old. Selling several of one thing is fine; running Etsy as an outlet for a wholesale job lot is the kind of shop that draws questions.

### Photos

Vintage listings must show the actual item you will post, not a catalogue shot, a stock image or a photo of "one like it" ([Listing Image Requirements](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/policy/listing-image-requirements/253962679005)). This matters more for vintage than for anything else, because condition is the product. Photograph wear, repairs, fading and missing parts, and photograph whatever you are relying on to date the item. Our [phone photography guide](/guides/photos-on-a-phone) covers the setup.

## Proving age

Etsy does not publish a list of acceptable evidence. It says only that it may ask for extra photos or information about an item's source, age, designer or materials ([Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity)). So the job is to date each item from the object itself, write down how you did it, and keep the photos. If a question comes, you answer it in minutes from your records rather than from memory.

### Dating clues that hold up

| Clue | What it tells you | How reliable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Printed date (copyright page, date stamp, hallmark date letter, dated receipt or tag) | A year, or an earliest year | Strong, if it is original to the item and not a reprint date |
| Maker's marks, logos and label designs | A range of years, if the maker changed them over time | Good, with a published reference for that maker |
| Care labels and fibre content labels | Rough era, from the style of label and the information on it | Moderate; use with other clues |
| Construction (zip type, stitching, fixings, materials) | Rough era | Moderate; needs knowledge of the category |
| Phone numbers, addresses, postcodes, prices and currency on packaging | An earliest or latest date | Good, where a change of format has a known date |
| Provenance (where it came from, who owned it) | Supporting context | Weak on its own; useful alongside the object |

Two or three independent clues that agree are far better than one. Write the range, not a guess: "1978 to 1983 from label design and zip type" is honest and checkable; "1980" from nothing is not.

### A record per item

Keep a short record for every vintage listing. A spreadsheet row is enough.

1. Your stock number and the Etsy listing link.
2. What it is, maker and model if known.
3. Your date range and the clues behind it.
4. Photos of the clues (label, mark, stamp, printed date), taken before you list.
5. Where and when you bought it, and what you paid.

The last line doubles as your purchase record for tax. See [bookkeeping and tax for resellers](/guides/bookkeeping-and-tax-for-resellers).

### If Etsy asks

Answer promptly with the photos and the reasoning, in plain words. If you cannot support the age, say so and remove the listing yourself rather than argue. A shop that keeps defending weak listings looks worse than one that corrects them.

## Craft supplies: the other door

Resellers often find craft supplies a better fit than vintage. Etsy allows tools and materials whose main purpose is making something new by hand: knitting needles, yarn, beads, fabric, craft blanks that are not meant to be used as they are, and craft kits ([Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity)). Old haberdashery, vintage buttons, fabric remnants, destash yarn and beading lots all fit, whether new or old.

The test is purpose, not category. A plain wooden round meant for painting is a craft blank. A plain wooden chopping board is a kitchen item and is out. A blank tote bag sold for printing is borderline: Etsy names blank items "ready to use without craft intervention" (t-shirts, hats, tumblers) as not allowed. If a buyer could use it as it is, assume it is not a craft supply.

Craft supply listings can use manufacturer images, subject to Etsy's intellectual property rules; vintage cannot ([Creativity Standards](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/creativity)). Old craft supplies over 20 years that you list as vintage should use your own photos.

## What resellers cannot list on Etsy

| Common reseller stock | On Etsy? | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Modern high street or branded clothing, under 20 years | No | Ready-to-use commercial item, not vintage |
| Amazon returns, liquidation and clearance stock | No | Commercially available, ready to use |
| "Mystery boxes" of mixed stock | No | Etsy names mystery boxes and generic themes as not qualifying gift baskets |
| A themed gift set of different items you chose, every item listed | Yes | Handpicked gift basket |
| New generic kitchen, garden, DIY or office items | No | Named as not allowed under sourced items |
| Vintage kitchenware, 20 years or more | Yes | Vintage |
| New yarn, fabric, beads, craft tools, craft kits | Yes | Craft supplies |
| Party decorations for a specific event | Yes | Party supplies |
| General seasonal decor, costumes, party favours | No | Named as not allowed |
| Rocks, fossils, shells you found yourself | Yes, subject to other Etsy policies | Items from nature |
| Bulk natural items bought in to resell | No | Resold items from nature are named as not allowed |

Other Etsy policies still apply on top of the Creativity Standards, including its prohibited items and intellectual property policies. UK law applies as well: dealing in most items containing elephant ivory, and ivory from some other species, has been banned since 6 June 2022, with narrow exemptions that must be registered before sale ([GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/dealing-in-items-containing-ivory-or-made-of-ivory)). Vintage sellers meet old ivory more often than they expect, in piano keys, cutlery handles, brooches and boxes. If in doubt, do not list it.

## Where the rest of the haul goes

Stock that fails the Etsy test is not stuck. Modern clothing belongs on the clothing platforms; branded goods and returns stock on eBay or Amazon; bulky low-value items on a collection platform. Our [multi-channel selling guide](/guides/multi-channel-selling) covers which platform suits which stock and how to run two without selling one item twice.

Items that are 18 or 19 years old are worth a "not yet" box. List them elsewhere if they will sell there; otherwise hold them and diary the year they turn 20.

## What goes wrong

- **Listing "vintage style" as vintage.** The item is judged on age, not on look. It is the commonest reason vintage listings are removed.
- **Dating from the brand, not the item.** An old brand does not make a new item old.
- **Using catalogue or stock images for vintage.** The rule is your photos of the actual item.
- **No record of how you dated it.** When Etsy asks, a reseller without notes has nothing to send.
- **Treating ready-to-use blanks as craft supplies.** Blank t-shirts, hats and tumblers are named as not allowed.
- **Mystery boxes and single-type bundles.** Neither counts as a curated gift basket.
- **Forgetting the fees.** A removed listing still costs the listing fee already charged, so a batch of out-of-policy listings is money spent for nothing. See [every Etsy fee on one sale](/guides/etsy-fees-on-one-sale).
