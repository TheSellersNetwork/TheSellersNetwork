This is for UK sellers on Etsy, and for resellers deciding whether Etsy is worth adding, who want to know what actually comes off a sale. After reading it you will be able to work out the fees on any order in a couple of minutes, know when Offsite Ads applies and whether you can switch it off, and recognise a payment reserve if one lands on your account. Every figure links to Etsy's own page. Etsy states most fees before VAT and some in US dollars, and it changes them, so check the pages before you rely on the numbers. None of this is tax or financial advice.

## The fees at a glance

| Fee | Rate for a UK seller | Charged on | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Listing fee | 0.20 USD per listing, converted to your payment account currency | Each listing published or renewed, sold or not; again for each extra item sold from a multi-quantity listing | [Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees), [Etsy Fee Basics](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360035902374) |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% | Item price plus postage and gift wrap (and any tax included in your price, for sellers outside the US) | [Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees) |
| Payment processing fee | 4% + £0.20 | Total sale amount, including postage and tax | [Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/etsy-payments) |
| Regulatory operating fee | 0.48% | Item price plus postage and gift wrap | [What is a Regulatory Operating Fee](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/1500011073202) |
| VAT on seller fees | Charged where applicable if you have not given Etsy a VAT ID | Etsy's fees | [How VAT is collected on seller fees](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360040584433) |
| Offsite Ads fee | 15%, or 12% once your shop has made 10,000 USD in a 365 day period | Orders from a buyer who clicked an Etsy ad elsewhere and bought within 30 days; capped at 100 USD per order | [How Etsy's Offsite Ads work](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000338367), [Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees) |
| Currency conversion | 2.5% | Sale amount, only if you list in a currency other than your payment account currency | [Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees) |
| Shop set-up fee | May be charged once when you open a shop; shown during set-up | One-off | [Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees) |

Two notes on that table. The fees policy says all its fees are listed exclusive of VAT. And the set-up fee amount is not published as a figure for UK sellers on the pages we checked: Etsy says it is shown and charged during the set-up process, so you will see it before you pay.

## One sale, worked through

Take a vintage jug listed at £40 with £4 postage, sold in the UK to a buyer who found it in Etsy search. The seller is not VAT registered and has not given Etsy a VAT ID. The order total is £44.

| Line | Working | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Buyer pays | £40 item + £4 postage | £44.00 |
| Transaction fee | 6.5% of £44 | £2.86 |
| Payment processing fee | 4% of £44 + £0.20 | £1.96 |
| Regulatory operating fee | 0.48% of £44 | £0.21 |
| VAT on the transaction and regulatory fees | 20% of £3.07 | £0.61 |
| Fees on the sale | | £5.64 |
| Left after sale fees | | £38.36 |
| Listing fee | 0.20 USD converted, plus VAT | a few pence; see below |
| Postage you actually pay | Your label cost | varies |

So on a £44 order, about 12.8% goes in sale fees before the listing fee and before you buy the label. The 20% VAT rate is the UK standard rate from [GOV.UK](https://www.gov.uk/vat-rates).

Three assumptions in that table need saying plainly.

**VAT on the processing fee.** Etsy says VAT is collected on its fees where it applies and that it "may also need to collect VAT on Etsy Payments processing fees" in some countries ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360040584433)). The pages we checked do not say whether a UK seller is charged VAT on the processing fee. The example above leaves it out. Your monthly VAT invoice in the Payment account lists exactly which fees carried VAT; check yours and adjust. If it is charged, it adds 20% of £1.96, which is £0.39.

**The listing fee in pounds.** Etsy sets it at 0.20 USD and converts at the market rate when it is charged ([Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees)), so the sterling amount moves with the exchange rate. Look at a recent listing fee line in your Payment account for the real figure.

**Rounding.** Etsy rounds each fee; your statement may be a penny different.

### The same sale through Offsite Ads

Now suppose the buyer clicked an Etsy advert on a search engine or social platform and bought within 30 days. The Offsite Ads fee is charged on the order total, and the fees policy says VAT is collected on all Etsy fees for sellers in countries where it applies.

| Line | Working | Amount |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fees on the sale, as above | | £5.64 |
| Offsite Ads fee at 15% | 15% of £44 | £6.60 |
| VAT on the Offsite Ads fee | 20% of £6.60 | £1.32 |
| Total fees | | £13.56 |
| Left after fees | | £30.44 |

The same jug now costs about 31% of the order in fees. On a shop that has passed the 10,000 USD threshold the ads fee is 12%, which is £5.28 plus £1.06 VAT here.

## Each fee in more detail

### Listing fee

You pay it when a listing is published and again each time it renews. Listings last four months and auto-renew by default until you switch a listing, or the shop, to manual renewal ([Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees)). A multi-quantity listing is charged again for each extra item sold, and editing a listing is free. For one-off vintage items, the auto-renew on sale setting is the one to watch: with a single item there is nothing to renew, so keep quantities accurate.

### Transaction fee

6.5% of what the buyer pays you for the item, postage and gift wrap. Postage is included, so a high postage charge does not escape the fee. Personalisation charges count as part of the item price ([Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees)).

### Payment processing fee

For a UK bank account it is 4% plus £0.20 per order, on the total including postage and tax ([Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/etsy-payments)). The fixed 20p is why very cheap items are poor value on Etsy: on a £5 order it is 4% on its own.

### Regulatory operating fee

Etsy charges this in some countries to cover the cost of regulation there. For UK sellers it is 0.48% of the item price plus postage and gift wrap, and it is subject to VAT where applicable ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/1500011073202)).

### VAT on fees

If you have not added a VAT ID to Etsy, VAT is added to your fees and taken from your balance. If you have added one, Etsy does not charge it and instead issues a monthly invoice showing that no VAT was charged. Invoices appear on the first of each month in the Payment account on a computer ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360040584433)). What a VAT-registered seller then does with those fees on their own return is a question for whoever does their VAT; our [bookkeeping guide](/guides/bookkeeping-and-tax-for-resellers) covers the basics. For a seller who is not VAT registered, the VAT on fees is simply a cost.

### Currency conversion

If you list in a currency other than the one your payment account uses, Etsy charges 2.5% to convert the sale ([Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees)). A UK shop listing in pounds with a UK bank account avoids it. Buyers abroad still see prices in their own currency.

## Offsite Ads: when you can opt out

Etsy advertises listings on search engines, social platforms and partner sites. You do not choose which listings; Etsy does. You pay only when a buyer clicks one of those ads and then orders from your shop within 30 days, and a single click can lead to more than one charged order in that window. If the buyer's last click was an Etsy Ad instead, only the Etsy Ads fee applies ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000338367)).

| Your shop's sales in any 365 day period | Fee | Can you opt out? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Always under 10,000 USD | 15% | Yes |
| 10,000 USD or more, at any time | 12% for the lifetime of the shop | No, and a previous opt-out stops applying |

The threshold is item price times quantity, less discounts and cancelled orders, not counting postage, tax, gift wrap or Etsy's fees, converted to dollars at the rates Etsy used at the time ([Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees)). Etsy checks on the first of each month and tells you when you cross it. The fee on any one order is capped at 100 USD.

### Should you opt out while you can?

The honest answer is to decide on margin, not on principle. Offsite Ads only charges when it produces a sale, but it charges on the whole order, and 15% plus VAT on top of the normal fees is a lot for a low-margin item.

- **Opt out** if your typical item leaves less than about a fifth of the order total as profit after the normal fees and postage. An Offsite Ads sale at 15% plus 20% VAT on that fee costs 18% of the order, which would take all of it.
- **Stay in** if your margins are wide, your stock is one-off vintage that sells slowly, and you would rather pay for sales than wait. Watch the Offsite Ads dashboard for a couple of months: it shows the revenue, clicks and fees from the programme.
- **Price for it** if you are over the threshold and cannot leave. Assume a share of orders will carry 12% plus VAT, and set prices so those orders still make money.

The opt-out is in Shop Manager under the Offsite Ads settings ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000338367)).

### A way to pay less: Share and Save

If a buyer orders within 30 days of clicking a trackable link you shared yourself (on social media, in a newsletter), Etsy refunds 4% of the order total, calculated the same way as the transaction fee. Links sent in Etsy messages or placed in listing descriptions do not count ([Etsy](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-gb/articles/16981332744087)). On the £44 jug that is £1.76 back, which takes the fee share from about 12.8% to about 8.8%. You have to join the programme in Shop Manager for it to apply.

## Payment reserves and holds

A reserve is Etsy holding back a percentage of your balance for a set time instead of paying it out. Etsy applies one when it thinks there is a high level of financial risk in your account, your business model or your transactions. It emails you with the percentage, the clearing window and when it will be reviewed, and the details are visible in your Payment account. While you are in reserve, you need a threshold amount in your balance before anything is paid out ([Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/etsy-payments)).

Separately, Etsy can put a hold or deposit delay on your balance if it expects disputes, chargebacks, fraud or counterfeiting claims, and it can refund buyers and recover the money from you, including by charging the card on file, when a seller is under reserve or has overdue orders ([Etsy Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/etsy-payments)).

Etsy does not publish the exact triggers. In practice, new shops with a sudden jump in sales, shops with late dispatches, and shops with a run of cases are the ones that report reserves. What you can control:

- Dispatch on time with tracking, and keep your processing times honest.
- Answer messages quickly and resolve problems before they become cases.
- Grow steadily. A shop that goes from ten orders a month to three hundred looks like a risk even when it is not.
- Keep enough in your own bank to buy stock and postage for a month without Etsy payouts. A reserve then costs you patience, not your business.

## Fees owed when you have no balance

Fees come out of your Payment account balance. If the balance does not cover them, you must pay within 15 days of your monthly statement, and Etsy can charge your card on file or suspend selling until you do ([Fees and Payments Policy](https://www.etsy.com/uk/legal/fees)). A quiet month with plenty of renewals can put you in this position.

## What we could not confirm

- The shop set-up fee in pounds for UK sellers. Etsy shows it during set-up.
- Whether a UK seller without a VAT ID is charged VAT on the payment processing fee. Check your monthly VAT invoice.
- The sterling value of the 0.20 USD listing fee on any given day. Check your Payment account.

## What goes wrong

- **Pricing from the 6.5% alone.** Processing, the regulatory fee and VAT take a UK order to roughly 12% or 13% before listing fees.
- **Forgetting postage is in the fee base.** Every fee here is charged on postage too.
- **Cheap items.** The 20p processing fee and the listing fee make low-price items a poor fit.
- **Ignoring Offsite Ads until the statement arrives.** On thin margins, 15% plus VAT turns a sale into a loss. Decide before you cross 10,000 USD, because after that you cannot leave.
- **Listing in dollars or euros from a UK account.** That is 2.5% for nothing.
- **Letting unsold listings auto-renew for years.** Each renewal is another fee. Review old listings every few months.
- **Out-of-policy listings.** A listing removed under the [Creativity Standards](/blog/etsy-creativity-standards) keeps its fees. See [Etsy's vintage rules](/guides/etsy-vintage-rules).
