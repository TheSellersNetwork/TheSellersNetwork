This is for UK TikTok Shop sellers who post their own orders and want to stop losing money and score on late parcels and returns. After reading it you will know the three ways to ship and which suits a reseller, the deadlines that matter, what happens when parcels arrive late, and who pays return postage in each situation. Rules link to TikTok's Seller Academy. They change often, so check the page before relying on it.

## The three ways to ship

TikTok uses slightly different names in different places. The policy says "Ship by TikTok" and "Ship by Seller"; the Academy guides say "Shipped via Platform". They are the same thing.

| | Ship by TikTok (platform shipping) | Ship by Seller | Fulfilled by TikTok (FBT) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who stores and packs | You | You | TikTok's warehouse |
| Who books the carrier | TikTok assigns it and the label | You, from TikTok's approved carriers | TikTok |
| Who pays the carrier | Charged through your settlement | You, directly | Through FBT fees |
| Tracking | Automatic | You must upload valid tracking | Automatic |
| Handover | Drop off at a parcel shop, or collection from you | Your choice of approved carrier | Not your job |
| Suits | Most resellers | Sellers with a carrier deal TikTok supports | Repeat new stock in volume |

### Ship by TikTok

The [Shipped via Platform guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=3148241730815745&default_language=en-GB) (last updated July 2026) explains the flow: when an order comes in, TikTok assigns a tracking number and a label that you print in Seller Center. You pack it and either drop it at a local parcel shop (the default) or book a collection. Labels printed before 7pm get a next-day collection if you use pickup; collections run 9am to 6pm, Monday to Friday. Parcels can weigh up to 30 kg for collection or 20 kg for drop-off, with a maximum length of 120 cm and girth of 225 cm. Oversized parcels may be charged extra.

The shipping cost comes out of your settlement as "TikTok Shop shipping fee" ([Finance Report Guide](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753826522744578)). Rates are on TikTok's rate card in Seller Center; check them against your own postage before you set the buyer's shipping charge. Our [postage guide](/guides/postage-and-royal-mail-choices) and [parcel size checker](/tools/postage?tab=size) help with the comparison.

### Ship by Seller

You book and pay for delivery yourself with one of TikTok's integrated carriers, and you must upload valid tracking. The [shipping policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099426562) gives you 36 hours to correct wrong tracking details, and repeated failures bring order volume limits. You also carry the delivery promise: 4 working days from order to delivery for UK orders.

### Fulfilled by TikTok

With [FBT](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753849801213697&default_language=en-GB) (last updated 25 August 2026) you send stock to TikTok's warehouse and it picks, packs and ships. The guide lists a single operation fee per order covering picking, packing, inbound handling and packaging, with examples starting at £0.78 for a single small item; storage is free for stock up to 60 days old and tiered after that; and for orders of £22 or more, the buyer gets free shipping and the seller pays £1.99 per parcel. Check the current rate card before sending stock.

FBT only makes sense for repeatable new stock: many units of the same thing that sells steadily. For one-off used items it is the wrong tool.

### Our recommendation

Most resellers should start on **Ship by TikTok**. Tracking is automatic, so there is no valid tracking rate to manage; the label is generated for you; and TikTok covers change-of-mind return postage on returns it arranges (see below). Move to Ship by Seller only if you have a carrier arrangement that is clearly cheaper and TikTok supports it. Consider FBT only when you have a line selling several units a day.

## Dispatch deadlines and what happens when you miss them

The [shipping policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753847099426562) (last updated 27 August 2026) sets these limits:

- Confirm the order, pack it and create the label, each within 2 working days.
- With Ship by TikTok, hand it to the carrier within 2 working days.
- Any order not handed to a carrier within 7 working days is cancelled automatically, and that counts against you.

The metric that now matters most is the **on-time delivery rate**: the share of your parcels, over 7 calendar days, delivered within the policy's timeframes. If it falls below 70%, TikTok charges late delivery fees on the late orders, usually weekly on a Monday, deducted from your balance. The policy says this applies whatever caused the delay, including the carrier.

On-time delivery also feeds your [Shop Performance Score](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=2437046845359874), which replaced late dispatch rate with on-time delivery rate. The older late dispatch rate still appears in the policy but now applies only to overseas sellers shipping into the UK.

### A dispatch routine that works

1. Print labels once a day at a fixed time, before any collection cut-off.
2. Pack from a pick list, not from memory. Our [storage and stock numbers guide](/guides/storage-and-stock-numbers) sets this up.
3. Drop off or have collected the same day.
4. Before days you cannot post, deactivate listings or set quantities to zero rather than take orders you cannot ship.
5. Avoid cancelling: every seller-fault cancellation counts. If you sell the same stock elsewhere, keep quantities synced.

The policy also lists things never to do: shipping empty or incomplete parcels, misrepresenting tracking, shipping to an address other than the buyer's, and underpaying postage.

## Returns

TikTok's [Cancellation, Return and Refund Policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=7753822475355906) (last updated 26 August 2026) sets the rules.

### The windows

- Buyers can request a return or refund for up to and including 30 calendar days from delivery. That applies to change of mind as well as faults, which is longer than the legal minimum.
- You must respond within 2 working days if the request is not automatically approved. If you do not respond, it is processed automatically.
- Once a return is marked received, if you take no action within 2 calendar days, TikTok refunds the buyer automatically.

### Who pays the return postage

| Reason for the return | Who pays return postage |
| --- | --- |
| Product issue (faulty, not as described, wrong item) | You |
| Change of mind, return arranged by TikTok | TikTok |
| Change of mind, return arranged by you | You |

That table is from the policy, and it is one of the strongest reasons to use TikTok-arranged returns. Your return settings in Seller Center control how returns are arranged.

The original delivery charge is separate. If the buyer's request was made within 14 days of delivery, you must refund the shipping they paid, unless they chose a delivery option more expensive than the standard one. That mirrors UK distance selling law; see [returns without losing money](/guides/returns-without-losing-money) for the legal background.

### Items that cannot be returned for change of mind

The policy excludes personalised items, sealed hygiene products once unsealed, sealed audio, video or software once opened, items that deteriorate quickly, newspapers and magazines, and trading cards opened live. Faulty or misdescribed items can still be returned.

### Returnless refunds

You can set rules for refunding without asking for the item back, by category, reason or value. The policy says these are entirely your responsibility and cost. For low-value items where return postage costs more than the item, a returnless refund is often cheaper than a return. Set a ceiling you can afford.

### Handling a return well

1. Read the reason and the photos the buyer supplied the day it arrives.
2. If it is change of mind and within policy, approve. Arguing costs you score and rarely wins.
3. If you think the claim is false (a different item came back, or damage the buyer caused), gather evidence: your listing photos, packing photos if you take them, and photos of what arrived. Use the dispute escalation route rather than refusing.
4. Never offer to refund outside TikTok. The [content policy](https://seller-uk.tiktok.com/university/essay?knowledge_id=8913678280345345) says returns and refunds must go through TikTok Shop.
5. Relist the item once it is back and checked.

Policy is clear that you must not reject requests without justification, or where the buyer has a legal right to a refund.

## What goes wrong

- **Taking orders you cannot ship in 2 working days.** A few days away from the shop can wreck the on-time rate.
- **Blaming the carrier.** Late delivery fees apply whatever caused the delay.
- **Ship by Seller without valid tracking.** Order volume limits follow.
- **Arranging change-of-mind returns yourself** and then paying postage TikTok would have covered.
- **Not refunding the original shipping** on returns within 14 days.
- **Rejecting returns to protect margin.** It costs more in score than the item is worth.
- **Using FBT for one-offs.** Operation and storage fees on stock that sells once.
- **Oversized parcels on platform shipping.** Extra charges appear later on the statement.
