This is for UK sellers thinking about selling live on eBay, whether you already sell on eBay, already stream on another platform, or both. After reading it you will know who eBay Live is open to, how to get access, what a live sale costs in fees, what you can and cannot sell, and how a show differs from normal listings. eBay Live is still changing quickly, so check the linked pages before you plan a first show around them.

## What eBay Live is

eBay Live is livestream shopping inside eBay. You schedule an event, stream video from a phone or camera, and run a lineup of your own eBay listings alongside the stream: auctions that can last seconds, Buy It Now items buyers can grab while you talk, and, for approved sellers, case breaks. Buyers find shows on the eBay Live hub, through a link or QR code you share, or through notifications when a seller they follow goes live. Each purchase is a normal eBay order with eBay's usual buyer protections.

eBay's [Livestream selling with eBay Live](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/livestream-selling-ebay-live?id=5762) help page is the manual. The [eBay Live seller page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/ebaylive/seller) is the front door.

## Who can sell on eBay Live

The help page says eBay Live is available to **eligible business sellers and sole proprietors** who meet eBay's seller, risk, policy and category requirements. There are two routes in.

### If you already sell on eBay

Eligible existing sellers who have business policies set up (postage, returns and payment policies in your account settings) can automatically receive access through the [Live Studio](https://www.ebay.co.uk/ebaylive/studio) link in Seller Hub. So the first thing to do is open Seller Hub and look for it. If it is not there:

1. Check you are registered as a business seller, not a private seller.
2. Turn on business policies and move your listings onto them.
3. Make sure your account is in good standing: eBay does not publish a numeric threshold, but it names seller, risk and policy requirements, so a Below Standard rating or open restrictions are likely to keep you out.
4. Check your stock is in a category eBay Live accepts (below).

### If you are new to eBay

New sellers apply on the [eBay Live seller page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/ebaylive/seller) using its Register option, which creates a business account aimed at Live. eBay says it will notify you when you are eligible to start selling live. It does not publish how long that takes or what it looks for, so if you already stream elsewhere, have your channel links and sales history ready to point to.

### What you need to run a show

From the help page:

- A desktop or laptop for Live Studio and the Stream manager. You cannot set up and manage events from a phone alone.
- A stable internet connection.
- A camera: the eBay app on a phone, or a camera through a third-party broadcast tool.
- eBay recommends a ring light and a wireless microphone.

## Categories and what you cannot sell

The help page lists Collectibles, Luxury, Sneakers, Apparel, Sporting Goods, Home & Garden, Health & Beauty, Parts & Accessories and Electronics as categories for live events, and says availability varies by market. When we looked at the UK hub in September 2026, the shows running were mostly trading cards, coins, trainers, clothing and plush, which gives you a sense of what UK buyers are watching.

The [eBay Live policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478) bans some things on Live even though they may be allowed on eBay generally, unless you have prior written approval from eBay. The list includes:

- Baby gear and essentials (safety, feeding, nursery items, strollers, car seats and baby toys)
- Gift cards
- Anything needing age verification, such as alcohol and e-cigarettes
- Bladed products
- E-scooters, e-bikes and hoverboards
- Single-use and rechargeable batteries
- Products containing hazardous materials

Other categories, including vitamins, supplements, over-the-counter medicines and CBD products, are allowed only under specific conditions. Read the policy page in full if you sell anything near those lines.

## What it costs

eBay Live has its own fee schedule, separate from normal eBay fees. From the [eBay Live selling fees](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/selling/ebay-live/ebay-live-selling-fees?id=5870) page and the [seller pricing page](https://www.ebay.co.uk/ebaylive/seller-pricing), exclusive of VAT:

| | Coins, Bullion, Sneakers | All other categories |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Commission | 4% of the total sale, capped at £100 per item | 6% of the total sale, capped at £100 per item |
| Payment processing | 2.42% of the total sale plus 30p per order | 2.42% of the total sale plus 30p per order |
| Combined | 6.42% plus 30p | 8.42% plus 30p |
| Promotion until 31 December 2026 | 10p per order fee on orders of £10 or less | 10p per order fee on orders of £10 or less |

"Total sale" means item price plus postage, handling and tax the buyer pays, as with normal eBay fees. The pricing page's "Sneakers" are the men's and women's trainers categories.

Other points from the fee page:

- **No insertion fee** for Live listings. If a listing started on the normal marketplace and you import it into a Live event, eBay credits the insertion fee within 30 days.
- **Every purchase in a show is a separate order** with its own per-order fee, even if you later combine them into one parcel.
- **Promoted Listings general ad fees are not charged** when a promoted listing sells through Live. Cost-per-click ads are not covered by that waiver.
- **Sales outside the show are not Live sales.** A purchase or bid made outside an event, or after it ends, is a normal eBay order at normal marketplace fees. Case break purchases count as Live even outside an event.

Worked example from eBay's pricing page: a £100 sale including postage in a 6% category costs £6 commission plus £2.72 processing, £8.72 plus VAT.

## How a show differs from normal listings

| | Normal eBay listing | eBay Live |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Fees | Category final value fee plus per-order fee | Live schedule above |
| Best Offer and reserve prices | Available | Not allowed on Live listings |
| Auction length | 1 to 10 days | Can be under a minute; optional 5-second extension on late bids, and a "sudden death" countdown |
| Listing limit per event | Not applicable | Up to 750 listings per event |
| Combined postage | Your rules, any time | Applied during an 8-hour shopping window from the scheduled start |
| Handling time starts | When the buyer pays | When the 8-hour shopping window closes |
| Proof of what was sold | Your photos and description | Your listing, plus an automatic Video Receipt clip kept for up to 180 days |
| Flash deals | Not applicable | Timed discounts during the show; need a Shop subscription |

Some details that matter in practice:

- **Listings are not exclusive by default.** You choose per listing: reserved for Live, reserved with a preview, or active on the marketplace before the show. Preview three to five featured items to build reminders.
- **A Buy It Now listing must be revised to auction format in Seller Hub** if you want to auction it in the show.
- **Orders sit as "Paid, wait to ship" for 8 hours** after the scheduled start, so buyers can keep adding items and get your combined postage. Set up combined postage rules before the show, not after.
- **The listing still has to describe the item.** eBay's policy says it is your responsibility that buyers get all the information they need, and that anything that might cross an Authenticity Guarantee threshold during bidding needs enough detail for the authenticator to match it: at least 3 images, title, size and condition. The authenticator never sees your stream. See the [Authenticity Guarantee guide](/guides/ebay-authenticity-guarantee).

## Rules to know before you go live

From the [eBay Live policy](https://www.ebay.co.uk/help/policies/member-behaviour-policies/ebay-live-policy?id=5478):

- **No simultaneous streaming on another live-commerce platform.** Streaming at the same time to non-auction platforms such as Twitch, YouTube or Instagram is allowed.
- **Pack rips, breaks and unboxings must stay in frame** and in view the whole time they are being opened.
- **No giveaways, competitions or promotions** unless eBay has approved them in advance. Sellers coming from other platforms often get caught here.
- **No inflating viewers, bids or engagement,** and no shill bidding.
- **Music, sounds and video you do not own the rights to** can get a listing, stream or replay removed.
- **No misleading claims** about rarity, limited editions or scarcity unless you can back them up.
- **Abandoned streams end automatically** after 60 minutes of inactivity, except for case break sellers.
- **No contact or payment details in chat** before a sale completes, and no moving buyers off eBay.

## Getting your first show right

1. Get access first; do not build a lineup until Live Studio is open to you.
2. Pick 30 to 60 items you know well. Price auction starts at what you would accept if one bidder turns up.
3. Set combined postage rules and make sure your postage policies can take a stack of small items in one parcel.
4. Create the event on the desktop, add a clear image and title, and preview a few items.
5. Share the event link and QR code wherever your buyers already are.
6. Do a test with Save as Draft, which gives you a shareable test link.
7. Plan to post within your handling time after the 8-hour window closes.

For running order, camera setup and pacing, the [Running a show](/community/c/running-a-show) forum and our [first Whatnot show guide](/guides/whatnot-first-show) apply to eBay Live as much as anywhere.

## What goes wrong

- **Applying as a private seller.** Live is for business sellers and sole proprietors.
- **No business policies.** The automatic access route needs them.
- **Listings with Best Offer or a reserve.** They cannot go into a Live event.
- **Thin listings for high-value items.** The authenticator and the buyer both rely on the listing, not the stream.
- **A giveaway to warm up the chat.** Not allowed without eBay's approval.
- **Multistreaming to another auction app.** Against the policy.
- **Posting before the 8-hour window closes.** The buyer's next purchase would have gone in the same parcel.
