This is for UK Vinted sellers who want to post quickly and never lose money on a parcel. After reading it you will know which carriers Vinted offers, how the label or QR code works for each, the size limits, the deadlines, which options to switch on, and what happens, step by step, when a parcel goes missing. Everything links to Vinted's Help Centre; carriers and limits change, so check the page before relying on a detail.

## How Vinted postage works

The buyer chooses and pays for postage at checkout, from the carriers you have switched on ([how shipping works](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/419/753-how-shipping-works)). You then get a prepaid label or QR code in the conversation with the buyer and by email. You never buy postage yourself on a normal Vinted sale.

Three rules drive everything else:

1. **Use the label for that order.** A different label, a different carrier, or no tracking means the order is cancelled automatically, 5 business days after purchase, because there is no tracking to show it was sent ([wrong shipping option](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/243)).
2. **Send within 5 business days.** Miss it and the order is cancelled. You can ask the buyer to extend by 3 or 5 more business days; they can accept or decline ([how shipping works](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/419/753-how-shipping-works)).
3. **Nothing changes after the sale.** Carrier, label type, address and parcel size are fixed once the buyer pays. The only way to change them is to cancel and have the buyer order again ([shipping an item](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/482-shipping-an-item)).

## The UK carriers

Vinted lists Evri, InPost, DPD, Royal Mail, Relay, AnyVan and DHL Express as integrated carriers in the UK ([shipping on Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/234-shipping-methods)).

| Carrier | How you send | How the buyer receives | Label | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [InPost](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953) | InPost locker or shop | Locker or shop pick-up, or home delivery | Print at home, or a QR code to scan at the locker or show in the shop (shop staff print the label) | Mainland England, Scotland and Wales only; needs InPost points near both of you |
| [Evri](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706) | Evri parcelshop or locker | Parcelshop or locker, or home | Print at home, or a QR code to print at the drop-off | Most parcelshops have a print in store device; check yours first. Higher value orders go to the address with a signature |
| [Royal Mail](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528-royal-mail-standard-2nd-class-shipping) | Post Office, Collect+ parcel shop, locker or customer service point | Pick-up point or home | QR code, scanned or shown at the drop-off, which prints the label | Do not use a postbox. Higher value home deliveries go as Tracked 48 Signed For |
| [DPD](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1101) | DPD parcel shop | DPD parcel shop | QR code; staff print the label | Not available in Northern Ireland, the Channel Islands, the Isle of Man, the Scottish Highlands or Scottish islands |
| [Relay](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1418) | Relay parcel shop | Shop pick-up or home | QR code; staff print the label | Selected regions only; hidden if either of you is outside the area |

### The Royal Mail postbox trap

Vinted's [Royal Mail page](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528-royal-mail-standard-2nd-class-shipping) is explicit: if you drop a Vinted parcel in a postbox, Royal Mail cannot register the collection, you get no tracking to prove you sent it, the parcel will not be delivered, and no compensation is offered. Always use a counter, a Collect+ shop or a locker. This is the single most expensive mistake in this guide.

### What about Vinted Go

Vinted Go is Vinted's own shipping company. The UK terms refer to Vinted Go packaging rules, and say parcels that are compensated as lost or left uncollected become the property of Vinted Go UAB ([terms, section 10](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions); [compensation](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)). At the time of writing, Vinted's UK carrier list does not include Vinted Go branded lockers as a choice for UK sellers. If that changes, it will appear on the [shipping on Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/234-shipping-methods) page.

## Which options to switch on

Go to Profile, Settings, Postage. Under Shipping as a seller you choose from your address or from a drop-off point, and toggle each carrier ([manage your shipping options](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/447-managing-shipping-options-for-selling)). Some options cannot be switched off, so that every buyer has at least one way to pay. A disabled carrier can still appear at checkout if it is the only option for that buyer; if you then cannot use it, you can cancel without negative feedback. Changes only apply to future orders.

Our recommendation: switch on every carrier you can reach easily, and switch off only the ones with no drop-off near you. Vinted says more options tend to mean faster sales, and the buyer pays either way. Before switching one off, check the carrier's drop-off map again; new points appear, and the nearest one is not always the one you already know.

For sellers in Northern Ireland, InPost and DPD are not available per Vinted's pages, so check which carriers your buyers can actually choose.

## Sizes and limits

Vinted suggests a parcel size when you list, based on category, item size and brand. Check it. Measure the item, choose packaging that fits snugly, and measure the packed parcel ([choosing the right parcel size](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/51-choosing-the-right-parcel-size)).

Maximum sizes and weights for standard items, from Vinted's [parcel size limits](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1227):

| Carrier | Max dimensions (cm) | Max weight |
| --- | --- | --- |
| InPost locker or shop, and InPost home delivery | 64 x 38 x 41 | 2 kg |
| Evri | Length plus twice width plus twice height no more than 245 | 2 kg (15 kg for heavy items) |
| Royal Mail 2nd Class Medium Parcel | 61 x 46 x 46 | 2 kg (20 kg for heavy items) |

If the packed parcel is bigger than the carrier allows, the carrier may charge extra or refuse it. For Royal Mail, if Vinted chose the size and you were charged extra, Vinted may compensate you; if you chose the wrong size yourself, it usually will not. For a bundle where the automatic size is wrong, you can change the size at the drop-off at no extra cost ([Vinted help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/160-can-i-change-the-parcel-size-at-the-drop-off-point)). If an item is over every carrier's limits, you cannot list it. Our [parcel size checker](/tools/postage?tab=size) helps with Royal Mail sizes.

## Packing and proof

Vinted's [packing rules](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/65) matter for money, not just tidiness: if an item is not packed properly and is damaged in transit, Vinted may refund the buyer and you may get no compensation.

- Clothes and shoes: a padded bag or a box. Not a thin unpadded bag.
- Fragile items, toys, cosmetics: a box with padding and a Fragile label.
- Electronics: sturdy box, device switched off, battery installed, padding on every side, no more than two batteries per parcel.
- Label flat on the largest side, barcode fully visible, old labels removed.

If you print Evri or InPost labels on a 4x6 thermal printer or on A6 labels, our free [Vinted label cropper](/tools/vinted-label-cropper) crops the label out of the PDF or a screenshot so it prints full size.

**Keep proof until the order is complete:** the label, the drop-off receipt or confirmation email, and photos of the item and the packed parcel, inside and outside. Vinted asks for exactly this if something goes wrong. See [packaging cheaply](/guides/packaging-cheaply) for materials that pass these rules.

## Tracking and delivery times

Tracking can take up to 48 hours to appear on Vinted after you send. Vinted says parcels can take up to 25 business days to arrive and that most arrive within that time ([tracking a parcel](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/100)). If the buyer received it but tracking has not updated, they can press Track parcel, then Mark as received.

## When a parcel is lost or damaged

### What the buyer does

From Vinted's [refund policy](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/465):

- **Lost in transit:** the buyer can claim once tracking shows "Parcel seems to be lost", or more than 25 business days after shipping. The order is suspended while Vinted investigates with the carrier, which can take several weeks.
- **Marked delivered but not received:** the buyer presses I have an issue within 2 days of the delivered notice.
- **Damaged:** the buyer reports within 2 days, with photos of the item and the inner and outer packaging.
- **Returned to sender:** the buyer is refunded once you receive the parcel back and confirm.

### What you get as the seller

If you used the Vinted label and the carrier confirms the parcel lost or damaged, you can receive compensation up to a maximum of £500, provided it was properly packed ([compensation for sellers](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1018)). Vinted's terms add that the carrier's information is treated as accurate unless you provide evidence otherwise before the transaction completes, and that you may not be compensated if you broke the packaging rules ([terms, section 10](https://www.vinted.co.uk/terms_and_conditions)).

Two catches:

- **Ownership passes to Vinted Go.** If you take compensation for a lost parcel and it later turns up, it belongs to Vinted Go UAB, not you.
- **Own postage means your problem.** If you sent the order any other way, you deal with the carrier yourself for compensation.

### What to do, in order

1. Check tracking in the conversation. Wait the 48 hours before worrying about no scans.
2. If the buyer says it has not arrived but tracking says delivered, ask them to check neighbours, safe places and the pick-up point, then raise it through I have an issue within the 2 days.
3. If it is stuck, wait for "Parcel seems to be lost" or 25 business days.
4. Send Vinted your proof: label, drop-off receipt, packed parcel photos.
5. Expect weeks, not days. Your money is held while the order is suspended.

## What goes wrong

- **Postbox drop-off on Royal Mail.** No tracking, no delivery, no compensation.
- **Using your own label or a different carrier.** The order cancels after 5 business days.
- **Missing the 5 business day deadline.** Ask the buyer for an extension before it runs out, not after.
- **Guessing the parcel size.** Measure the packed parcel; extra charges from a wrong size you chose are usually not refunded.
- **Thin packaging on anything breakable.** Damage from poor packing is not compensated.
- **Throwing away the drop-off receipt.** Keep it until the order completes.
- **Switching off carriers without checking the map.** Fewer options, fewer sales.
