This is for UK Vinted sellers who want every parcel out of the door quickly, with a label that scans first time. After reading it you will know when you can skip printing altogether, which paper suits how many parcels you send, how to get a Vinted label onto a 4x6 thermal label or A6 sheet at full size, and how to fix the usual printing problems. Vinted's carrier options change, so each fact links to Vinted's own Help Centre; check the page before relying on a detail.

If you already have a label PDF and a 4x6 or A6 printer, go straight to the free [Vinted label cropper](/tools/vinted-label-cropper). It crops the label out of the page in your browser, and nothing is uploaded.

## First decide: do you need to print at all

On a normal Vinted sale the buyer pays for postage and you get a prepaid label or QR code in the conversation with the buyer and by email ([how shipping works](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/419/753-how-shipping-works)). Whether you ever print depends on the carrier the buyer chose.

| Carrier | What Vinted gives you | Do you print? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Evri](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706) | Your choice of a printable label or a digital label (a QR code used to print the label at the drop-off) | Only if you choose the printable label |
| [InPost](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953) | Your choice of a printable label or a QR code to scan at the locker or show in the shop | Only if you choose the printable label |
| [Royal Mail](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528-royal-mail-standard-2nd-class-shipping) | A QR code in the app and by email; staff or the drop-off point print the label | No |
| [DPD](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1101) | A QR code; staff at the drop-off print the label | No |
| [Relay](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/1418) | A QR code; staff at the drop-off print the label | No |

For Evri and InPost you choose between the printable label and the QR code when you press Get shipping label in the conversation. Choose carefully: Vinted says that once an item is sold, the carrier and the label type, printable or digital, cannot be changed without cancelling and ordering again, so treat your first choice as final ([shipping an item](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/482-shipping-an-item)).

Two notes on the table. Vinted says most Evri parcelshops have a Print In Store device but to check your drop-off point before you go ([Evri shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706)). And Yodel is not on Vinted's list of UK carriers ([shipping on Vinted](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/234-shipping-methods)); InPost bought Yodel in 2025 and says Yodel by InPost has moved fully under the InPost brand ([InPost's Yodel help](https://inpost.co.uk/yodel-help)), so follow the InPost row.

### Our recommendation

If you send a handful of parcels a week, use the QR code every time you can. It is free, there is no printer to maintain, and the label that comes out at the counter is made for the carrier's scanners. Print at home only when it saves you real time: when the nearest drop-off with a working printer is out of your way, when you drop at lockers and want to walk in with every parcel ready, or when you send enough that queueing for each label adds up.

## Choosing what to print on

If you do print, there are three sensible set-ups. The label is the same in each; what changes is the paper and how much trimming and taping you do.

| Set-up | Paper | Good for | Watch out for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Thermal label printer | 4 x 6 inch (101.6 x 152.4 mm) direct thermal labels on a roll or fan-fold stack | Sellers printing labels most days | Up-front cost; labels must be direct thermal |
| Ordinary printer, label sheets | A4 sheets of sticky labels, 2 or 4 to a sheet | A few labels a week when you already own a printer | Ink or toner cost; line the label up with the sheet |
| Ordinary printer, plain paper | Plain A4, cut out and taped on | The odd label | Tape over the barcode can stop it scanning |

A6 labels (105 x 148 mm) and 100 x 150 mm labels are close to 4 x 6 but not the same size. Buy the size your printer's settings list, and print to that exact size.

### Our recommendation

Start with plain A4 and the QR code. Move to A4 label sheets once taping becomes a chore. Buy a thermal printer only when you print labels most days, or when you also print labels for other platforms. ## Why a Vinted label comes out wrong on a label printer

Vinted's help does not publish the size of its printable labels, so look at the file you download. When the label takes up only part of a bigger page, sending that page to a 4x6 printer goes wrong in one of three ways:

- The print window shrinks the whole page to fit, so the label comes out small in the middle of your 4x6 label, with a small barcode.
- The printer prints the page at full size, so only part of the label lands on your 4x6 label and the rest is cut off or spills onto the next label.
- The page is turned the wrong way, so the label prints sideways and is squashed.

The fix is to crop the label out of the page first, so the file you print is the label and nothing else, at the size of your labels. Then print it at actual size.

## How to crop and print a Vinted label

This works on a computer, a tablet or a phone with a recent browser.

1. **Get the printable label.** In the conversation with the buyer, press Get shipping label and choose the printable label. Save the PDF from the app or the email. If you only have it on screen, a screenshot works too, but a PDF gives a sharper barcode.
2. **Open the [Vinted label cropper](/tools/vinted-label-cropper)** and add the file. You can drop it on the page, choose it, or paste a screenshot. The label is found and a crop box is drawn round it.
3. **Check the crop.** The whole barcode, the address and any code must be inside the box. Drag the box or its corners if anything is outside it.
4. **Choose the size.** 4 x 6 inch for most thermal printers; A6 or 100 x 150 mm if that is what your labels are; A4 with 2 or 4 labels a sheet for sticky label sheets on an ordinary printer.
5. **Download** one PDF for all your labels, or a zip with a PDF for each.
6. **Print at actual size.** Open the PDF, pick your label printer, set the paper size to your label size, and choose Actual size or 100% scale.
7. **Check the label** before you stick it on: sharp barcode, nothing cut off, the right parcel.

You can add several Vinted labels at once and print them in one go, which is where a thermal printer saves the most time. The cropper runs on your device: files are read in your browser and never uploaded, which matters because every label carries your buyer's name and address.

### Print settings that matter

- **Scale:** Actual size or 100%. Never Fit to page, Shrink to fit or Fit to printable area.
- **Paper size:** the same size as your labels, in the print window and in the printer's own settings or app. If the printer thinks it has A4 loaded, it will spread one label over several.
- **Orientation:** portrait for a 4x6 label. The cropper already turns each label to fit.
- **Darkness:** leave it at the default unless labels come out faint. Royal Mail warns that resizing labels or changing contrast settings can stop barcodes scanning ([Click & Drop printing help](https://help.parcel.royalmail.com/hc/en-gb/articles/360000811694-How-to-print-Click-Drop-labels)); the same care applies to any carrier's barcode.

## Carrier notes for printed labels

**Evri.** Printed labels go on the parcel and the parcel goes into an Evri parcelshop or locker ([Evri shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/706)). If you do not trust your printer, choose the digital label from the start and let a parcelshop with Print In Store print it; you cannot switch type later.

**InPost.** A printed label goes on the parcel before you drop it at an InPost locker or shop. With the QR code instead, you scan it at the locker, or shop staff print and stick the label on for you ([InPost shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/953)). Vinted lists InPost within mainland England, Scotland and Wales.

**Royal Mail, DPD and Relay.** There is no label to print or crop. Show the QR code at the drop-off. For Royal Mail, never use a postbox: Vinted says Royal Mail cannot register the collection, so you have no tracking to prove you sent it ([Royal Mail shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528-royal-mail-standard-2nd-class-shipping)).

Whatever the carrier, use the label Vinted gives you for that order. Vinted says sending an order without a Vinted-generated label can get it cancelled automatically, with automatic negative feedback ([shipping label not received or not working](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/154-shipping-label-not-received-or-not-working)).

## When the label will not come

Vinted's [label help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/154-shipping-label-not-received-or-not-working) covers the usual causes. Labels are emailed from no-reply@vinted.com, so check spam and junk. If the label or code will not work, try another drop-off point, try again later, or try a different device or browser, and contact Vinted with a screenshot of any error. If you cannot print at home, Vinted suggests printing at an office, a supply store, a university or a library, or getting the label at the drop-off point.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Label prints tiny in the middle of the 4x6 label | Fit to page shrank a bigger page | Crop the label first, print at Actual size |
| Half the label is cut off | The page is bigger than the label and printed at full size | Crop the label first; set the paper size to your labels |
| Label prints on two labels, or a blank one feeds out | Paper size in the print window does not match the labels | Set the same label size in the print window and in the printer's settings |
| Label prints sideways or squashed | Orientation or paper size wrong | Portrait, correct paper size; the cropper turns the label to fit |
| Barcode is blurry | You cropped a low-quality screenshot or photo | Use the PDF, or take a fresh screenshot at full screen size |
| Barcode is faint or patchy | Thermal labels are old, the wrong type, or darkness is low | Use fresh direct thermal labels; raise darkness one step and test |
| Barcode will not scan at the counter | Tape over the barcode, a crease, or the label wrapped round a corner | Stick it flat on the largest side and do not tape over the barcode |
| An old barcode is scanned instead of yours | Old labels left on reused packaging | Remove or cover every old label and barcode |

If a label will not scan, reprint it from the PDF at actual size before you go back, and follow Vinted's [label help](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/154-shipping-label-not-received-or-not-working) if it still fails.

## What goes wrong

- **Printing when a QR code would do.** For Royal Mail, DPD and Relay there is nothing to print, and for Evri and InPost the code saves you paper and time.
- **Fit to page.** The single most common reason a label comes out small or unreadable.
- **Cropping a blurry photo.** The cropper cannot make a picture sharper than it is. Use the PDF.
- **Guessing the label size.** 4 x 6 inch, A6 and 100 x 150 mm are all slightly different. Match the file to the labels you bought.
- **Throwing the label away.** Keep a copy of the label and your drop-off receipt until the order is complete; Vinted asks for them if a parcel goes missing ([Royal Mail shipping](https://www.vinted.co.uk/help/528-royal-mail-standard-2nd-class-shipping)).
- **Using your own label.** A label that is not the one Vinted generated for that order can get the sale cancelled.
